# Product roadmap

What we plan to build next, in plain words. Each item becomes its own design and PRs when
picked up. Newest additions at the top of each section.

## Apps

- **Every app looks great.** Redesign each app's pages in the admin (connect page, settings,
  lists, empty states) to one polished, modern standard, so apps feel part of Koomerce rather
  than bolted on. Added 7 Nov 2026.
- **Test every app end to end.** Only Delhivery and Razorpay have been run on a live store;
  every other app shows an "Untested" badge (`tested` in the app's manifest) until it has.
  Mark an app `tested: true` once it has been tried for real. Added 7 Nov 2026.

## Selling anything from one store

- **One site for every kind of seller.** A merchant can sell any mix of physical products,
  digital products (files, licence keys), subscriptions and courses from the same store, with
  one checkout, one customer account and one admin. Today Koomerce is built around physical
  products. Added 7 Nov 2026.

## Shipping

- **Bulk shipments redesign.** Per order: Air and Surface side by side with prices,
  insurance shown in the price (always on for Delhivery, a switch for Shiprocket and
  RapidShyp), a batch total, and weight and box size that can be fixed in the flow. Couriers
  such as Bluedart, DTDC, Xpressbees and Amazon come through Shiprocket or RapidShyp. Design in
  progress (7 Nov 2026).

## Payments and customers

- **Store credit balance.** A real balance per customer: history and staff adjustments,
  paying with it at checkout and on admin orders, refunds to store credit, and import from
  Shopify. Planned in five steps (7 Nov 2026).
