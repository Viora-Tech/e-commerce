# Real logos for every third-party app

`apps/admin/src/components/apps/app-logo.tsx` now has an official logo for every third-party app,
not only payments and the first couriers: Blue Dart, DTDC, Ekart, Shadowfax, Xpressbees, India Post,
DHL Express, FedEx, Chatwoot, GrowthBook, Umami, Meilisearch, Google Search Console, WhatsApp,
Amazon S3, Amazon SES and Cloudflare R2. Files are in `apps/admin/public/app-logos/` (PNGs at most
256 px). Built-in apps (reviews, GST invoices, forms…) keep their gradient tiles. Sources and terms:
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
