# Markets › Catalogs (2026-10-30)

Shopify-style catalogs: which products a market sells, and its price changes on top of the
market's conversion.

## Data (migration 0193_catalogs)

- `catalog`: name, `status` (`active` / `draft`), `scope` (`all` / `chosen`),
  `price_adjustment_bp` (−9000…+20000).
- `catalog_product`, `catalog_collection`: what a `chosen` catalog sells (a collection brings its
  products, also ones added later).
- `catalog_price`: a variant's fixed price in `currency` minor units, used as typed in a market of
  that currency.
- `market.catalog_id` (on delete set null): a market uses at most one catalog. A draft catalog,
  or none, sells every product at the market's own prices.
- RLS via `apply_store_rls()`; the four tables carry the `storefront_cache_notify` trigger and are
  in `STOREFRONT_CACHE_TABLES`.

## Where it applies

- **Prices**: `storeMarketPricing` (core `markets/rates.ts`) composes each market's pricing with its
  active catalog (`withCatalogPricing`, `markets/catalog-rules.ts`): the catalog percent after the
  market's own adjustment, then the market's rounding. Fixed prices travel as
  `MarketPricing.fixedMinor` and win in `presentmentMinor/Cents/formatPresentment` when a
  `variantId` is passed (Liquid product data, Liquid cart lines and total, native `Price` on the
  product page, sticky bar and single-variant cards). A rupee market never takes catalog prices
  (its checkout charges the product's own price); the admin refuses them there.
- **Availability**: storefront `inStore` sets `app.catalog_id` when the request's market has an
  active `chosen` catalog (`lib/market-catalog.ts`); `soldInRequestCatalog(product.id)` is added to
  browse/search (`baseQuery`), `listCardProducts`, `listCollectionProducts`, and the Liquid data
  source (`loadProducts`, collection lists and counts). The product page still renders: native
  shows "Not available in your country" (`ProductDetail.notSoldHere`), Liquid gets no variant for
  sale. `storeRead` and the Liquid render cache key on the catalog.
- **Cart and checkout**: `addToCart(…, { marketId })` stores the market on the cart and throws
  `NotSoldInMarket`; `placeOrder`'s `loadCart` re-checks the cart's market (`unsoldInMarket`).
  India (no catalog) is unchanged.

## Admin

- Markets › Catalogs (`/markets/catalogs`, sidebar child next to Rollouts): list, create, edit
  (full page: details, markets, products, prices), delete. API `catalogs.*` (Settings read/manage).
- The market page's "Products and catalog" row links to its catalog (or to Catalogs).

## Left

- Native cart drawer / cart page / checkout summary still convert line totals from rupees (fixed
  prices show on the product, not yet on those lines). Checkout still charges rupees everywhere.
- Native product cards with several variants show the converted price, not a fixed one.
- A market can use one catalog (Shopify allows several).
