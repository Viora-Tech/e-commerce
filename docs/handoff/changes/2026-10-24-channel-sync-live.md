# Sales channel sync updates live

- Social channel pages (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Google) re-read every 3 s while a sync
  runs, so the bar moves from "First sync in progress" to "Catalog shared" without a reload.
- The sync bar pulses while nothing is counted yet instead of showing an empty track.
- Manage dialog: wider, buttons on one row (Disconnect left, Pause and Sync now right);
  "Last sync" says "Syncing now…" and Sync now is off while a sync runs.
