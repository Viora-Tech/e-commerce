# GST invoices list: tabs by type, one month at a time (2026-11-08)

- Apps → GST invoices → Invoices now has tabs: Tax invoices (default), Credit notes, Vouchers
  (receipt and refund) and All documents, replacing the type dropdown.
- The From/To dates became a month switcher (‹ November 2026 ›), the way GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B
  are filed. Next is disabled on the current month.
- `gstInvoices.list` (and the bulk filter) takes one kind or a list (`ListFilter.kind`).
