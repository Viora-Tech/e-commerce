# Pay page: a way back and what is being paid for

`/checkout/pay/<n>` (the gateway window page) only had the store logo as a way back and showed just the amount. Now: a visible "Back to your order" link, the order summary (lines, images, totals) in the left column like checkout, "Paying with Razorpay: UPI, cards, netbanking", and after the window is closed or fails: Try again, "See your order and other ways to pay" (thank-you page with Retry), a line that nothing was charged, and Continue shopping.
