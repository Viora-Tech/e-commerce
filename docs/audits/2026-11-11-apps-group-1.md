# Apps audit, Phase 1b group 1 (11 Nov 2026)

Roadmap Phase 1b, group 1: the apps in use or needed for the Claw Gears go-live. GST invoices,
Shiprocket, RapidShyp, Reviews, WhatsApp, Amazon SES email, Inbox.

**What this audit is.** A code-level read plus the local tests, on `origin/dev` at `d35bb658d`.
No live store, no real provider account and no provider call was used (couriers, Meta and AWS
were faked). So "works end to end" is not proven here: each app ends with a short live check the
owner runs on the live store. **No manifest was set to `tested: true`.** Flip it after the live
check passes.

What was checked for each feature: is there a test; store scoping (`withStore` / RLS); who may
call it; error handling; secrets kept out of logs and errors; webhooks signed or token-checked,
compared in constant time, de-duplicated; timeouts and retries on outside calls; money in paise;
GST rules for invoices and credit notes.

## GST invoices (`packages/plugin-gst-invoices`)

**Features.** Invoice queue (orders queued on payment or dispatch by rule, reviewed, issued in
bulk with an invoice date); gapless numbering per GSTIN and per series (domestic and export),
reset on 1 April IST; one tax invoice per parcel when parcels ship from different GSTINs;
composition GSTIN gives a bill of supply; export under LUT (no IGST, place of supply 96);
receipt and refund vouchers for deposit pre-orders; packing slips; credit notes on refund (one
per GSTIN, returned units first, s.34(2) deadline); cancel before GSTR-1 is filed; regenerate an
unreported invoice (buyer GSTIN); recalculate GST from products; order-edit revision; GSTR-1
(B2B, B2CL, B2CS, CDNR, exports, HSN, Table 13) and period filing and closing; PDF render job;
email-a-copy job; order "Add to invoice queue" action; admin pages Queue, Invoices, Invoice,
Returns, Products, Settings.

**Coverage.** Strong. Numbering under concurrency, per-GSTIN series, FY reset, period locks,
cancellation rules, credit-note deadline, per-parcel credit notes, refund allocation to the
paisa, order edits, pre-order vouchers, store isolation. Not covered by a test: the PDF render
and email jobs (they only call shared code), the admin pages.

**Code read.** Money is integer paise throughout; refund allocation adds back to the paisa.
Credit notes take an advisory lock per refund and are idempotent on the refund. Store scoping is
by `withStore` everywhere; an isolation test proves a store cannot see another's invoices.

**Bug fixed.**
- #1550: on an order with one invoice, credit notes were capped per note but not in total, so
  two refunds could credit more supply (and GST) than the invoice charged. Now every invoice is
  capped at its total less earlier credit notes; nothing left means no credit note.

**Note.** One run showed two pre-order tests failing and the next run (and every later one)
passing; it did not repeat. Likely worker-database state; worth watching.

**Owner's live check.**
1. Settings: business details, GSTIN, series prefix, template look right on a preview.
2. Place a paid test order; it lands in the Queue. Issue it; the number is the next one; open
   the PDF: seller, buyer, HSN, CGST+SGST (same state) or IGST (other state) correct.
3. Refund part of it; a credit note appears against that invoice with the right amount and GST.
   Refund the rest; the second credit note brings the total to exactly the invoice, no more.
4. Cancel a fresh unreported invoice; its number is not reused by the next one.
5. Returns page: GSTR-1 for this month lists the invoice and credit notes; download the JSON.
6. On the order page, "Add to invoice queue" works for an order not yet queued.

## Shiprocket (`packages/shipping-shiprocket`)

**Features.** Connect page (API user email and password, pickup nickname, origin PIN, optional
webhook token) with a credential check; serviceability and checkout rate (cheapest courier);
per-courier rate table in Shipments; push an order as pending, cancel a pushed order; book
(create order, assign AWB with the picked courier, label); reverse pickup for returns; tracking
poll; tracking webhook; NDR reattempt and change of address; wallet balance; pincode delivery
checker storefront block.

**Coverage.** Every API call has a faked test (login reuse, failed login, rates, booking, picked
courier, rejected order, label failure, return pickup, tracking, NDR, wallet, webhook token
checks, base-URL allow-list).

**Code read.** Outside calls go through `courierFetch` (6 s timeout, 401/403/429 typed, URLs never
in errors). Password and webhook token are `secret: true`. The webhook fails closed with no token
and compares in constant time; its event id is AWB + status + the scan's own time.

**Bugs fixed.**
- #1555: scan times (India time, no offset) were read in the server's zone, 5 h 30 min late on
  UTC servers.
- #1556: an unreadable scan date threw and stopped the parcel's tracking.
- #1557: Shiprocket's webhook Token is sent in `x-api-key`, which was not read, so every genuine
  webhook would be refused. Now read (old headers still work); copy says "Token field".

**Gaps (not fixed, owner decision).**
- Cancelling a **booked** Shiprocket shipment from Koomerce is not wired (`cancel` says to use
  the Shiprocket dashboard). Shiprocket's API collection has "cancel shipment by AWB"
  (`POST /orders/cancel/shipment/awbs`); wiring it needs one live test.
- Pickup scheduling and RTO from Koomerce are not wired (Shiprocket dashboard).
- If AWB assignment fails after the Shiprocket order was created, the order stays in Shiprocket
  with no AWB; booking again sends the same order id.
- The login token is cached per process for 9 days with no re-login on a 401 (other couriers use
  the shared `LoginTokenCache`). Only matters if Shiprocket revokes a token early.

**Owner's live check.**
1. Connect with the API user; "Connected" shows. Wallet balance matches the Shiprocket dashboard.
2. Rate table on a real order lists several couriers with prices.
3. Book with a picked courier; AWB and label appear; the shipment is in Shiprocket with that courier.
4. Register the webhook (Settings > API > Webhooks): paste the URL and the same token in the
   **Token** field, "Test and Save". **If Shiprocket refuses the URL** (its form is said to refuse
   URLs containing "shiprocket"), tell us: the URL then needs an alias path.
5. After the next scan, the shipment's timeline shows the scan at the time Shiprocket shows (IST).
6. Book a return pickup on a return; an AWB comes back.

## RapidShyp (`packages/shipping-rapidshyp`)

**Features.** Connect page (API key, store name, pickup nickname, origin PIN) with a credential
check; pickup locations (list, create); serviceability with COD per courier, checkout rate, rate
table, EDD; book through the wrapper API (AWB, label, pickup in one call), reassign to the picked
courier, assign-AWB fallback; push as pending and cancel; cancel by RapidShyp order id; tracking
poll (no webhook: RapidShyp has none for this API); NDR reattempt and return; reverse pickup;
delivery checker block.

**Coverage.** Every call has a faked test, including reassignment, the fallback, cancel by order
id, NDR codes, the undelivered scan, return pickup, pickup locations and EDD dates.

**Code read.** `courierFetch` with timeouts; API key secret; base URL allow-listed.

**Bugs fixed.** #1555 and #1556 (same as Shiprocket; RapidShyp's dates elsewhere are DD-MM-YYYY,
which used to throw).

**Gaps (not fixed).**
- If reassigning to the picked courier fails, booking fails although RapidShyp already booked
  the order with its own choice; that order stays in RapidShyp.
- #1524 (open, separate): RapidShyp misses the 800 ms checkout rate deadline.

**Owner's live check.**
1. Connect; pickup locations list matches RapidShyp.
2. Rate table lists couriers; COD marked per courier; delivery dates are not all the same.
3. Book with a picked courier; AWB, label and pickup come back; the courier in RapidShyp matches.
4. Cancel a booked test shipment; RapidShyp shows it cancelled.
5. After a scan, the timeline time matches RapidShyp's panel (IST).

## Reviews (`packages/plugin-reviews`)

**Features.** Reviews page (moderate, reply, publish, hide, delete, photos); settings (who can
review, auto-publish rules, daily limit, request timing); review requests by email and WhatsApp
after delivery, reminders, stop link; requests for past orders; QR cards for packaging; photo
review reward (discount code); AI summary ("What customers say"); questions and answers;
Judge.me import (with photo copy job); storefront widgets (stars, list, form, Q&A) and Liquid for
imported Shopify themes; order action "Review QR card".

**Coverage.** Good: submit rules, requests and reminders, past orders, QR, rewards, questions,
summary, widgets, import, settings, tokens.

**Code read.** Storefront endpoints take the store from the hostname only, are rate-limited per
IP, have a honeypot, accept photos only with a signed token for this store, and verify buyers by
the signed request link or the signed-in account (a typed email never makes a review verified).
Request links are HMAC-signed with kind, store and expiry. No bug found.

**Small note.** The "one review per person per product" check and the insert are not under a
unique index, so two submits at the same instant could both land. Low impact (rate-limited).

**Owner's live check.**
1. Settings saved; a product page shows the stars and the form.
2. Submit a review as a signed-in buyer of that product; it shows "Verified buyer".
3. Mark a test order delivered; the review request email (and WhatsApp, if on) arrives; its link
   opens the form already tied to the order.
4. Moderate: hold, publish, reply; the reply shows on the product page.
5. Ask a question on the storefront, answer it in admin; the shopper gets the answer message.
6. Print a QR card from an order and scan it; it opens the review form.

## WhatsApp (`packages/notifications-whatsapp`)

**Features.** Connect with Facebook (embedded signup) or by hand (phone number id, access token,
template language, business account id, test mode); every order and account message as an
approved template (order placed, paid, shipped, out for delivery, delivered, cancelled, refunds,
returns, COD confirm, sign-in codes, review requests and more); test mode with plain text;
marketing broadcasts and automations with approved templates; template list for the campaign
picker; Meta account webhooks (signed with `X-Hub-Signature-256`) for account and quality status.

**Coverage.** Payload building, Graph errors, template list, config validation, plus the shared
notification-channel conformance suite. New: country code, empty value, timeout.

**Code read.** Access token is `secret: true`. The platform's Meta webhook checks the signature
and answers 200 once verified.

**Bugs fixed.**
- #1551: checkout stores Indian mobiles as 10 digits; the channel sent them with no country code,
  so **no domestic shopper would get a WhatsApp message**. Now `91` is added; numbers from abroad
  (stored with `+`) keep theirs.
- #1552: an empty template value made Meta refuse the message; now sent as `-`.
- #1553: Graph calls had no timeout; now 15 s.

**Owner's live check.**
1. Connect (Facebook or by hand); the WhatsApp page shows the number and quality.
2. Templates `order_placed`, `payment_received`, `order_shipped` (and any others you switch on)
   are approved in Meta with the same placeholder count as the app expects.
3. Place a test order with your own 10-digit mobile; the order-placed message arrives on that
   number. Ship it; the shipped message arrives with the tracking link.
4. Send a broadcast with an approved template to yourself.

## Amazon SES email (`packages/notifications-ses`)

**Features.** Sender domains in the platform's SES account (Easy DKIM identity, custom MAIL FROM,
status in plain words, safe adopt and delete); a store's own SES (region, keys, from address,
name); transactional email with attachments (invoices) and Reply-To; marketing email with
unsubscribe headers; retryable vs final failures for fallback to another provider.

**Coverage.** Good for both the sender and identity flows. New: timeout.

**Code read.** Secret key is `secret: true`. From header is built safely (no header injection;
tested).

**Bug fixed.** #1554: the SES client had no request timeout; a stuck connection hung the email
job. Now connect 5 s, request 15 s, and a timeout is a retryable failure.

**Gap (owner decision).** There is no bounce or complaint handling (SES to SNS to a webhook).
Without it the store keeps mailing addresses that bounced, which counts against the SES account
and can get sending paused. Worth adding before marketing email grows.

**Owner's live check.**
1. Sender email: add the store's address, the DNS records turn green.
2. Send yourself a test from Notifications; it arrives from the store's name, not in spam.
3. Place a test order; the confirmation arrives, with the invoice attached if that is switched on.
4. Reply to it; the reply reaches the store's Reply-To.

## Inbox (core `packages/core/src/inbox`, built-in app)

**Features.** Storefront chat widget (logo, theme font, guest email or phone ask, hours and away
message); Conversations (open, closed, all; close and reopen; quick replies with `/`); realtime
updates; AI suggested replies and chat summary; AI agent with rules and a knowledge base;
customer panel (orders, details); insights; blocking by hashed email or IP.

**Coverage.** 11 test files, 102 tests: conversations, staff permissions, panel, realtime, rules,
knowledge, assist, AI agent, insights, events.

**Code read** (identity and storage, not every line). Chat cookie is 32 random bytes and only its
hash is stored; emails and IPs stored as keyed hashes; contact phones encrypted with a blind
index. No bug found in what was read.

**Owner's live check.**
1. Turn it on; the chat button shows on the storefront with the store logo.
2. As a guest, send a message; it appears in Conversations within a few seconds; reply; the
   reply shows in the widget.
3. Close the chat; write again as the shopper; it reopens.
4. Ask a question the knowledge base answers; the AI agent (if on) answers it.
5. Customer panel shows the shopper's orders for a signed-in shopper.
