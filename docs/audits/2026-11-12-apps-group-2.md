# Apps audit, Phase 1b group 2 (12 Nov 2026)

Roadmap Phase 1b, group 2: the selling and marketing apps. Back in stock, Forms, Affiliates,
EMI & pay later, Search & discovery, Meilisearch, GrowthBook, Google Search Console, Umami,
Chatwoot, Amazon S3.

**What this audit is.** A code-level read plus the local tests, on `origin/dev` (read at `d35bb658d`, fixes rebased on `8073ecd91`).
No live store, no provider account and no provider call was used (Meilisearch, GrowthBook,
Google, Umami, Chatwoot and AWS were faked in the tests). So "works end to end" is not proven
here: each app ends with a short live check the owner runs on the live store. **No manifest was
set to `tested: true`.** Flip it after the live check passes.

What was checked for each feature: is there a test; who may call it (permission); store scoping
(`withStore` / RLS, store from the hostname on the storefront); error handling; secrets kept out
of logs, errors and the admin; webhooks and signed links; timeouts and retries on outside calls
(and the SSRF guard on merchant-typed addresses); money in paise; cookie consent for the
analytics and marketing apps.

## Back in stock (`packages/plugin-back-in-stock`)

**Features.** "Notify me" block on sold-out variants (email or WhatsApp; not on pre-order
variants); double opt-in by email (confirm link), WhatsApp signups confirmed at once;
unsubscribe link; restock job on `inventory.restocked` (one alert per confirmed signup,
throttled in batches per minute, stops when stock runs out, free monthly quota per channel,
paused above it); admin Overview (usage, most-waited variants), Signups (masked contacts),
Settings (channels, batch size, messages); "Bring over restock tags" from a Shopify import.

**Coverage.** Signups (dedupe, pre-order refusal, confirm, unsubscribe, masking), restock sends
(quota, throttle, stock running out, failures, WhatsApp), restock tags. New: the confirm message
is queued once per email signup; the confirm and alert messages' links; the link paths are real
storefront routes; the relay queues the confirm job.

**Code read.** Storefront action takes the store from the hostname, rate-limited per IP and per
email; tokens are 24 random bytes; confirm and unsubscribe run under the store's RLS. Each
signup is marked notified right after its send, so a retried job does not send twice.

**Bugs fixed.**
- #1587: nothing ever sent the confirm email. Email signups stayed "pending confirmation" and the
  restock job only alerts confirmed ones, so **no email signup was ever alerted**. Now the signup
  writes an outbox event in the same transaction and the notifications worker sends the confirm
  link.
- #1588: the alert's unsubscribe link pointed at `/back-in-stock/unsubscribe/…` (a 404; the route
  is under `/api/`) and was not shown in the email at all; "Buy it here" went to the home page;
  the store name was blank ("available again at ."). Now: store name, `/products/<handle>`, and a
  "Stop these alerts" link that works.

**Notes (not fixed).**
- `availableStockFor` sums every location, while its comment says "locations that fulfil
  online". A variant in stock only at a shop counter would trigger alerts. Low impact today
  (one location per store).
- The app's FAQ says its messages are edited in the app's Settings. The settings schema has
  subject and body fields, but the Settings page does not show them and the sender does not
  read them: the texts are the fixed ones in `@commerce/notifications`. Owner's decision: say
  so; the help text is corrected in #1600.

**Owner's live check.**
1. Install, place the Notify me block, make a test variant sold out.
2. Sign up with your email: the confirm email arrives; its link says "You are confirmed".
3. Restock the variant: the alert arrives with the store name and a link to the product page.
4. Click "Stop these alerts" in the alert: the page says you won't get more.
5. Overview shows the signup and one email sent this month.

## Forms (`packages/plugin-forms`)

**Features.** Makes an imported Shopify theme's `{% form 'contact' %}` work: each post is saved
(name, email, phone, message, other fields, page) and emailed to the store's contact address;
admin Submissions page (latest 200).

**Coverage.** Parsing and caps, honeypot, saving with the outbox event. Not covered: the
storefront action (store from hostname, rate limit), the worker email.

**Code read.** Store from the hostname only; rate-limited per store and IP; honeypot answers
"posted" without saving; every field capped; email sent through the outbox in the same
transaction. Submissions need `customers:read`. No bug found.

**Note.** There is no merchant-guide page for Forms, and no way to delete a message (they hold
shoppers' emails and phones).

**Owner's live check.**
1. On the imported theme's contact page, send a message with your email.
2. It appears on Forms › Submissions with the page it came from.
3. The store's contact address gets the email.

## Affiliates (`packages/plugin-affiliates`)

**Features.** Programs (rates, levels by order value, tiers by sales, product and collection
overrides and excludes, first-order or lifetime scope, cookie days, hold days); applications
with signup questions, auto or manual approval; referral link (`?ref=`) cookie and click counts;
coupon codes in the discount engine; code requests and rotation; "Share and earn" invite from an
order; commission on paid orders (ex-GST, after discount, no shipping or gift cards), hold until
delivery plus the return window, approval with TDS (194H), reversals on cancel, RTO and refund,
clawback after payout; fraud (self-referral by account, email, phone, address; blocklist;
review); payouts (candidates, create, mark paid, cancel), bank bulk CSV (decrypted details) and
TDS report (26Q, settings-level only); GST on payouts to registered affiliates; affiliate portal
(stats, sales with masked buyers, payouts, payout details encrypted); creatives; emails to the
affiliate and the merchant.

**Coverage.** Strong: 14 test files on programs, rules, commission maths, matching, hold and
approval, reversals, clawbacks, TDS, GST, fraud, blocklist, codes, enrolment, portal, CSV
injection, secrets rotation and erasure. New: two "Create payouts" at once.

**Code read.** Money is integer paise; payout details and PAN encrypted; CSV cells escaped
against formulas; one commission per order (unique index); bank file and TDS report need
settings-level permission.

**Bug fixed.**
- #1591: two "Create payouts" at the same time (double click, two staff, a retried request)
  each made a pending payout for the same commissions; the second kept the full amount with no
  commissions under it, so the bank file asked to pay the affiliate twice. Now each payout
  claims its commissions first and is built from what it got; one that got none is dropped.

**Notes (not fixed).**
- The referral cookie was set whatever the cookie banner said. Owner's decision: it waits for
  a yes to marketing (#1601; see the owner's answers at the end).
- Sums use `::int` (up to about ₹2.1 crore per affiliate); fine for now.

**Owner's live check.**
1. Apply as an affiliate from the storefront; approve in admin; the approval email arrives.
2. Open the affiliate link in a private window, buy something; the sale shows on the
   affiliate's dashboard with the right commission (ex-GST, after discount).
3. Use the affiliate's coupon on another order; it is credited too.
4. Mark the order delivered, wait out the hold (or "Approve due now"); create a payout; press
   it twice quickly: still one payout. Download the bank file; mark paid; the email arrives.
5. Refund a paid order; the clawback comes off the next payout.

variant), from a minimum price; Razorpay's own affordability widget when Razorpay is chosen

**Owner's live check.**

   Razorpay's widget; one below shows nothing.

## Meilisearch (`packages/plugin-meilisearch`)

**Features.** Connect (address, admin key; creates a search-only key for the store's index);
full reindex into a side index then swapped in; incremental sync on product changes; synonyms;
storefront search through Meilisearch with SQL fallback; status (documents, last sync, error).

**Coverage.** Client (errors, timeouts, SSRF guard), documents, settings, server (connect,
reindex, sync, engine cool-off).

**Code read.** Keys encrypted, never sent to the admin; merchant addresses go through
`safeFetch` unless listed as trusted; the storefront waits at most 300 ms, then SQL answers;
ids from Meilisearch are loaded again under RLS. No bug found.

**Owner's live check.**
1. Connect your Meilisearch; "Reindex now" finishes with the product count.
2. Search with a typo on the storefront; results come back.
3. Edit a product title; search finds the new title within a minute.
4. Stop Meilisearch; storefront search still works (SQL).

## Google Search Console (`packages/plugin-search-console`)

**Features.** Connect with Google (OAuth with PKCE and signed state); pick the property; daily
sync of the last 28 days and the 28 before (pages, queries, devices, countries); report; product
card; sitemaps list and submit; disconnect (revokes).

**Coverage.** OAuth helpers, state signing, ranges, the sync.

**Code read.** State is HMAC-signed with expiry and checked against the signed-in store; the
refresh token is encrypted; Google calls have a 20 s timeout; the sync never holds a
transaction while it waits. No bug found.

**Owner's live check.**
1. Connect with the Google account that owns the property; pick it.
2. "Refresh now"; the report shows clicks and impressions that match Search Console.
3. Submit the sitemap; it shows in Search Console.

## Umami (`packages/plugin-umami`)

**Features.** Tracker on the storefront (cookieless), wait-for-consent switch; analytics report
in admin with the API key; create the website in Umami.

**Coverage.** Settings, client, report. New: the opt-out switch.

**Bug fixed.**
- #1592: Umami counts client-side page views itself once loaded. When a shopper said no on
  the banner (or changed a yes to a no), the script tag was removed but the loaded tracker kept
  counting every page they opened until a full reload. Now a no also sets Umami's own off switch
  (`umami.disabled` in local storage), and a later yes clears it (a merchant's own opt-out is
  kept).

**Owner's live check.**
1. Connect; a visit with analytics accepted shows in Umami's realtime view.
2. Open cookie settings, choose no, browse three pages: Umami's realtime shows no new views.
3. Admin Analytics shows Umami's numbers with the API key saved.

## Chatwoot (`packages/plugin-chatwoot`)

**Features.** Chat widget (consent category chosen by the store, "necessary" by default);
signed-in shoppers identified with an HMAC `identifier_hash`; contact sync on customer created,
updated and order paid; conversations card on customer and order pages.

**Coverage.** Settings, client, contact sync and matching.

**Code read.** API token and HMAC secret encrypted; addresses through `safeFetch`; sync is
idempotent (no write when unchanged); a contact tied to another identifier is never taken over.
No bug found.

**Note.** Like Umami, once loaded the widget stays until a reload if the shopper later says no
to the category the store picked. Chat is something the shopper opens, so it was left as is.

**Owner's live check.**
1. Connect; the chat bubble shows on the storefront.
2. Signed in, open the chat: your name and orders show beside it in Chatwoot.
3. Customer page in admin lists the conversation.
