# Plans and billing

A store's plan lives in `store_plan` (one row per store). Its bills live in `plan_payment`
(migration `0123`). Prices, limits and the trial are in `@commerce/core/billing` (`PLANS`,
`TRIAL_DAYS = 14`, `RENEW_AHEAD_DAYS = 3`, `GRACE_DAYS = 7`). The decisions behind them are in
`memory-bank/pricing.md`.

## Bills are orders

Each bill is an order on the platform's **sales store** (`PLATFORM_SALES_STORE_ID`), so the
existing checkout, payment gateways, thank-you page and GST-invoice app do the work:

1. `createPlanBill` finds the plan product variant by SKU `plan-<plan>-<interval>` (for example
   `plan-growth-month`).
2. It then creates a draft, converts it, and creates a payment attempt with the sales store's
   first enabled gateway.
3. The bill's URL is the signed thank-you page on the sales storefront, which shows **Pay now**
   for an unstarted attempt.
4. When the order is paid, the outbox relay (`order.paid` on the sales store) calls
   `markPlanBillPaid`. That makes the plan `active`, starts the period at max(start, paid time),
   and sets `ai_settings.plan`. A refund or cancel calls `cancelPlanBill`.

## States

`trialing` → `active` (paid) → `due` (renewal unpaid past the end) → `paused` (after the grace
days). A downgrade on an active plan sets `next_plan` and takes effect at the period end.

The worker's hourly `{billing}-sweep` (`runBillingSweep`, `stores_with_plan_due`):
- bills 3 days ahead;
- marks plans due;
- pauses them after grace.

It is off while `PLATFORM_SALES_STORE_ID` is unset.

## Per-order fees

Each plan includes `PLANS[plan].orders` orders a month; every order after that carries
`feeBasisPoints` of its total (100 / 75 / 50 / 0, Scale charges none). The rules are in
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-pricing-revamp-design.md`.

- **Pure maths:** `monthFee(orders, periods)` and `feeOrderRows` in `billing/usage-fee.ts`. Only
  orders inside a **paid** `plan_payment` period count, so trial, grace and paused days carry no
  fee, and each order's rate is the period covering it (after an upgrade, the later one).
- **Inputs:** `loadMonthFeeInputs(tx, storeId, 'YYYY-MM')` in `billing/usage-fee-service.ts`
  (not draft, not cancelled, not test, not `refunded`). `feeEstimate` is this month so far for
  Plan & billing.
- **Bills:** `usage_bill` (migration `0179`), one row per store and month. `runUsageBilling`
  runs in the hourly sweep and bills last month once (`stores_with_paid_period`). Under ₹100
  (`USAGE_BILL_MIN_PAISE`, with what was carried in) the row is `carried`; otherwise a sales
  order with one `usage-fee-<plan>` line per rate plus `usage-fee-carried`, each with a computed
  `unitPricePaise`.
- **Paid / cancelled:** the relay calls `markUsageBillPaid` / `cancelUsageBill`
  (`usage_bill_store`). An unpaid fee bill never changes `store_plan.status`: `createPlanBill`
  adds every `due` fee bill to the next plan bill as a `usage-fee-carried` line, marks them
  `merged` and cancels their own orders; paying the plan bill pays them.
- **AutoPay** charges a fixed amount, so fee bills stay pay links.

`runFairUseCheck` (same sweep) compares the two finished months' `marketing_session` counts with
`visitsPerMonth`; two months over sets `store_plan.fair_use_notice_month` and emails the owner
and support. Nothing is blocked. The beacon starts no session for a bot user agent.

The pricing page's calculator uses `monthlyCost` / `bestPlan` (`billing/cost-estimate.ts`).
Prices outside India are stored in `billing/regional-prices.ts` and stay off
(`REGIONAL_PRICING_LIVE = false`) until a non-Indian gateway exists.

## Paid apps

`APP_PRICING` (`@commerce/core/billing`) lists each paid first-party app, the plan that includes
it, and its monthly price. Apps not listed are free.

`storeAppAccess` gives each app one of four states: `free`, `included`, `bought` (until a date) or
`buy`.
- `apps.list` attaches it as `billing`, for the Installed apps list and the install flow. The
  App Store website prices apps from the same `APP_PRICING` (`@commerce/core/app-catalogue`).
- `apps.install` refuses a `buy` app with `FORBIDDEN` and `data.reason = 'app_not_in_plan'`.

A purchase is an `app_purchase` row (migration `0132`) whose bill is a sales-store order with SKU
`app-<pluginId>-month`, placed by the same `placeSalesOrder` as plan bills.
- **Paid:** `order.paid` → `markAppBillPaid` makes the month active and installs the app.
- **Renewals:** the same hourly sweep bills 3 days ahead (`runAppRenewals`,
  `stores_with_app_renewals`). An unpaid renewal keeps the app for `GRACE_DAYS`, then lapses.
- **Stop renewing:** marks the purchase `cancelled`. Access lasts until `periodEnd`.

Moving to a smaller plan doesn't uninstall anything: apps already installed keep working.

## AI credits

Every AI call is metered in credits: 1 credit is ₹0.10 of model cost. `creditMillisFor(tier,
in, out)` in `@commerce/ai` prices tokens with `TIER_PRICES_INR`, and `recordAiUsage` adds
them to `ai_usage.credit_millis`. `checkQuota` refuses a call once the month's credits reach
`PLAN_QUOTAS[plan].credits` plus the store's bought credits (`ai_settings.extra_credit_millis`).
Whatever a call spends past the allowance comes out of the bought credits (`spendExtraCredits`).

A top-up (`AI_CREDIT_PACK`: 1,000 credits for ₹199, SKU `ai-credits-1000`) is an
`ai_credit_topup` row (migration `0133`). On `order.paid`, `markAiTopupPaid` adds the credits.

Update `TIER_PRICES_INR` whenever the model or its price changes.

## Unlimited stores

Koomerce's own stores (Waimers) run on an unlimited plan: `store_plan.unlimited`, set only with
`bun run platform:unlimited <slug>` (`--off` to undo; `setStoreUnlimited` in
`core/billing/unlimited.ts`). The store keeps Scale's features with no staff or storage limit, no
per-order fee, no renewals, due or pause, and no fair-use notice; `choosePlan` refuses with
`UnlimitedPlan`. AI moves to the `internal` tier, a 50,000-credit monthly safety cap.

## Bill emails and GSTIN

- The billing sweep emails the store's contact email (platform template `plan_bill`, `sendPlanBillEmail`) when it bills a plan or app renewal, and once when a plan goes overdue.
- Plan, app and credit orders carry the buying store's GSTIN (`buyer_gstin`, validated) and name, so the GST invoice is a B2B one.

## Setting up the sales store

- Create products with SKUs `plan-starter|growth|pro-month|year`: active, untracked inventory,
  GST-inclusive prices from `PLANS`.
- For paid apps, one product per app with SKU `app-<pluginId>-month` (`reviews`, `back-in-stock`,
  `forms`, `affiliates`, `emi-pay-later`, `google-search-console`), priced from `APP_PRICING`.
- AI credits: a product with SKU `ai-credits-1000` at ₹199 + GST.
- Enable one payment gateway on the sales store.
- Run migrations `0121`–`0134`.

## UPI AutoPay

Set `PLATFORM_RAZORPAY_KEY_ID`, `_KEY_SECRET` and `_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (the platform's own Razorpay
account, with Subscriptions enabled) to turn it on. The webhook is
`<ADMIN_URL>/api/billing/autopay-webhook`, for `subscription.*` events.

- `startAutopay` creates a Razorpay plan (the plan price + GST) and a subscription, and stores an
  `autopay_mandate` row (migration `0134`). The owner approves the UPI mandate on the
  subscription's `short_url`.
- `subscription.charged` → `handleAutopayWebhook` bills the period (`createPlanBill`), then
  captures that sales order's payment with `applyPaymentStatus`. `order.paid` then runs the
  same path as a pay-link bill: plan active, GST invoice issued. A repeated webhook for the same
  payment changes nothing (`last_payment_id`).
- While a mandate is `active`, the sweep sends no pay-link renewals. A halted, cancelled or
  turned-off mandate goes back to pay links.

Not live-tested yet: it needs a Razorpay account with Subscriptions enabled.

**Paused** shows a banner only and blocks nothing.
