# GST and invoices

Two separate things work together: **tax** is computed by core at checkout for every store, and
**documents** (invoices, credit notes, vouchers, packing slips) come from the GST invoices app.
A store under the GST threshold can skip the app and still charge prices that include tax.

## Tax: per parcel, from the shipping location

- The store's business details and GST numbers are in `business` and `gst_registration`; each
  `location` (warehouse) belongs to one GSTIN.
- Each product (or variant) carries an HSN code and GST rate, set on the product page (fields the
  GST invoices app adds through `productFields`) or in bulk on the app's Products page.
- At place order the cart is split into parcels by location
  ([Orders end to end](/developer-docs/how-it-works/orders)). `computeParcelGst`
  (`packages/core/src/tax/parcel-gst.ts`) taxes each parcel from **its** location's state: same
  state as the ship-to address gives CGST + SGST, another state gives IGST. The order stores the
  sum (`mergeOrderGst`).

Why per parcel: one order shipped from Delhi and from Mumbai is two supplies under two GSTINs.
Billing it as one would put the wrong GSTIN and the wrong tax split on the invoice. Merchant
guide: [Several warehouses and GST](/merchant-guide/money-and-gst/several-warehouses-and-gst).

## Documents: the GST invoices app

`packages/plugin-gst-invoices` (id `gst-invoices`), templates and PDFs in `packages/invoices`
(adapted from invoicely, MIT), settings schema in `packages/invoices/src/settings.ts`.

| Document | When |
| --- | --- |
| Tax invoice | One per parcel. `issueOn`: on payment (default), on fulfilment, or manual. Queued as a draft, numbered only when issued |
| Receipt voucher | A paid pre-order (an advance) before its goods ship; the tax invoice follows at dispatch unless the store invoices pre-orders at payment |
| Credit note | A refund that reduces an issued invoice: one per invoice it reduces |
| Refund voucher | A refund of an advance (receipt voucher) |
| Packing slip, order confirmation | On demand, or attached to the order email |

- **Numbering**: each GSTIN has its own series (`invoice_sequence`); a number is taken only at
  issue, never for a draft, so there are no gaps from cancelled drafts.
- **The queue**: Apps → GST invoices → Queue lists drafts waiting to be issued; issuing renders
  PDFs in the `{apps}-jobs` queue. Emailing many copies from the Invoices list runs as a batch
  (`invoice_batch`) the admin polls for progress.
- **Edits and cancels**: `order.edited` rebuilds queued invoices and replaces an issued one that
  is now wrong; `order.cancelled` cancels invoices of a sale that never shipped.
- **Shopper downloads**: a signed link (`/documents/<id>` on the storefront) on the order page and
  in emails.

The full hook list is on [Apps: worked example](/developer-docs/how-it-works/apps#worked-example-gst-invoices-builtin).

## Returns: GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B

The app's Returns page builds GSTR-1 (JSON in the government's shape) and a GSTR-3B summary per
GSTIN per month from issued documents. Filing a month (`invoice_period`) marks its invoices
reported, so they can no longer be cancelled; closing it stops issuing or backdating into it. Details:
[GST returns](/developer-docs/building/gst-returns).
