# Shipping

Shipping has two halves: **what the shopper pays** at checkout, and **getting the parcel there**
after the order. Both run on courier apps that implement one contract, so the Shipments
workspace works the same with Delhivery, Shiprocket, India Post or a hand-typed tracking number.

## The courier contract

Every courier app provides a `ShippingProvider` (`packages/shipping/src/types.ts`) through a
factory in its manifest's `provides.shipping`. The required methods are
`checkServiceability`, `createShipment`, `track`, `cancel` and `verifyCredentials`; the rest are
optional and the admin shows a feature only when the app has it: `quoteRate` / `quoteOptions`
(live rates), `getLabel`, `schedulePickup`, `ndrAction`, `getWalletBalance`,
`createReturnPickup`, `pushOrder` (send the order to an aggregator as pending) and `parseWebhook`.
Each app ships a conformance test against the contract. Apps today: Delhivery, Blue Dart, DTDC,
Ekart, Shadowfax, Xpressbees, India Post, Shiprocket and RapidShyp (aggregators), DHL Express,
FedEx and ShipGlobal (international), plus the core demo courier for development.

## At checkout: what the shopper pays

Two ways, chosen by the store:

- **Zones and rates** (Settings → Shipping, `shipping_zone`, `shipping_rate`, shipping profiles
  per product): flat or weight-based prices by state. Code: `packages/core/src/shipping/rates*.ts`,
  `profiles*.ts`.
- **Delivery options** app: Standard and Express cards priced from live quotes of the connected
  couriers for the shopper's PIN code, with an 800 ms deadline per app, a cache, a circuit
  breaker and a backup price. The chosen route is saved on the order and booked later.
  [Delivery options engine](/developer-docs/building/delivery-options).

**When each is used.** Zone rates are the base: fixed prices the merchant controls, with no
courier call, and the fallback whenever the Delivery options app cannot quote (the order is marked
**Placed on backup**). The Delivery options app is for stores whose real cost varies by courier,
PIN code and box, or that sell a paid fast option; without it, or with no options configured,
checkout shows the zone rates unchanged.

Courier answers (quotes, the PIN code check) are cached in Dragonfly with a fingerprint of the
courier's saved settings in the key (`courier-fingerprint.ts`), so new credentials never get the
old account's answers.

## After the order: booking

1. **Parcels.** The order's `fulfillment_order` rows say which location ships what
   ([Orders end to end](/developer-docs/how-it-works/orders)). Pickup is from that location.
2. **Create shipment** (order page, `/orders/[id]/ship`, or bulk from Orders → Shipments): the
   admin shows each enabled courier's rate for the parcel's weight and box
   (`courier-rates.ts`), the merchant picks one, and `createShipment` returns the AWB and label.
   The `shipment` row stores provider, waybill, status and label (`label-store.ts`).
3. **Labels and manifests**: labels print one per page or on sheets (`label-sheet*.ts`);
   pickups are requested per location and day (`pickup.ts`, `shipment_pickup`).
4. **Aggregators**: for Shiprocket and RapidShyp, `{shipping}-order-push` sends new orders as
   pending orders so staff can also work them in the aggregator's panel (`order-push.ts`).
5. **Ship it yourself**: a tracking number typed by hand (`manual-tracking.ts`).

**One box, several orders.** Two orders for one customer packed together share one AWB:
`shipment_shared_order` links the second order to the first's shipment, the order page says
"Same box as #A", and it is shipped, delivered or returned with that order (`shared-parcel.ts`). A tracking number already on another order is
refused (`order/waybill-owner.ts`) so a shipment can never move silently between orders.

## Tracking

- **Webhooks**: `POST /webhooks/shipments/:provider/:storeId` (or the neutral
  `/webhooks/shipments/c/:code/:storeId`, `webhook-path.ts`) on the worker. Each notice is stored
  once in `shipment_webhook_event` and applied.
- **Poller**: `{shipping}-track` every 30 minutes asks each store's courier for every open
  shipment (`apps/worker/src/shipping.ts`), for couriers without webhooks and for missed ones.
- `applyTracking` (`apply-tracking.ts`) moves the shipment through its states
  (`shipment-state.ts`) and writes outbox events: out for delivery, delivered, NDR raised or
  resolved, RTO.

## NDR and RTO

A scan that looks like a failed attempt opens an **NDR** (`ndr.ts`); the Shipments **Needs
attention** tab lists them, and staff ask for a reattempt (with a new date, address or phone) or
a return, through `ndrAction` when the courier supports it. A parcel coming back is **RTO**: the
order gets an RTO task and `order.rto` fires (the COD & RTO app, `packages/core/src/cod-rto/`,
learns from these). Returns
the shopper asks for use `createReturnPickup` when the courier offers reverse pickup.

## Wallets and charges

Prepaid couriers report a wallet balance (`getWalletBalance`, `courier_wallet_balance`, shown in
the app bar). The courier's charge per shipment, when the courier reports it, feeds the shipping
margin and Profit.
