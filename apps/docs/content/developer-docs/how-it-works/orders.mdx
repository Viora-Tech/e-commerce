# Orders end to end

  caption="Each change writes an outbox event that emails, WhatsApp, app hooks, automations and pixels react to."

## 1. Cart

The cart (`cart`, `cart_line`) lives in the storefront; its id is in a cookie. Discounts
(codes and automatic offers), gift cards and store credit are worked out by core
(`packages/core/src/discounts`, gift cards, `store_credit_account`) every time the cart changes,
and again at place order: the price the browser shows is never trusted.

## 2. Checkout

`apps/storefront/src/app/-s/[storeId]/checkout` collects contact, address, delivery option and
payment method. What it offers comes from the store's settings and installed apps:

- **Delivery options**: the Delivery options app quotes the connected couriers for the PIN code
  ([Delivery options engine](/developer-docs/building/delivery-options)), or plain zone rates
  from Settings → Shipping.
- **Payment methods**: online through a gateway app (Razorpay, Cashfree), UPI to the merchant's
  own UPI ID or bank transfer (the core `manual` provider), and cash on delivery (core `cod`,
  with an optional fee and rules). See [Payments](/developer-docs/how-it-works/payments).

## 3. Place order

`placeOrder` runs as a saga (`runWorkflow`, `packages/core/src/workflow`): each step has an undo,
run in reverse when a later step fails. In order: lock the cart (a second submit returns the
same order), check launch rules, resolve discount and shipping, **route and reserve stock into
parcels**, create the order (writes outbox `order.placed`), redeem gift cards, record the payment
attempt, mark the cart converted. Steps and pitfalls:
[Request flow, section 3](/developer-docs/core-concepts/request-flow#3-a-shopper-checks-out-and-pays).

**Parcels.** `routeAndReserve` (`packages/core/src/order/routing-service.ts`) splits the cart
over the locations that sell online: one location with everything, else the fewest parcels,
else the default location tops up and the parcel needs a transfer. Each parcel is a
`fulfillment_order` with its own lines, GST and cost. Why per parcel: stock, the GSTIN that bills
it and the pickup address all depend on which location ships it.

## 4. Paid

The order commits **before** the gateway is called, so a gateway error never loses an order.
Payment confirmation comes from the gateway's webhook (`/webhooks/payments/:provider/:storeId`),
the browser's return, a staff **Mark as paid**, or the expiry sweep asking the gateway. All go
through `applyPaymentStatus` (`packages/core/src/payments/confirm.ts`). Order statuses are never
set by hand: `recomputeOrderStatuses` (`packages/core/src/order/recompute.ts`) derives
`payment_status` and `fulfillment_status` from payments, refunds and parcels.

**Unpaid checkouts are drafts.** With **Keep unpaid checkouts as drafts** on (Settings →
Payments → Repeated checkouts), a checkout paid online or by UPI or bank transfer stays a draft
(`#D57`) until the money arrives, then becomes a numbered order; only COD and fully gift card or
store credit checkouts become orders at once (`packages/core/src/payments/checkout-drafts.ts`).
Why: failed card retries used to make a row of cancelled orders and use up order numbers. With
the setting off, the order is created at once and `{payments}-unpaid-expiry` cancels it after the
store's unpaid window, asking the gateway first. Merchant guide:
[Drafts](/merchant-guide/orders/drafts). Pre-orders may take a deposit; the balance is asked for, reminded
and collected by `{payments}-preorder-balance`.

## 5. Invoice

If the GST invoices app is installed, its `order.paid` or `order.fulfilled` hook issues a tax
invoice per parcel (or a receipt voucher for an advance).
See [GST and invoices](/developer-docs/how-it-works/gst-and-invoices).

## 6. Fulfil and ship

Staff pack the order on its page or in bulk (**Orders → Shipments**). Packing a parcel books a
courier through the store's shipping app (rate, AWB, label, pickup) or records a tracking number
typed by hand. Orders can be **held** (no shipment until released), and several orders for one
customer can go in **one box** under one AWB (`shipment_shared_order`; a tracking number already
on another order is refused, `packages/core/src/order/waybill-owner.ts`).
See [Shipping](/developer-docs/how-it-works/shipping).

## 7. Tracking, delivery, NDR and RTO

Tracking arrives by courier webhook and by the `{shipping}-track` poller every hour.
`applyTracking` moves the shipment along; **delivered** completes the parcel (outbox
`order.delivered`). A failed attempt raises an **NDR** (`shipment.ndr_raised`) that staff answer
with reattempt or return; a parcel that comes back is **RTO** (`order.rto`), which opens an RTO
task on the order for staff to close.

## 8. Returns, refunds, credit notes

A shopper (from their account) or staff asks for a return (`return_request`); staff approve,
receive and refund it. Money goes back to the original payment through the gateway, by hand, or
as **store credit** (`store_credit_entry`, a balance on the customer, never a gift card). Each
refund writes `refund.created`, and the GST invoices app issues a credit note. Exchanges become a
new order from a draft. Merchant guide: [Returns and RTO](/merchant-guide/orders/returns-and-rto).

## 9. Edit and cancel

**Edit order** (`/orders/[id]/edit`, `packages/api/src/routers/order-edit.ts`) changes a placed
order: add catalogue or custom items, remove items (with or without restock), change quantities,
prices of unpacked lines, shipping and discount. Stock, parcels and GST follow; items added after
fulfilment go in a new parcel; a higher total opens a balance sent as a pay link, a lower one is
refunded the way staff choose. It fires `order.edited` once. **Cancel** can cancel the courier label first (if the courier refuses, nothing is cancelled),
releases stock, refunds what staff choose, and fires `order.cancelled`. Both may wait for approval when the member's role ticks
it. Merchant guide: [Edit an order](/merchant-guide/orders/edit-an-order).

## 10. Analytics

Orders and sales on Home and Analytics leave out cancelled orders; sessions count real shoppers
only. Profit uses each line's cost at sale
(`order_line.cost_at_sale_paise`, from the weighted average cost purchase orders keep). Merchant
guide: [Analytics](/merchant-guide/analytics/overview).

## Who hears about it

Every step above writes an `outbox_event` in the same transaction. The relay turns them into:

| Consumer | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Shopper messages (email, WhatsApp) | `order.paid` → payment received; `shipment.out_for_delivery` → out for delivery |
| App hooks | GST invoices on `order.paid`; Affiliates on `order.paid`, `order.delivered`, `refund.created` |
| Automations | Workflows triggered by Order placed, COD order placed, Return requested, … |
| Pixels | Meta and Google conversions for the order |

The full list of event types is `OutboxEventType` (`packages/core/src/outbox/types.ts`).
