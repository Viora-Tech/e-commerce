# Changelog

What changed on the platform, newest first. Each entry links to its guide when there is one.

## 10 October 2026

About 80 changes landed today. The full list in plain words, grouped, is in
[What's new](/merchant-guide/reference/whats-new#10-october-2026). The short version:

- **Website builder.** Click any text to edit it, one bar above each block, easier section
  settings, 20 new sections with pictures in the Add panel, Pages, Media and Audit panels, zoom,
  version history, "Use content from…" lists, and product, collection and page templates in a
  Shopify theme import. Guide: [Online store](/merchant-guide/selling/online-store).
- **Stock and purchasing.** A new **Stock** menu, suppliers, purchase orders in any currency
  with freight and customs, landed cost, bills read by AI, and a bulk-edit spreadsheet for
  products. Guide: [Stock](/merchant-guide/stock).
- **Several warehouses and GST.** One order can ship from more than one warehouse, with an
  invoice per parcel and credit notes per GST number. Guide:
  [Several warehouses and GST](/merchant-guide/money/multi-warehouse-gst).
- **Profit.** A Profit card on every order and two profit reports. Guide:
  [Profit](/merchant-guide/money/profit).
- **Shipping.** A tabbed Shipments workspace, and a page for each abandoned checkout.
- **Growth and analytics.** Short links that count clicks and orders, "what marketing earned",
  a Live view that says how many people are on your store, and Umami visitor analytics. Guide:
  [Analytics](/merchant-guide/selling/analytics).
- **Admin polish.** A new look, discounts you can pause and duplicate, storage usage in Files,
  and recommended apps.

## 1 October 2026

- **Download your data, or delete your store.** A full copy of everything your store holds,
  as one zip, and a way for the owner to close the store for good. Both at the bottom of
  Settings → General. Guide:
  [Download your data or delete your store](/merchant-guide/settings/store-data).
- **Fair sharing between stores.** One store's big import, campaign or export no longer
  slows down other stores. Developers: [Architecture](/developer-docs/building/architecture),
  section 3, and [Key rotation](/developer-docs/operations/key-rotation).

## 28 September 2026

- **More ways for customers to sign in.** Google (with One Tap), Apple, Facebook and
  Truecaller, a sign-in link by email, passkeys, and "Remember this device". Each one is a
  switch in Settings → Customer accounts, and customers only see the ones you turned on.
  Guide: [Customer accounts](/merchant-guide/settings/customer-accounts). Developers:
  [Shopper login options](/developer-docs/building/shopper-login).
