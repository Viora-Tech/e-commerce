# Changelog

What changed on the platform, newest first. Each entry links to its guide when there is one.

## 28 September 2026

- **More ways for customers to sign in.** Google (with One Tap), Apple, Facebook and
  Truecaller, a sign-in link by email, passkeys, and "Remember this device". Each one is a
  switch in Settings → Customer accounts, and customers only see the ones you turned on.
  Guide: [Customer accounts](/merchant-guide/settings/customer-accounts). Developers:
  [Shopper login options](/developer-docs/building/shopper-login).

## 27 September 2026

- **Settings → Customer privacy and Settings → Customer accounts.** Cookie banner, marketing
  consent per channel, data requests, and sign-in by email, SMS or WhatsApp code.
  Guide: [Customer accounts](/merchant-guide/settings/customer-accounts).
- **Settings → Taxes & GST.**
- **Settings → Policies and Settings → Checkout.**
- **Hindi in the admin.**
- **Guided onboarding.**
- **EMI & pay later app.**
- **Cashfree connector finished.**
- **Product page UX.**
- **Storefront speed pass.**
- **Theme editor, part 2**, **Theme editor modern look**, **Dynamic sources in the theme
  editor** and **Build your own sections.**
- **Images built off the server** and **Shopify-import images processed automatically.**
- **Custom WebGL globe.**
- **Worker dev restarts.**
