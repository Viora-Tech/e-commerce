# Inventory

**Stock → Inventory** lists every product variant that tracks stock, with its counts at one
place at a time.

## What this is for

- Spotting what is low or sold out before shoppers do.
- Correcting a count after you counted the shelf.
- Adding stock that arrived outside a purchase order, or writing off something damaged.
- Knowing what your stock is worth at cost.

## What the numbers mean

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Available** | What you can sell right now. |
| **Committed** | Reserved for orders that are not packed yet. |
| **Incoming** | On a purchase order or transfer that has not arrived. |
| **On hand** | What is physically there. |

The top has four tiles: **SKUs tracked**, **Out of stock**, **Low stock** (5 or fewer available)
and **Stock value** (what is on hand, at cost). The tabs **All SKUs**, **Out of stock** and **Low
stock** filter the list. You can also filter by **Stock**, **Vendor** and **Product type**, and
search by name or SKU.

## Change a count

<Steps>

### Pick the place

Use **Location** at the top if you have more than one. Counts are per place. The place marked
**Online orders** is the one your online store sells from.

### Pick a reason

**Reason for edits** is **Correction**, **Received**, **Damaged**, **Returned** or **Theft or
loss**. It is saved with the change so the history makes sense later.

### Change the number

In the **Available** column press **−** or **+** to add or remove one, or type a new number. You
will see a short message such as "Glass mouse pad: 12 available".

</Steps>

To change many at once, tick the rows and choose **Adjust by** (add or remove the same number
from each; use a negative number to remove) or **Set on hand to** (every ticked row gets this
count). Add a **Note** if you like.

Select **Stock history** on a row to see every change: who, when, how many and why. **Export**
downloads the list as a CSV file.

<Callout type="info">
  To receive goods from a supplier, use a [purchase order](/merchant-guide/stock/purchase-orders)
  instead of typing the new number. Then the product's cost updates too.
</Callout>

## Add a place

Select **Add location** to add a warehouse, shop or partner: name, address, state, PIN code and
phone. Its state decides which GST number it bills under. A new place does not sell online until
you turn on **Ships online orders** for it. See
[Locations & warehouses](/merchant-guide/settings/locations).

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | Why it happens | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A product is missing from the list | **Track quantity** is off for it. | Open the product and turn on **Track quantity** under **Inventory**. |
| The number I typed went back | You may not have permission to change stock. | Ask the owner to give your role the inventory permission. |
| **Available** is lower than **On hand** | Some units are **Committed** to orders you have not packed. | Pack the orders and the numbers meet again. |
