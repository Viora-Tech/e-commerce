# Suppliers

Save each supplier once under **Stock › Suppliers**: name, country, the currency they bill
in, GSTIN (for Indian suppliers), contact details and payment terms. On a purchase order,
search for the supplier or click **New supplier**. A new order starts in that supplier's
currency.

## More ways to reach a supplier

Besides the main email and phone, the supplier's **Contact** card has **Other ways to reach
them**: add a WhatsApp or WeChat ID, a second phone or email, a website, or anything else, each
with an optional name like "Sales, Lily". The address field holds their full postal address.

## Edit many suppliers at once

On the Suppliers list, tick the suppliers you want and choose **Bulk edit**. Tick what to change
(the currency they bill in, their country, or payment terms) and click **Apply**; everything you
leave unticked stays as it is on each supplier. Leave payment terms empty to clear them.
**Delete** removes the selected suppliers; their purchase orders keep the supplier's name.
