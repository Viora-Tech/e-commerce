# Transfers

**Stock → Transfers** records stock moving from one of your places to another. The count at
each end stays right while the goods are on the road.

## What this is for

- Sending 50 mouse pads from your Jaipur warehouse to your Bengaluru warehouse.
- Topping up the place that sends online orders.
- Fixing an order that shows **Needs stock moved** (see
  [Locations & warehouses](/merchant-guide/settings/locations)).

If you have only one place, the page tells you there is nowhere to move stock to yet. Add a
warehouse, shop or partner place first.

## Make a transfer

<Steps>

### Start it

Select **Stock → Transfers → Create transfer**. Under **Route** pick the **Origin** and the
**Destination**. They must be different places.

### Add the products

Search under **Products** and add each variant, then type the **Quantity**. Next to it you see how
many are available at the origin. Add a note, such as the carrier or tracking number.

### Save the draft

Select **Save draft**. Nothing has moved yet. You can edit a draft, or delete it; deleting a
draft does not touch stock.

### Mark it as shipped

When the goods leave, select **Mark as shipped**. The stock leaves the origin and shows as
**incoming** at the destination. A shipped transfer can no longer be edited.

### Receive it

When it arrives, select **Receive transfer**. The stock is now on hand at the destination.

</Steps>

A transfer is numbered like **#T12** and is **Draft**, **In transit** or **Received**.
