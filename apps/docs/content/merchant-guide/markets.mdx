# Markets

Go to **Markets** in the sidebar. A market groups countries that shop in one currency and
language, like dirhams for the Gulf. Shoppers there see your prices converted, on an address of
their own such as `yourshop.com/ae`.

India is your **primary market**. Its country and its currency, the rupee, stay fixed: your
prices, GST and reports are kept in them. You can rename it and choose its languages.

## Add a market

1. Go to **Markets** and click **Add market**.
2. Give it a name and pick its **Countries**. A country can be in one market only; the region
   buttons (European Union, Gulf, North America, Australia and NZ) add a group at once.
3. Pick the **Currency**. Prices are converted from rupees, then shown and charged in it.
4. Pick a **Default language** and any **More languages** shoppers can switch to. Text you have not
   translated shows in the default language.
5. Choose **How shoppers reach it**:
   - **A subfolder of your address**, like `/ae` or `/en-us`.
   - **Its own domain**, one you connected in **Settings → Domains**, like `yourshop.ae`.
   - **Shopper's choice**: no address of its own; shoppers pick it in the country selector.
6. Under **Prices**, set a **Price adjustment** (a percent, -50 to 200), the **Rounding**, and
   optionally a **Fixed exchange rate** (leave it empty for the daily reference rate).
7. Choose who pays **Import duties**: the buyer on delivery, or collected at checkout.

## Turn a market on or off, or delete it

The **On** switch shows or hides a market from shoppers. Deleting a market sends its countries
back to your primary market. If it already has orders, it is turned off instead, so those orders
keep it.

## Who can use it

Markets follow the **Settings** permission: **Settings: read** to see them, **Settings: manage** to
add, change and delete them.

To schedule a new theme, checkout look or discounts, see [Rollouts](/merchant-guide/rollouts).
