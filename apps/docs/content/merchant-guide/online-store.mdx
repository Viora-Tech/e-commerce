# Online store

## Themes

**Online Store → Themes** shows the look of your storefront. One theme is live at a time;
you can keep others ready to publish, and pick from presets or customise colours, fonts and
spacing.

<Screenshot name="Themes list — live theme and presets" />

## The editor

Open a page (or the theme) in the editor and you'll see a narrow icon rail down the left side
with three panels:

- **Sections** — the page's building blocks, top to bottom, with reorder, duplicate and
  remove on each row.
- **Add** — search and browse sections to add, in two tabs (**Sections** and **Apps**, below).
  It fills the whole panel, with its own list scrolling inside.
- **Theme** — colours, fonts and other store-wide look and feel.

The canvas in the middle shows the page itself. A floating dock under the canvas switches the
preview between **Desktop**, **Tablet** and **Mobile** widths, toggles **Light**/**Dark**
preview (when your theme has a dark mode), and holds **Undo**/**Redo**.

### Selecting and editing a section

Hover a section on the canvas to see its name; select it to open a small floating toolbar
above it with **Move up**, **Move down**, **Duplicate** and **Remove**, plus its settings in
the panel on the right.

### Section settings

Every section's settings panel is grouped into four collapsible sections:

- **Layout** — content width (narrow, normal, wide or full), alignment and the gap between
  items inside it.
- **Spacing** — padding above and below, with separate sliders for mobile, tablet and desktop,
  so a section can be tighter on a phone than on a wide screen.
- **Style** — colour scheme, an optional background colour or image (with an overlay), corner
  rounding, a border toggle and a shadow.
- **Advanced** — show or hide the section on mobile or desktop, an entrance animation
  (fade, slide up or zoom in as it scrolls into view) and an anchor ID for linking straight to
  it.

Older sections you edited before this panel existed keep looking exactly the same — their old
colour scheme and padding are carried over into these groups automatically the first time you
open them.

### Apps tab and app blocks

The **Add** panel's **Apps** tab lists sections that installed apps add for you. The core
plugin always contributes at least one ("Pre-order countdown," a launch countdown for a
pre-order product); installed apps add more, for example:

- **Notify me** — a "back in stock" button shoppers can click on a sold-out product.
- **Delivery checker** — lets a shopper type their PIN code and see an estimated delivery
  date, powered by whichever courier you've connected.

If none of the apps that add blocks are installed yet, this tab explains which ones do
(Back in stock, Delhivery, Shiprocket), with a link to browse and install them.

## Saving and discarding

<Callout type="info">
  Changes in the editor aren't live until you save. The toolbar shows **Unsaved changes**
  while you're editing, and **All changes saved** once you save. If you want to throw away
  your edits, select **Discard**.
</Callout>

## Pages

**Online Store → Pages** lists every page built with the editor — title, URL, status and when
it was last updated. Select **Add page** to start a new one; each page also has its own
**Search engine listing** (title and description) and **Visibility**.
