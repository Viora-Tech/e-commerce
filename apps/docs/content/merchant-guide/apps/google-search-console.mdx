# Google Search Console

The **Google Search Console** app shows how shoppers find your store on Google: what they
searched for, which of your pages Google showed, how many people clicked, and where your
pages rank. It also points out the quickest wins: pages just off the first page of Google,
and pages or searches that are losing clicks.

[Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) is Google's free tool for website
owners. This app reads your numbers from it; it never changes anything in your Google account
except sending your sitemap when you ask.

## What you need

- A Google account that has your store in Search Console, as a **verified** property. A
  **domain property** (it looks like `sc-domain:yourstore.in`) is best: it counts `www`, no
  `www`, `http` and `https` together.
- Not set up yet? In Search Console click **Add property**, choose **Domain**, type your store's
  domain and follow Google's steps to verify it (a TXT record at your domain provider).

## Connect it

1. Go to **Apps**, find **Google Search Console** under **Connections** and click **Install**.
2. Open **Apps → Google Search Console → Settings**.
3. Click **Connect Google Search Console** and sign in with your Google account. Allow
   Search Console access when Google asks.
4. Back on Settings, pick your property. The one that matches your store's domain is marked
   **Matches your store**. Click **Use this property**.

Your numbers arrive within a minute. After that they update by themselves once a day.

## What you see

All numbers are for the **last 28 days** Google has final numbers for, compared with the 28
days before. Google's numbers trail by about three days.

- **Overview**: clicks, how often Google showed your store (**views**), click rate and average
  position, with the change since the 28 days before. Below: top searches, top pages, devices
  and countries.
- **Top searches**: what people typed on Google before they saw your store.
- **Top pages**: your products, collections and pages on Google. Click a name to edit it.
- **Almost page 1**: pages Google shows at position 8 to 20, just off the first page, at least
  100 times. A sharper search title and description, and a little more text, can move them up.
  **Improve** opens the product's SEO tab.
- **Losing traffic**: pages and searches with fewer clicks than before, biggest drop first.
- **Sitemaps**: the sitemaps Google knows for your property, whether it read them, and
  **Submit to Google** for your store's own sitemap (`/sitemap.xml`: every live product,
  collection and page).

**Refresh now** gets fresh numbers straight away. You can use it once every 15 minutes.

## On your products and the SEO report

- Each product's **SEO** tab gets a **Google search** card: the product's clicks, views,
  click rate and position, and the searches that found it.
- **Online store → SEO** adds two kinds of issue: products and pages Google shows often that
  get **few clicks**, and ones **almost on page 1**. They do not lower your SEO score; they
  are chances to win more clicks. **Fix with AI** can rewrite their search titles and
  descriptions.

## Disconnect

**Settings → Disconnect** removes the connection at Google and deletes the Google numbers
from your store. Search Console itself keeps everything; connect again any time.

## If something is wrong

- **"Google no longer accepts this connection"**: someone removed the access in your Google
  account, or changed the password. Click **Connect again** on Settings.
- **No properties listed**: this Google account has no Search Console properties. Add and
  verify your domain in Search Console, or connect with the account that has it.
- **"Not verified"** next to a property: Google gives no numbers for it until you verify it.
- **No numbers yet**: a new property can take a few days to collect data in Search Console.
- **Not available yet**: your platform has not set up its Google connection; ask your platform
  admin.
