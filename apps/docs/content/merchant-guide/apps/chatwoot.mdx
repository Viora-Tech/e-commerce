# Chatwoot (support inbox)

The **Chatwoot** app puts a chat bubble on every page of your store and sends the messages to
[Chatwoot](https://www.chatwoot.com), a free, open-source support inbox where your team answers
website chat, WhatsApp, email and more in one place.

- **Live chat** on your store, in our themes and in a theme you imported from Shopify.
- **Shoppers recognised**: a signed-in shopper's chat arrives with their name, email, phone, the
  date they became a customer, how many orders they placed, how much they spent and their last
  order.
- **Contacts kept in sync**: when a customer signs up, changes their details or pays, their
  Chatwoot contact is created or updated.
- **Conversations card** on each customer page and order page, with links straight into
  Chatwoot.

Chatwoot runs on its own: use [Chatwoot Cloud](https://app.chatwoot.com) or your own Chatwoot
server. The app only connects to it.

## What you need

- A Chatwoot account (Cloud or self-hosted) and a **Website** inbox in it. To add one: in
  Chatwoot, **Settings → Inboxes → Add inbox → Website**, type your store's address and finish
  the steps.
- For contacts and the Conversations card: an **API access token**. In Chatwoot, click your
  avatar → **Profile settings** and copy the **Access token** at the bottom.
- For recognising shoppers: the inbox's **identity secret**. In Chatwoot, open the website inbox
  → **Configuration** → **Identity validation**, copy the HMAC token and turn on **Enforce user
  identity validation**.

## Connect it

1. Go to **Apps**, find **Chatwoot** under **Connections** and click **Install**.
2. Open **Apps → Chatwoot**.
3. **Chatwoot address**: where your Chatwoot opens, for example `app.chatwoot.com` or
   `https://support.yourstore.in`. Tip: paste a full dashboard link and the account id fills
   itself in.
4. **Account id**: the number after `/app/accounts/` in your Chatwoot address bar.
5. **Website inbox token**: in the website inbox's **Configuration**, copy the `websiteToken`
   value from the script shown there.
6. Paste the **API access token** and the **identity secret**. Both are stored encrypted and are
   never shown again; paste a new one any time to replace it.
7. Click **Test connection**. It checks the token can open the account and the website token
   belongs to it. It creates nothing in Chatwoot.
8. Click **Save**.

The chat shows on your store straight away. Open your store in a private window to see it.

## Shoppers

- **Recognise signed-in shoppers** (on by default): a shopper who is signed in to your store
  chats as themselves, so you never ask "what's your order number?". The identity secret proves
  it is really them, so nobody can chat as somebody else.
- **Keep customers as Chatwoot contacts** (on by default): every new customer, every change to a
  customer's details, and every payment updates their Chatwoot contact. A shopper who chatted
  as a guest with the same email is joined to their customer record instead of being added
  twice.

Next to each chat, Chatwoot shows these contact attributes: `customer_since`, `orders_count`,
`total_spent` (in rupees), `last_order` and `last_order_at`. To show them neatly, add them in
Chatwoot under **Settings → Custom attributes** (applies to: Contact).

## Privacy and the cookie banner

Chatwoot keeps a cookie so a shopper's chat carries on from page to page. Under **Privacy →
Load the chat** pick when the chat loads:

- **Always** (default): a chat is something the shopper asks for, so it loads for everyone.
- **After the shopper allows analytics cookies**, or **marketing cookies**: the chat waits for
  that answer on your cookie banner.

The chat never loads on the sign-in pages, and never in the theme editor's preview.

## Conversations on customer and order pages

With an API access token saved, each customer page and order page has a **Conversations** card:
the shopper's latest chats with their status (Open, Pending, Snoozed, Resolved), the last
message and how long ago it was. Click one to open it in Chatwoot. An order placed by a guest
shows the conversations of the email on the order.

## Good to know

- Customers imported in bulk (from Shopify) become Chatwoot contacts the next time they sign in,
  change their details or pay.
- If Chatwoot is busy or down, the sync tries again by itself a few times; a problem that does not
  clear shows on **Apps → Installed apps**.
- Uninstalling the app keeps your settings, so installing it again picks up where you left off.
