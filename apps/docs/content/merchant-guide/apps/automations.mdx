# Automations

## What this is for

Some jobs are the same every time: tag orders over ₹5,000 as "high value", hold risky cash on
delivery orders, WhatsApp the packer when an order is paid, email the team when stock runs low.
**Automations** does them for you, on every order, customer or product, the moment something
happens. It works like Shopify Flow.

Automations is a free app that every store has from the start. Your marketing flows (abandoned
checkout, welcome, win-back) live here too.

## How it works

A **workflow** has three parts:

1. A **trigger**: what starts it. For example **Order paid**, **COD order placed**, **Return
   requested**, **Customer created**, **First order placed**, **Customer lapsed**, **Stock running
   low**, **Out of stock**, **Checkout abandoned**, a **Scheduled time**, or **Run on an order /
   customer / product** by hand.
2. **Conditions**: checks such as **Order total**, **Is cash on delivery**, **Shipping state**,
   **Shipping PIN code**, **Order tags**, **Sales channel** or **Number of items**. **Then** runs
   when the check is true, **Otherwise** when it is not.
3. **Actions**: what to do. **Add order tags**, **Add customer tags**, **Add order note**, **Set
   order extra field**, **Add product to collection**, **Hold fulfilment**, **Release fulfilment
   hold**, **Fulfil order**, **Cancel order**, **Email your team**, **WhatsApp your team**, **Send
   marketing email**, **Send marketing WhatsApp**, **Send order update to customer**, **Send HTTP
   request**, and more. A **Wait** step pauses from 1 minute to 60 days.

Workflows run in the background a few seconds after the event. Every run is logged step by step,
and each order, customer and product shows the automations that touched it. Other apps can add
their own triggers and actions.

## Start from a template

Go to **Apps → Automations → Templates**. Each template makes a **paused** copy you can change
before you switch it on.

| Template | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Tag high-value orders** | Orders of ₹5,000 or more get a "High value" tag and your team gets an email |
| **Hold risky COD orders** | Large cash on delivery orders are held from fulfilment with a note until you check them |
| **Tag repeat customers** | Customers with three or more orders get a "Repeat" tag |
| **Low stock alert** | Emails your team when a product is running low |
| **New arrivals collection** | Adds every new product to a collection you pick |
| **Alert on return requests** | Emails your team when a shopper asks to return an order |
| **WhatsApp the packer** | Sends your packer a WhatsApp message when an order is paid |
| **Remind unpaid orders** | Every morning, reminds shoppers of UPI and bank transfer orders still unpaid after 3 days |
| **Cancel unconfirmed COD orders** | Cancels COD orders not confirmed in 48 hours, after you approve each one |

Templates that need an app you have not installed say so.

## Build or change a workflow

<Steps>

### Create

Select **Create workflow**, then a template or **Start from scratch**, and pick the trigger.

### Add steps

On the canvas, add a condition ("Check something, then go one way or the other"), an action, or
a wait. Select a step to set it up on the right. Your changes are saved as a **draft**; the live
version keeps running until you publish.

### Test it

Select **Test with a record** and pick a real order, customer or product. The test walks the
draft and shows what each step would do. **Nothing changes and no message is sent.**

### Publish

Select **Publish** and tick **Switch it on now**. The new version starts with the next event;
runs already going finish on the version they started on. Workflows with sensitive steps (such as
cancelling orders) ask you to type the workflow name.

</Steps>

## Safety

- **Ask before running**: any step can wait for a person. The run stops at that step and waits in
  **Needs approval** until someone with the step's permission approves or declines it. Cancelling
  orders asks first by default, and only the owner can turn that off.
- **If this step fails**: stop the run, or **Carry on to the next step**.
- **Run once per record every**: stops a workflow running twice for the same order or customer in
  a short time.
- Steps that **message customers** follow consent and unsubscribe rules; shoppers who did not
  agree to marketing are skipped.
- **Pause** a workflow from the list any time; runs already going can finish or stop.

<Callout>
  Test every new workflow on a real order before you switch it on. The test shows the exact path
  it would take, with nothing changed.
</Callout>

## Runs, limits and settings

- **Runs** lists every run with its status (**Completed**, **Waiting**, **Failed**, **Skipped**,
  …). Open one to see each step and why it went the way it did.
- **Settings** shows this month's active workflows and runs against your plan's limit. Near the
  limit you are warned; at the limit new runs are skipped until next month or a bigger plan.
  Marketing automations from Growth are not counted. Secrets for **Send HTTP request** steps are
  kept here.

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Does not work with this trigger" | The action needs a different record (an order action on a customer trigger). Pick another action or trigger |
| A workflow shows **Turned off** | A step stopped working, often because its app was uninstalled. Open it to see the problem |
| Nothing ran | Check the workflow is active, the trigger happened after you published, and **Runs** for a skipped run's reason |
