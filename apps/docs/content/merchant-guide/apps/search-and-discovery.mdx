# Search & discovery

Install **Search & discovery** from the App store. It has five pages: Overview, Synonyms,
Filters, Boosts and Recommendations.

## Overview

What shoppers search for on your store and what happens next, for the last 7, 30 or 90 days,
compared with the period before.

- **Search funnel**: sessions that searched, then clicked a result, added a product to the cart
  and placed an order. A step counts when it comes within 30 minutes of the step before.
- **Top searches**: the most searched words, with how many products they found and how often
  shoppers clicked a result.
- **Searches with no results**: shoppers looked for something and found nothing. Click
  **Add synonym** to tie their word to one your products use (Synonyms opens with the word
  ready), or **Pin a product** to show a product for it anyway.
- **Searches with no clicks**: the search found products but nobody clicked one. Usually the
  best match is missing from the top: **Pin a product**.
- **Recommendations**: how often the "You may also like" and "Pairs well with" rows on product
  pages were seen, clicked, and led to an add to cart.

Only shoppers who allow analytics in the cookie banner are counted. The log keeps the search
words (never ones that look like an email address or a phone number), how many products were
found, and an anonymous browser code. No names, no IP addresses. Entries are deleted after
90 days.

## Synonyms

Words that mean the same thing to your shoppers, one group per line (`kb, keyboard`). A search for
any word in a group also finds the others.

## Filters

Choose which filters appear on collection and search pages, and in what order. Until you save
your own list, the store shows the default filters: availability, price, every variant option,
then product type, brand and tags.

Click **Add filter** to pick where a filter's values come from:

- **Built in**: availability, price, product type, brand, gift cards or products, or every tag.
- **Tag groups**: tags shaped `prefix-value` become one filter. `hand-large`, `hand-medium` and
  `hand-small` give a filter **Hand** with Large, Medium and Small. Rename it (for example to
  "Hand size") in the name box. You can also type any other prefix.
- **Product fields**: product extra fields of a simple type (text, choice, number, yes/no).
- **Variant options**: Size, Colour and the rest.

Use the arrows to change the order and the bin to remove a filter. A filter with nothing to pick
on a page is hidden there. **Use default filters** goes back to the default list.

The same list drives our themes and imported Shopify themes (`collection.filters` and
`search.filters`).

## Boosts

Put the right products first in search results.

- **Pinned products**: click **Pin products**, type a search (capitals, extra spaces and a
  question mark do not matter) and choose up to 10 products in order. For that search they show
  first, even when its words would not find them. Up to 200 searches can have pins.
- **Boost and bury**: products with a tag, product type or vendor move up (**Boost**) or go to the
  end (**Bury**) in every search. The value must match the product exactly, so pick it from the
  list. A boosted product climbs past weaker matches; a buried one always comes after the rest.
  Up to 50 rules.

Boosts change the order only when results are sorted by relevance (the default); sorting by
price or name ignores them. Collection pages are not affected.

## Recommendations

Choose the products shown under a product page: **You may also like** (similar items, other
colours or sizes) and **Pairs well with** (add-ons, accessories, refills). Click
**Choose a product**, then **Add products** in either row, order them with the arrows and click
**Save**. Up to 10 per row.

Your picks show first, then the store fills the row by itself (products from the same
collections, type, brand and tags, then best sellers). Draft and archived picks are skipped on
the store. Picks brought over from a Shopify import still work until you save your own for that
row. Imported Shopify themes use the same picks.
