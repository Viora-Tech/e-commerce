# International couriers

DHL Express and FedEx carry orders from India to other countries. Connect your own account
with either (or both). They sit beside your Indian courier: an order going abroad shows DHL
Express and FedEx rates in **Create shipment**, and an order inside India keeps using
Delhivery, Shiprocket or RapidShyp. Neither app carries parcels within India.

## What you need first

- **A business account with the courier.** A DHL Express account number or a FedEx account
  number, both 9 digits. Open one on [mydhl.express.dhl](https://mydhl.express.dhl/in/en/home.html)
  or [fedex.com](https://www.fedex.com/en-in/open-account.html) if you don't have one.
- **API keys.** DHL: create an app on [developer.dhl.com](https://developer.dhl.com/api-reference/dhl-express-mydhl-api)
  with **DHL Express - MyDHL API**. FedEx: create a project on
  [developer.fedex.com](https://developer.fedex.com/api/en-in/home.html) with Ship, Rate, Pickup
  Request and Track, and link your shipping account to it. Both give you test keys at once;
  live keys come after the courier approves you.
- **Your IEC** (Importer-Exporter Code, 10 characters, from DGFT). It goes on the commercial
  invoice; Indian customs needs it to clear a commercial export.
- **Your GSTIN** in **Settings › Taxes & GST › GST & invoices**. It goes on the invoice as the
  exporter's tax number.
- **An HS code on every product you sell abroad**, in the product's **Shipping** section,
  with **Country of origin** if it was not made in India. Customs reads the commercial
  invoice line by line, so a product without an HS code cannot be booked.

## Connect DHL Express or FedEx

<Tabs items={['DHL Express', 'FedEx']}>
<Tabs.Tab>

From **Apps**, install **DHL Express**, then open its **Connect** page.

<Steps>

### Pick Test or Live

**Test** uses DHL's sandbox: no real parcels, no charges. DHL's sandbox only tracks its own
test waybills, so a test booking shows no tracking. Switch to **Live** with your live keys.

### Paste your keys and account

Enter **API key**, **API secret** and **DHL account number**. Test keys only work in Test
mode, live keys only in Live mode.

### Add your export details

Enter the **Origin PIN code** and **Origin city** parcels leave from (DHL needs both to quote),
and your **IEC**. Leave **Book a DHL pickup with each shipment** on unless you drop parcels at
a DHL point or already have a daily pickup.

### Test and save

**Test connection** checks your keys with a read-only address lookup at DHL. Nothing is
booked. Select **Save and connect**.

</Steps>

</Tabs.Tab>
<Tabs.Tab>

From **Apps**, install **FedEx**, then open its **Connect** page.

<Steps>

### Pick Test or Live

**Test** uses FedEx's sandbox and its test key pair. **Live** uses your production key pair.

### Paste your keys and account

Enter **API key**, **Secret key** and **FedEx account number**.

### Add your export details

Enter the **Origin PIN code**, **Origin city** and your **IEC**. With **Request a FedEx pickup
after each booking** on, a parcel booked before 3 pm is collected the same day, later ones the
next day from 10 am.

### Test and save

**Test connection** asks FedEx for a sign-in token with your keys. Nothing is booked. Select
**Save and connect**.

</Steps>

</Tabs.Tab>
</Tabs>

## Ship an order abroad

Open the order and select **Create shipment**, as for any order
([Shipments](/merchant-guide/orders/shipments)). For an address outside India the rate table
lists every DHL Express and FedEx service with its price in rupees, its transit days and
what it charges for (freight, fuel). Your Indian couriers show "Delivers within India only".

When you book, the app sends the courier:

- the shopper's name, address and phone,
- one customs line per item: name, quantity, price in rupees, HS code, country of origin and
  weight (an item without its own weight gets a share of the parcel's),
- your GSTIN and IEC, and the order number as the invoice number.

**Print label** opens one PDF: the label first, then the commercial invoice. Print the invoice
copies and send them with the parcel.

<Callout type="info">
  The customs value is the order's value in rupees, and the shopper pays any import duty in
  their country (DAP). Exports are prepaid: cash on delivery is not offered abroad.
</Callout>

## Tracking and cancelling

Neither courier pushes tracking updates, so both are checked on the same polling schedule as
RapidShyp rather than updating instantly. **Cancel** works before the courier collects the parcel:

- **FedEx** cancels the shipment.
- **DHL Express** cancels the pickup booked with it. DHL has no "cancel waybill": a waybill
  that is never handed over is never billed.

## Problems

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "needs an HS code on every item" | Add the HS code in the product's **Shipping** section, then book again. |
| "did not accept this API key and secret" | Copy both keys again, and check **Test** or **Live** matches them. |
| "needs the destination city" | The order's address has no city. Edit the address, then quote again. |
| A courier's own message, such as "The postal code is invalid" | Fix what it names in the shopper's address and book again. |
