# Forms

## What this is for

Themes imported from Shopify often have a **Contact us** page or an "ask an expert" form. On
Shopify those forms send messages to the shop. Here, the **Forms** app does that job: without it,
the theme's forms have nowhere to send and show as unavailable.

You only need it if your theme was imported from Shopify and has contact forms.

## How it works

- A shopper fills in the theme's form and sends it.
- The message is saved in **Apps → Forms → Submissions**, with the shopper's name, email, the
  message and the page it was sent from.
- Each one is also emailed to your store's contact address (**Settings → Store details**).

## Set it up

<Steps>

### Install

Go to **Apps → App Store**, find **Forms** and select **Install**.

### Check your contact address

Make sure **Settings → Store details** has the email you want messages sent to.

### Try it

Open your store's contact page, send a test message, and check it shows under **Submissions**
and arrives by email.

</Steps>

<Callout>
  Reply from your own email: the shopper's address is on the message. For a shared team inbox
  with WhatsApp and chat, see [Inbox](/merchant-guide/apps/inbox).
</Callout>
