# Reviews

The **Reviews** app shows what customers say about your products, right on the product page,
with stars, photos and your replies. It replaces Judge.me when you move from Shopify.

## What this is for

- Shoppers trust a product with real reviews from real buyers. Each review from someone who
  bought the product gets a **Verified buyer** badge.
- You stay in control: you choose which reviews go live straight away and which wait for you.
- Customers are asked for a review by email a few days after their order arrives, so reviews
  keep coming without you chasing anyone.
- Your old Judge.me reviews come over with their stars, dates, photos and your replies.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Apps**, find **Reviews** under **Built-in apps** and click **Install**.
2. **Reviews** now shows in the sidebar under **Apps**.
3. Your product pages show the reviews section (with **Write a review**) wherever your theme
   has the **Product reviews** section.

## The Reviews page

**Apps → Reviews** lists every review, newest first.

- The four boxes at the top: your **average rating**, how many reviews are **published**, how
  many are **waiting for you**, and how many have **photos**.
- The tabs: **All**, **Waiting**, **Published**, **Hidden**.
- Search by product, customer or words in the review. **Filter** by stars, product, or reviews
  with photos.
- Tick several reviews to **Approve**, **Hide** or **Delete** them together.

Click a review to open it. There you can:

- **Approve and publish** it, or **Hide** it.
- **Reply**. Your reply shows under the review on your store, and the customer gets an email
  the first time you reply. If AI is on for your store (Settings → AI), **Write with AI**
  suggests a reply you can change before posting.
- Switch **Show on product page** on or off.
- Switch **Feature on homepage** on: featured reviews come first in your theme's reviews
  showcase.
- **Delete review** removes it for good.

The **Activity** box shows what happened: the request email, when it was written, whether it
was held for you and why, and when it was published or answered.

## Settings

**Apps → Reviews → Settings.**

| Setting | What it means | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Publishing** | **Publish right away**, **Publish 4★ and 5★ right away, hold the rest**, or **Hold every review for approval**. | Publish 4★ and 5★, hold the rest |
| **Always hold reviews with photos** | Photos wait for you, so you can check them first. | On |
| **Who can write a review** | **Only customers who bought the product** (from the link in their review email, or signed in to their account) or **Anyone**. | Only buyers |
| **Most reviews one customer can send in a day** | A limit per email address. Bots are always blocked with a hidden trap field and a limit per visitor. | 3 |
| **Send review request emails** | Ask customers for a review after delivery. | On |
| **Send requests by** | **Email**, **WhatsApp** or **Email and WhatsApp**. See [Requests on WhatsApp](#requests-on-whatsapp). | Email |
| **Send after delivery** | How many days after the order is delivered. | 7 days |
| **Reminder if no review** | One reminder this many days later. 0 sends none. | 5 days |
| **Email subject** | `{{ product.title }}` becomes the product's name. **Preview email** shows it. | `How was your {{ product.title }}?` |
| **Show star ratings on product cards** | Stars on product cards in your theme's collections and search. | On |
| **Show customer photos** | Photos on review cards. | On |
| **Sort reviews by** | Most helpful, newest, highest or lowest rating first. | Most helpful |

### Asking for reviews

The emails go out by themselves once an order is delivered (by the courier's tracking, or when
you mark it delivered). Every email has a **Stop review emails** link; customers who used it,
or who unsubscribed from your marketing emails, are never asked again.

### Requests on WhatsApp

Pick **WhatsApp** or **Email and WhatsApp** under **Send requests by** to reach customers on
the phone number of their order. It uses your WhatsApp connection (Settings → Notifications) and,
like every WhatsApp message a business starts, needs templates approved by Meta:

| Template name | Body placeholders |
| --- | --- |
| `review_request` | `{{1}}` customer name, `{{2}}` store name, `{{3}}` review link |
| `review_reminder` | the same |

Orders without a phone number get nothing on WhatsApp (with **Email and WhatsApp** they still
get the email). The **Send after delivery** and **Reminder** days apply to both. Customers who
used the stop link are not asked again on either channel.

### Request reviews from past orders

Just switched from Shopify, or never asked before? Click **Ask for reviews** on the Reviews page
and pick when the orders were delivered (the last 60 days to start with). The dialog counts the
orders that will be asked before anything is sent: delivered orders with no review, never asked,
whose customer did not opt out. Requests go out 200 orders an hour, so a long list trickles out
over a few hours instead of all at once; one run asks up to 2,000 orders, newest first.

## Bring your reviews over from Judge.me

1. In your Shopify admin, open **Judge.me → Settings → Data export → Export reviews**, export
   all reviews and download the CSV file Judge.me emails you.
2. In **Apps → Reviews**, click **Import from Judge.me** and choose that file.
3. Check the numbers: **Ready to import**, **Store reviews**, **No matching product** and
   **Photos to copy**. Each review is matched to your product by its SKU, then by its Shopify
   product ID (products brought over with the Shopify importer remember it), then by its
   product handle. Products the file names that are not in your store are listed by handle.
4. Choose whether to copy photos, publish the reviews straight away, and import reviews with
   no matching product as store reviews. Then click **Import**.

What comes over: stars, title, text, reviewer name, the original date, the verified buyer
badge, your reply and its date, and photos. Reviews Judge.me marked as spam or unpublished
come in hidden; ones not yet curated there wait for your approval. The reviewer's email is
kept private (never shown on your store), and IP address and location are not imported.

**Store reviews** are reviews of your shop rather than one product (Judge.me leaves their
product empty). They show in your theme's review showcases and in the store-wide rating and
count, not on product pages. On the Reviews page they say **Store review** in the Product
column.

Photos are copied into your **Files** in the background, with location data removed. It is
safe to import the same file again, or a newer export later: reviews already imported are
skipped, so nothing is duplicated.

## Show reviews around your store

Once the app is installed, open **Online Store › Themes › Customize**, choose **Add section**
and go to the **Apps** tab. Under **Reviews** you can add:

- **Reviews carousel**: your reviews in a row of cards that moves by itself (you can turn that
  off, and shoppers can pause it). Choose how many, the lowest star rating, the card style and
  whether to show the average, product names, photos and dates.
- **Review testimonials**: a few of your best reviews as large quotes.
- **All reviews**: every review with the star breakdown, and chips to see one star rating or
  only reviews with photos. Add it to a page such as **Reviews** (`/pages/reviews`).
- **Review photos**: customers' review photos in a grid; a photo opens with its review.
- **Review snippet**: on a product page, the rating and a short quote from the best review.
  Place it just under the product section, near Add to cart.

The carousel, testimonials, all reviews and photos sections show the whole store's reviews, or
only the products you list (their handles, separated by commas).

For a **floating reviews tab** on every page, go to the theme editor's **Apps** panel and turn
on **Floating reviews tab**. It sits on the side of the screen with your rating and opens your
latest reviews.

Only published reviews show, with the reviewer's first name and last initial. A section with no
reviews to show stays hidden on your store.

## Imported Shopify themes

If your store uses an [imported Shopify theme](/merchant-guide/online-store/import-a-shopify-theme),
its review parts work with this app once it is installed: the stars on product cards, the
**Reviews showcase** and **Review stack** sections, and the comparison table's rating row all
show your reviews.

## Good to know

- Reviews for products you have not brought over are left out of a Judge.me import unless you
  import them as store reviews. Bring the product over, then import again to attach them.
- Average ratings and counts update straight away when you approve, hide, delete or import.
- When a customer asks for their data to be deleted, their reviews stay as text from "A
  customer", without their name or email.
