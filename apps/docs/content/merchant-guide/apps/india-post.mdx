# India Post

India Post reaches every PIN code in India, including the ones private couriers skip. The
India Post app prices each parcel from India Post's published Speed Post tariff, prints an
address label with a barcode, and keeps the parcel's status on the order.

<Callout type="info">
  India Post has no booking or tracking API for online shops (its booking API is only for
  contracted bulk customers). So you **book each parcel at the post office** and type the article
  number from the receipt; the app does everything else.
</Callout>

## Set it up

From **Apps**, install **India Post**, then open its **Connect** page.

<Steps>

### Enter your post office PIN code

The PIN code of the post office where you hand in parcels. Rates and delivery times are worked
out from it.

### Add your business account, if you have one

- **Speed Post customer ID**: your India Post business (BNPL) customer ID. It is printed on the
  label.
- **I have contract rates**: price parcels from India Post's contractual card instead of the
  counter rates.
- **Collect cash on delivery**: only if your India Post business account collects COD for you.
  Add the **COD fee per parcel** from your agreement so the rate you see includes it.

Without a business account, counter rates apply and every parcel is prepaid.

### Optional: a data.gov.in API key

Sign up free at [data.gov.in](https://www.data.gov.in), open **My Account** and copy your API
key. With it the app looks each PIN code up in India Post's own directory and shows its post
office and district. Without it, rates and delivery times still work.

### Test and save

**Test connection** checks the PIN code; nothing is booked.

</Steps>

## Ship an order

1. Pack the parcel and book it at the post office counter as **Speed Post** (or Speed Post with
   registration). Keep the receipt.
2. In the admin, open the order and choose **Create shipment**. Pick **India Post Speed Post**
   (or **Speed Post, registered**) in the courier list; the price shown is India Post's tariff
   plus 18 % GST.
3. Type the **article number** from the receipt, like `EE123456785IN`. Its last digits are a
   check digit, so a typo is caught before it is saved.
4. **Print label** opens a 4 × 6 inch label with the barcode, both addresses and, for COD, the
   amount to collect.

## Keep the status up to date

India Post sends no tracking to apps, so open **Apps → India Post**. Each parcel on the way has
a **Track on India Post** link (paste the article number there) and a status you set: booked,
handed in, in transit, out for delivery, delivered or returned. **Delivered** closes the order
and **Returned to you** starts the return (RTO) task, just like a courier scan. Shoppers see the
same status and a link to India Post's tracker.

## Rates and delivery times

Rates come from India Post's Speed Post Parcel tariff effective 1 August 2026 (retail or
contractual card), by weight and zone:

| Zone | When | Delivery (India Post's standard) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local | Same city or district | 1-2 days |
| Metro | Between Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru and Hyderabad | 1-3 days |
| Within state | Same state | 1-4 days |
| Other states | Everywhere else | 4-5 days |
| North East, J&K, Ladakh, islands | Charged as other states | 4-7 days (our estimate) |

Delivery through a branch post office takes a day more, so the dates shown already include it.
If your receipt shows a different price (a negotiated card), paste your own rates under
**Connect → Your own rates**.
