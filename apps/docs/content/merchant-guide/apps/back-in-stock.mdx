# Back in stock

Back in stock is a built-in app. Install it from the [App store](/merchant-guide/apps/app-store). It adds a **Notify me** block that you place in the [website builder](/merchant-guide/online-store/website-builder), and its pages are Overview, Signups and Settings.

The messages use a standard template: you cannot edit their text. A shopper who signs up by email first gets a **confirm** email (one tap to confirm the alert); when the item is back they get the **alert**, with your store's name, the product and a link to buy it. Every message has a link to stop the alerts. **Settings** chooses the channels (email, and WhatsApp if your store has it) and how many alerts go out per minute.
