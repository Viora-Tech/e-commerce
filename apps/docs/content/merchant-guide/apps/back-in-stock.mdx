# Back in stock

Back in stock is a built-in app. Install it from the [App store](/merchant-guide/apps/app-store). It adds a **Notify me** block that you place in the [website builder](/merchant-guide/online-store/website-builder), and its pages are Overview, Signups and Settings. Edit its messages in the app, not in **Settings → Notifications**.
