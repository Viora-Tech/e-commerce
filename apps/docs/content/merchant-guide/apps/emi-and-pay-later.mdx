# EMI & pay later

**EMI & pay later** is a built-in app. It adds a short line under the price on your product
pages, like **or ₹1,000/month with EMI or pay later**, so shoppers see a big purchase can be
split into monthly payments. With Razorpay it shows Razorpay's own offers box instead, with
your real banks, plans and offers.

## What this is for

- Helping shoppers say yes to higher-priced products.
- Showing no-cost EMI or bank offers you already set up in Razorpay, before checkout.

## Before you start

The app only shows offers shoppers can actually use, so two things need to be on first:

1. **Razorpay or Cashfree is taking payments** (Settings → Payments).
2. **EMI is on at checkout**: in Settings → Payments, under "Shoppers can pay with", turn on
   **EMI**.

The app's page tells you, in one line, which of these is still missing and links to it.

## Set it up

<Steps>

### Install

Open **Apps**, find **EMI & pay later** under Built-in apps, and select **Install**.

### Pick who gives the EMI

Open **Apps → EMI & pay later**. Under **Who gives the EMI**, pick the payment company your
checkout uses:

- **Razorpay** shows Razorpay's offers box, with your banks, tenures and offers from your
  Razorpay account.
- **Cashfree** shows a simple monthly price.

### Set the lowest price

Under **Lowest price**, enter the cheapest product that should show the line. The default is
₹1,000, which is where most lenders start. Cheaper products show nothing.

### Save

Select **Save**. The line at the top says **Live** once shoppers can see it. Open any product
over your lowest price on your store to check.

</Steps>

## How the monthly price is worked out

The simple line divides the price by 12 months and rounds up, the way a no-cost EMI plan
looks: a ₹12,000 product shows **or ₹1,000/month**. It is an estimate; the real plan (tenure,
interest, eligibility) is decided by the bank or lender at checkout.

With **Razorpay**, Razorpay's offers box replaces the line once it loads and shows the real
plans for your account. If it can't load (a slow connection, or an ad blocker), the simple
line stays, so there is always something under the price.

## Where it shows

- **Under the price** on every product page, following the variant the shopper picks. The
  theme editor's **EMI hint under the price** switch on the product section turns it off.
- **Anywhere else on the product page**: in the theme editor, **Add → Apps → EMI offers**.
  This copy uses the product's first available variant.

Only one Razorpay offers box can show on a page. If you place both, one of them shows it and
the other shows the simple line, so there is no need to add the block when the line under the
price is on.

## Dark mode, phones and motion

- The simple line uses your theme's colours, so it looks right in light and dark mode.
- In **dark mode** the simple line shows instead of Razorpay's box, so shoppers never get a
  bright white panel on a dark page.
- It fits phone screens, and it only fades in when the shopper's phone allows animations.

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | Why it happens | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nothing shows under the price | EMI is off at checkout, or the gateway isn't set up. | Follow the link at the top of **Apps → EMI & pay later**. |
| Nothing shows on one product | It costs less than your lowest price. | Lower the **Lowest price**. |
| Simple line instead of Razorpay's box | Cashfree is picked, the page is in dark mode, or Razorpay's box couldn't load. | Pick Razorpay, or check in light mode. |
| Razorpay's box shows no offers | No EMI or offers are set up in your Razorpay account. | Set them up in the Razorpay Dashboard. |

## FAQ

<Faq>

<FaqItem question="Does this cost anything?">

The app is free. EMI and pay later fees are between you and your payment company, the same as
any other payment.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is my Razorpay secret key shared?">

No. Razorpay's offers box only needs your public **Key ID** (the `rzp_...` value), the same one
checkout already uses in the shopper's browser. Your secret key stays encrypted on the server.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What happened to the old EMI hint?">

Stores without this app keep the older built-in "EMI from ₹X/month" hint when Razorpay EMI is
on. Once you install this app, it takes over that spot.

</FaqItem>

</Faq>
