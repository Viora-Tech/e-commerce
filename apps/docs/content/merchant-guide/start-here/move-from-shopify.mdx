# Move your store from Shopify

The **Shopify importer** app (Apps → Shopify importer) copies your Shopify store into this one.
It only reads from Shopify; nothing there changes. Every import is previewed first, and running
it again updates what it brought over before instead of making copies.

## What comes over

| In Shopify | Here | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Products, variants, stock, images | Products | Images are copied and optimised. |
| Collections (manual and smart) | Collections | Smart rules we cannot express become a manual collection with its current products. |
| Customers | Customers | Matched by email, then phone. |
| Orders | Orders | Read-only history: no stock change, no messages, no invoices. |
| Pages, blog posts, menus, URL redirects | Pages, Blog, Navigation, Redirects | |
| **Files** (Content → Files) | **Files** | Pictures (up to 50 MB) and mp4 videos (up to 1 GB). |
| **Metaobjects** | **Content types** and their **entries** | Links between entries, to products, collections and files are kept. |
| **Metafields** on products and collections | **Extra fields** | Each Shopify field becomes an extra field of the matching type. |
| **Discounts** | **Discounts** | Code and automatic discounts our discount model can express. |
| **Gift cards** | **Gift cards** | Balances, with a **new code** (see below). |

## Run an import

1. Open **Apps → Shopify importer → Connect store**. If you see **Install the Koomerce app**,
   type your store address (`your-store.myshopify.com`), press **Install**, and approve the app
   on Shopify: you come back connected, with every scope below already given. Otherwise connect
   your own Shopify app (see [Apps](/merchant-guide/apps/app-store#connect-your-shopify-store)).
2. Press **Test connection**. Any scope listed under **Missing** has to be added to the app in
   Shopify first (list below).
3. Under **What to import**, tick what you want. Everything is ticked to start with.
4. Press **Preview import**. Nothing is written yet. The preview shows how many of each thing
   would be new or updated, a few samples, **What stays in Shopify** and a list of problems.
5. Press **Import now**. You can leave the page; the import keeps going.

**Import within a day of the preview and it goes faster.** The import reuses what the preview
already read from Shopify (products, collections, customers, orders, pages, blog posts and
files) and only asks Shopify for what changed since. The run page says so: "Using the preview
from … + 3 changes since". Stock is always read fresh. After a day, or for menus, redirects,
content types, extra fields, discounts and gift cards, the import reads Shopify again.

Run it again any time (**Run again**): records it brought over before are updated, never
duplicated.

**Before you import.** Under "What to import" a card lists anything that isn't set up yet,
with a **Fix this** button: no file storage connected (connect Cloudflare R2 or Amazon S3, or
images are skipped), a Shopify access the app is missing (that one thing can't be ticked
until you add it), or orders chosen without products. You can still preview with warnings.

**See what is left.** The importer's first page lists every type with Shopify's count, how
many are here, and what is still missing, with a button on each row (**Import 3**, **Copy
images**, **Copy files**) and **Import remaining** for all of it. Only the missing records are
brought over; everything already here is left alone. On an import's page, problems are grouped
by cause ("1,400 images and files not copied: file storage isn't working") with one fix each.
A row only says **Done** when Shopify's number is known and everything is here. If it is not
known yet, the row says **Not checked yet** with a **Run a preview** button. **Product images**
always has its own row: how many images your Shopify products have, how many are on your
products here, and **Copy images** for the rest (each picture is downloaded once, never twice).

**Stop an import.** While an import is waiting or running, press **Stop import**. What's
already imported stays; the page says who stopped it and when. Bring in the rest later with
**Run again**, **Retry**, or **Import remaining**.

**Retry only what was missed.** When an import finishes with problems, press **Retry the 12
that were missed**: only those records, images or files are brought over again; everything
that already worked is left alone. **Import everything again** still runs the whole import.

If an import ever stops on its own (the server restarted mid-way), the page says **The import
stopped unexpectedly** and offers **Try again**. Trying again is safe: it runs again without
duplicating anything.

An image or file that Shopify is slow to send is tried three times; if it still does not come
through, the import carries on without it and lists it as a problem, so **Retry the ones that
were missed** can bring it over later. If nothing at all moves for 10 minutes, the import stops
with **The import stopped moving** and offers **Try again**.

## What stays in Shopify

The run page has a card called **What stays in Shopify**. It lists, in plain words and with a
count, everything that did not come over or arrived changed. The problems list below it names
each record. What you can see there:

- **Extra fields on other things.** Extra fields here are on products and collections only for
  now. Values on variants, customers, orders, pages, blog posts and your shop are counted
  ("40 extra field values on customers") and stay in Shopify.
- **Field types with no match.** Money, rating, measurements (weight, volume, size), JSON, and
  links to variants, pages or customers. The field and its values stay in Shopify. Colours and
  IDs come over as text; date and time fields keep the date; link fields keep the web address.
- **Files we cannot hold yet.** PDFs and other documents (download them from Shopify), YouTube
  or Vimeo links (add them to a section by their link), 3D models, and videos over 1 GB.
- **Discounts our model cannot express.** Buy X get Y, discounts made by Shopify apps, discounts
  for chosen customers or segments, a minimum number of items, single variants, an amount off
  each item, free shipping limited by country or shipping price, and codes with characters other
  than letters, numbers and hyphens. A Shopify discount with several codes becomes one discount
  per code here. A minimum purchase amount here counts the whole cart, not only the items the
  discount applies to.
- **Gift cards**, explained next.

## Gift cards get a new code

Shopify never shares a gift card's full code with other apps, only its last four characters.
So a card cannot keep its old code here. What the importer does instead:

- A card with a balance, still active and with a customer gets a **new code** holding the same
  balance and linked to that customer. Its staff note says which Shopify card it replaced
  ("ending ab12"). The new code is **not** sent automatically: open the card under
  **Products → Gift cards** and press **Resend** to email it to the customer when you are ready.
- A card with a balance but **no customer** stays in Shopify (no one to give a new code to). The
  report lists it by its last characters and balance; it keeps working on Shopify.
- Used-up, disabled and expired cards stay in Shopify.

Running the import again moves each card's balance to match Shopify, unless the card has already
been spent in this store; then its balance here is kept.

## Store settings

**Apps › Shopify importer › Store settings** brings your store's setup over, not just its
catalogue. Press **Check Shopify** to see, part by part, exactly what would change; tick the
parts you want and press **Import**. Nothing changes before that, and running it again is safe:
anything already here shows as matching.

| Part | What comes over | What you set up by hand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Store details | Store name, contact email and phone, business address; the default location gets the address if it has none | Currency, time zone and weight unit are compared and noted, never changed |
| Tax and GST | Whether prices include tax; your GST number if an app saved it on your Shopify store | A GST number already here is never replaced (shown as *Kept yours*). Shopify does not share its own GST setting with apps |
| Locations | Each location's name, address and PIN code, matched by name. Your first location takes the name of Shopify's first shipping location | New locations hold no stock and do not sell online until you turn that on. App-run locations (fulfilment services) are skipped |
| Shipping | Zones and rates from your general shipping profile: flat, by order value, by weight, free over an amount | Carrier-calculated rates, rates using value and weight at once, zones outside India, product-specific profiles and in-store pickup |
| Packages | Nothing: Shopify does not share saved packages with apps | Add your box sizes in **Settings › Shipping** |
| Policies | Refund, privacy, terms of service, shipping and contact policies, with scripts and styling removed | Pictures inside a policy, legal notice, terms of sale and subscription policies |
| Extra fields | Definitions on products, variants, collections, customers and orders whose type exists here | Types with no match (money, rating, JSON…); a field that already exists here as another type keeps yours |
| Markets | Markets for other countries, with currency, languages, subfolder and price adjustment. Shopify drafts come over switched off | India stays your primary market; a market whose countries another market here already has |
| Purchase orders | Purchase orders in rupees with their supplier (matched by name, or added), destination and products. Units still on order show as incoming | Orders in another currency (Shopify gives no exchange rate). Import products first: an order with a product not here yet waits |
| Transfers | Transfers between your locations, as drafts, in transit or received. Units in transit show as incoming at the destination | Cancelled transfers, and purchase order receipts (they are the purchase order here) |

## The Koomerce app on Shopify

**Install the Koomerce app** is the one-click way in. The app only reads your Shopify store; it
never changes anything there. Its access refreshes itself, so you do not need to reconnect.

- To disconnect, press **Disconnect** on the Connect page, or remove the app in Shopify
  (**Settings → Apps**). Removing it in Shopify disconnects it here too, within a minute.
- What you already imported stays: it is your store's data now.
- Shopify asks the app's maker, not you, to approve access to customer details and orders older
  than 60 days, so those work as soon as the app is installed.

## Shopify scopes to give the app

If you connect your own app instead, give it these read scopes in the Shopify Dev Dashboard:

`read_products`, `read_inventory`, `read_customers`, `read_orders`, `read_all_orders`,
`read_content`, `read_online_store_navigation`, `read_files`, `read_metaobject_definitions`,
`read_metaobjects`, `read_discounts`, `read_gift_cards`, `read_shipping`, `read_legal_policies`,
`read_purchase_options`, `read_markets`, `read_inventory_purchase_orders`,
`read_inventory_transfers`.

`read_purchase_options` brings pre-orders over: products Shopify sells through a pre-order
purchase option (a selling plan named like "Pre-order") arrive with pre-order switched on.
Without it the products still import, and the run says pre-orders were left out.

Pre-order is brought over per variant too. A variant set to **Continue selling when out of
stock** keeps selling as a pre-order; one that stops at zero arrives with **Allow pre-order**
off, so it shows crossed out once sold out, as on Shopify. A store using **Essential Pre-Order**
also brings its campaigns over (read from your storefront, no extra scope): the variants in a
campaign sell as pre-orders and the product takes the campaign's message as its **Pre-order
note**. Check the note afterwards: a fixed date in it ("end of November 2026") does not move
when you change the ship date, `{ship_month}` does.

`read_shipping`, `read_legal_policies`, `read_markets`, `read_inventory_purchase_orders` and
`read_inventory_transfers` are for **Store settings** (shipping rates, policies, markets,
purchase orders and transfers). Purchase orders and transfers never change the stock you have:
it came over with your products. An app connected before
they were added keeps working: add them in the Dev Dashboard, release a new version, then press
**Reconnect** on the part that asks for them.

If Shopify refuses one (for example gift cards on some plans), the import still brings over
everything else and the problems list says which scope to add.
