# What's new

Everything we've shipped, newest first. Big days get a plain-words summary below. The tables after
that list every change by area, one merged pull
request per row; select the number to see exactly what changed.

<Callout type="info">
  This page is built from the merged pull request titles in the repository (`git log
  --merges`), so it matches what's actually in the product. **Fix** marks a bug fix.
</Callout>

## 10 October 2026

A big day: about 80 changes. In plain words, grouped by what you do.

### Website builder

- **Click any text to edit it**, in every section, not only a few. Select words for **Bold**,
  **Italic**, **Underline** and **Link**.
- **One bar above each block** with move up and down, duplicate, remove, hide and copy.
- **Section settings are easier**: width and alignment as buttons, spacing for each screen with
  **−** and **+** and quick sizes, colour swatches, a background colour or picture, corners,
  border, shadow, show or hide on phone or computer, and an entrance animation you can preview.
  Almost every section has them now, including the hero, header and footer.
- **Pick a style when you add a section**, such as a hero as a slideshow, centred, split or with
  a video. **Pictures in the Add panel** show what each section looks like.
- **20 new sections**: heroes, logo cloud, stat cards, calls to action, feature cards, how it
  works, testimonials, FAQ, comparison table, plans, lookbook, email signup band, timeline, team
  and trust badges.
- **New side panels**: **Pages**, **Media** (click or drag a picture onto a block) and **Audit**,
  which finds pictures with no description, buttons with no link and hard-to-read colours. A path
  at the top of the settings shows where you are.
- **Zoom** from 25 % to 200 %. Phone, tablet and computer previews now use the real screen width.
- **Templates**: blogs and blog posts can use different layouts; each layout shows how many items
  use it and can be renamed, duplicated or deleted.
- **Use content from…**: fill a list of rows (questions, quotes, team) from your content types or
  a collection's products.
- **Version history**: every time a theme goes live, a version is kept. **Preview** or **Restore**
  one. The Themes page shows a picture of each theme and who saved it last.
- **Shopify theme import now brings product, collection and page templates**, not only the home
  page. If you imported a theme earlier, import it again to get them.

Always try changes on a duplicated theme first. See [Online store](/merchant-guide/online-store/website-builder).

### Stock and purchasing

- **A new Stock menu**: Inventory, Purchase orders, Inbound shipments, Suppliers and Transfers.
  See [Stock](/merchant-guide/stock/overview).
- **Suppliers**, with their currency and GSTIN.
- **Purchase orders in any currency**, with an exchange rate, and charges such as freight, customs
  (BCD, SWS, IGST) and clearing agent fees, split by weight or value.
- **Landed cost**: the real cost of each product, with a weighted average on every receipt, and
  **late bills** that update the cost of what is still on the shelf.
- **Receive with accept and reject**, **cancel and reopen**.
- **Upload a bill and let Vextro read it**, then check and apply.
- **Print, download or email a purchase order** to the supplier.
- **Bulk edit**: change prices, stock, tags and more for many products in a spreadsheet.
  See [Products and collections](/merchant-guide/products/products-collections).

### Several warehouses and GST

- **Send online orders from more than one warehouse**, in the order you choose.
- A big order can **split into parcels**, each billed by its own warehouse's GST number, with one
  **tax invoice per parcel** and **credit notes per GST number**.
- Shoppers see **Ships in 2 parcels** at checkout, on the thank-you page and in the email.
- Orders show **Ships from**, and **Needs stock moved** when a part must wait for a transfer.
  See [Several warehouses and GST](/merchant-guide/money-and-gst/several-warehouses-and-gst).

### Profit

- Every order has a **Profit** card: sales without GST, minus the cost of goods, courier,
  payment fees and returns.
- **Settings → Costs for profit**, and **Profit by month** and **Profit by product** reports.
  See [Profit](/merchant-guide/analytics/profit).

### Shipping

- **Shipments** has tabs (To book, Booked, In transit, Delivered, Returned), **Book selected**,
  a **Pickups** panel, a delivery-problems panel and your Shiprocket wallet at a glance.
  See [Shipments](/merchant-guide/orders/shipments).
- **Abandoned checkouts** has its own page for each cart, with **Send reminder** and **Copy cart link**.
  See [Orders](/merchant-guide/orders/orders).

### Growth and analytics

- **Links**: short links like `yourstore.in/l/diwali` that count clicks, orders and sales.
- **Growth overview** says what your marketing earned: campaigns, automations, discounts and links.
  See [Growth](/merchant-guide/growth/overview).
- **Live view** shows how many people are on your store right now, with a pause button.
- **Umami**: visitor analytics without cookies, with a **Visitors** section on Analytics.
  See [Analytics](/merchant-guide/analytics/overview) and
  [Visitor analytics (Umami)](/merchant-guide/analytics/visitor-analytics).

### Admin polish

- **A new look** across the admin: new sidebar and top bar, Home, Orders, Products, Customers,
  Content, Analytics, Growth, Apps, Settings and the login page. Light and dark.
- **Discounts**: **Pause**, **Resume** and **Duplicate**.
- **Files**: how much space you use, a **Documents** tab and a list of your largest files.
  See [Files and storage](/merchant-guide/content/files-and-storage).
- **Apps**: a **Recommended for your store** strip. See [Apps](/merchant-guide/apps/app-store).
- **Hindi** for more screens, including Umami and the purchase order form.
