# Live view

**Analytics → Live view** is for sale days. At the top, **N people on your store right now**.
Under it, visitors in the last 30 minutes, visitors per minute, carts and orders in the last
hour, revenue in the last hour, and a list of the **Latest activity**: each order and each cart
started.

A globe shows where today's orders are going. Drag to spin it, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use
**Globe settings** to change the look. Those settings are saved on that device only.

The page updates itself every 15 seconds. Select **Pause updates** to stop it while you read, and
**Resume updates** to start again. If your device is set to reduce motion, the globe stays still
until you drag it.
