# Profit

Sales tell you what came in. Profit tells you what you kept. Koomerce works it out for every order,
using what the goods actually cost you (your landed cost from purchase orders: price, freight,
customs and the rest), and then for every month, after your running costs.

One order's profit is an estimate: two identical orders can end up ₹10 apart once the courier
bills. What to trust is the **average over time**, so the reports show averages, and every figure
is a **floor**: you made at least this much, based on the costs you entered.

## What this is for

- Seeing if an order made money, on the order page.
- Finding which products earn the most per unit, and which barely pay for themselves.
- Seeing that bigger baskets earn more (one box and one delivery for the whole parcel).
- Knowing whether the month paid for your rent, staff and apps.

## How an order's profit is worked out

1. **Net sales (without GST)**: what the customer paid, less refunds, without the GST inside it.
   If the order was cancelled or came back undelivered (RTO), only the money you actually
   collected counts.
2. **Less the cost of goods**: what each item cost you *when it sold*. This is saved on the order
   when it is placed, so later cost changes do not change old orders. Items returned to stock do
   not count.
3. **Less packing**: the cost of the box each parcel goes in, once per parcel, from your boxes in
   **[Settings → Shipping](/merchant-guide/settings/shipping)**. The box is the one the products
   pick (else your main box). When the parcel is booked or packed, that box's cost is saved on it,
   so changing a box price later never changes past orders. Until then it shows as estimated.
4. **Less delivery**: what the courier charged for each parcel. Until that is known, a cautious
   estimate: your **highest** charge in the last 60 days for the same courier and state, else
   your **default delivery cost** from **[Settings → Costs for
   profit](/merchant-guide/settings/costs)** (₹100 until you change it). Three items in one
   parcel pay one delivery and one box.
5. **Less payment fees**: what the payment gateway kept, the actual fee when Razorpay or Cashfree
   reports it, else your rates from **Settings → Costs for profit**.
6. **Less RTO costs**: what the courier charged to bring an undelivered parcel back.

What is left is the **profit**, and the **margin** is profit as a share of net sales.

No packing or delivery is counted for an order cancelled before anything was packed or booked,
a parcel with only gift cards, or an order with no address to ship to. A booking you cancel keeps
its packing cost (the box was used) but not its delivery charge.

GST: if you are GST-registered, type box costs and the default delivery cost **before GST** (you
claim it back). If not, type what you actually pay.

## Net profit each month

Add your running costs in **[Settings → Monthly
expenses](/merchant-guide/settings/monthly-expenses)**: rent, salaries, apps, your website. Each
month's net profit is the month's order profit less those expenses.

- A month still going counts only the days gone ("so far"): on the 10th of a 30-day month, a third
  of each expense. Otherwise a fresh month would look like a loss.
- If the report covers only part of an older month, that part of each expense counts.

## Estimated, and "at least"

A figure is **estimated** while any packing, delivery or payment fee in it is still an estimate.
It becomes real when the parcel is booked, the courier's charge arrives or the gateway reports its
fee. Estimates are cautious, so real profit is usually a little higher.

Orders from before this was added get estimates too, and say so: the Profit report shows how many
orders in each month are still estimated (for example "12 of 140 orders still estimated").

Wherever profit is shown with an estimate in it, you see one line: *Based on the costs you
entered. Your real profit may be higher or lower if some costs differ.*

## Where to see it

- **Order page → Profit**: each line above. **Packing** and **Delivery** say *estimated* until
  known, the card shows an **Estimated** tag and the profit reads "at least". You can type the
  courier's charge under the card.
- **Analytics → Reports → Profit by month**: packing, delivery, fees, RTO, profit, monthly expenses
  and **net profit** per month, and how many orders are still estimated. Above the chart:
  net profit, average profit per order and per unit, profit per order by basket size (1, 2, 3+
  items), and a verdict: *Profitable in these dates*, or *₹X short: about N more orders at your
  average profit*.
- **Analytics → Reports → Profit by product**: each product's share of its orders' packing,
  delivery, fees and RTO, with **profit per unit**.

## Try it

1. Open **Settings → Shipping** and give each box its cost.
2. Open **Settings → Costs for profit**, check the fee rates and your default delivery cost.
3. Add your rent and salaries in **Settings → Monthly expenses**.
4. Open **Analytics → Reports → Profit by month** to see net profit and your averages.
