# Reports

**Analytics → Reports** is where you look things up: how much each state bought, which
products sold best this month, how many orders were cash on delivery.

## What this is for

- Asking "Sales by state last 30 days" and getting the answer, without building anything.
- Keeping the three or four reports you check every week one click away.
- Exporting a report as a CSV for your accountant.

## Ask a question

Type your question in the box at the top, or pick one of the examples under it (**Sales by state
last 30 days**, **Top 10 products this month**…). Koomerce AI answers in the AI bar. It only reads
your store's data here; it never changes anything. If AI is off for your store you'll see "Turn
on Koomerce AI in Settings to ask questions here", and asking opens **Settings → AI**.

## Saved reports

Open any report under **Templates**, pick the dates, then select **Save report**, give it a name
like "Sales by state · monthly" and **Save**. It shows up under **Saved reports** with the report
and dates it runs, and opens with those dates. Use the card's **⋯** menu to **Rename** or
**Delete** it; deleting only removes the saved copy, the report itself stays under Templates.

Saved reports belong to the store, so everyone on your team who can see Analytics sees them.

## Templates

The ready-made reports are grouped by topic: **Sales**, **Finance**, **Orders**, **Shipping and
delivery**, **Returns**, **Customers**, **Inventory** and **Marketing**. Each one shows a chart
and a table for the dates you pick, and **Export CSV** downloads the table.
