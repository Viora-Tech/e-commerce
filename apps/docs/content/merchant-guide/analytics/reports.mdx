# Reports

**Analytics → Reports** is where you look things up: how much each state bought, which
products sold best this month, how many orders were cash on delivery.

## What this is for

- Asking "Sales by state last 30 days" and getting the answer, without building anything.
- Keeping the three or four reports you check every week one click away.
- Exporting a report as a CSV for your accountant.

## Ask a question

Type your question in the box at the top, or pick one of the examples under it (**Sales by state
last 30 days**, **Top 10 products this month**…). Koomerce AI answers in the AI bar. It only reads
your store's data here; it never changes anything. If AI is off for your store you'll see "Turn
on Koomerce AI in Settings to ask questions here", and asking opens **Settings → AI**.

## Saved reports

Open any report under **Templates**, pick the dates, then select **Save report**, give it a name
like "Sales by state · monthly" and **Save**. It shows up under **Saved reports** with the report
and dates it runs, and opens with those dates. Use the card's **⋯** menu to **Rename** or
**Delete** it; deleting only removes the saved copy, the report itself stays under Templates.

Saved reports belong to the store, so everyone on your team who can see Analytics sees them.

## Templates

The ready-made reports are grouped by topic: **Sales**, **Finance**, **Orders**, **Shipping and
delivery**, **Returns**, **Customers**, **Inventory** and **Marketing**. Each one shows a chart
and a table for the dates you pick, and **Export CSV** downloads the table.

## Custom query

**Write a query** opens the query editor: a few lines in a small query language, much like
Shopify's ShopifyQL. It only reads your store's data. Koomerce AI writes these same queries when
you ask it about your numbers, and shows the query it used under its answer, so you can open it
here and change it.

```
FROM products
  SHOW net_quantity, gross_sales
  WHERE product_title CONTAINS 'triaina'
  GROUP BY variant_title
  DURING all_time
```

That one answers "how many TRIAINA mice have we sold, ever, by colour". The parts:

| Line | What it does |
|---|---|
| `FROM sales` | The table: `sales` (one row per order), `products` (one row per item sold) or `refunds` |
| `SHOW net_sales, orders` | The numbers you want |
| `WHERE vendor = 'Waizowl'` | Which rows count. Text goes in single quotes and capitals don't matter. Use `=`, `!=`, `IN ('a', 'b')`, `CONTAINS`, `STARTS WITH`, `ENDS WITH`, joined with `AND`, `OR`, `NOT` and brackets |
| `GROUP BY month` | Splits the rows, by up to three fields (`GROUP BY product_title, variant_title`) |
| `HAVING orders >= 2` | Keeps only groups whose totals pass (money in rupees) |
| `SINCE -3m UNTIL today` | The dates: `-30d`, `-4w`, `-3m`, `-2y`, `today`, `yesterday` or a date like `2026-10-01` |
| `DURING last_month` | A named range instead: `this_week`, `last_week`, `this_month`, `last_month`, `this_quarter`, `last_quarter`, `this_year`, `last_year`, `this_financial_year`, `last_financial_year`, `all_time` |
| `COMPARE TO previous_period` | Adds the numbers from just before (or `previous_year`) |
| `ORDER BY net_sales DESC` and `LIMIT 10` | Sort and how many rows |

`all_time` starts from your store's first order. A query can cover up to 20 years; a longer range
gets an error rather than a shortened answer. Cancelled orders, drafts and test orders never count.
Customer names show only to staff who can see customers.
