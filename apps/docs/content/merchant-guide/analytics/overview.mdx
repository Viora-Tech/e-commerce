# Analytics

**Analytics** answers "how is the store doing?" in four places: the **overview** page, **Reports**,
**Live view**, and the **Visitors** section when you connect Umami.

## What this is for

- Checking this week's sales against last week's.
- Finding which products, states and payment methods earn the most.
- Watching the store live during a sale.
- Seeing how many visitors turn into orders.

## The overview

**Where to find it:** **Analytics** in the sidebar. **What to do:** pick a date range at the top
(**Today**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Custom**). **What you'll see:**

- Five tiles: **Sessions**, **Revenue**, **Conversion rate**, **Average order value** and
  **Returning customers**. Each shows how it changed against the period before (**vs previous
  period**). Revenue includes tax and shipping; cancelled orders and drafts are left out.
- **Sales over time**, by day or by week, with the total and your best day. **Export** saves it
  as a CSV file.
- **Top products**, **Sales by state**, **Payment methods**, and **From visit to order**: how
  many sessions turned into carts, checkouts and orders.
- **Visitors**, once you connect Umami. See [Visitor analytics](/merchant-guide/analytics/visitor-analytics).

Sessions and the visit-to-order steps come from your own storefront, so they fill in as the store
gets traffic.

## Reports

**Analytics → Reports** has ready-made reports in groups: **Sales**, **Finance**, **Orders**,
**Shipping and delivery**, **Returns**, **Customers**, **Inventory** and **Marketing**. Type in
the box at the top to find a report by name. Asking questions in plain words is coming soon.

Open a report and you'll see:

- **What I understood**: the date range and, if you like, a **Compare** period. Change them
  there.
- A chart, with a **Bar** or **Line** switch.
- A **Details** table with totals. **Export CSV** saves it.

Stock reports show stock as it stands right now, so the date range does not apply to them. The
**Finance** group has **Profit by month** and **Profit by product**; see [Profit](/merchant-guide/analytics/profit).

## Live view

The full-page globe is covered in [Live view](/merchant-guide/analytics/live-view).

## Visitors

With the Umami app connected, the overview ends with a **Visitors** section: visitors, visits,
page views, top pages, where people came from, campaigns, countries and devices. It counts a
visitor only after they allow analytics on your cookie banner, unless you turn that off. Set it
up in [Visitor analytics (Umami)](/merchant-guide/analytics/visitor-analytics).

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | Why it happens | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sessions are zero | The store is new, or has no visitors yet. | Give it a few days of traffic. |
| Numbers differ from your payment dashboard | Analytics leaves out drafts and cancelled orders, and counts tax and shipping. | Compare the same date range and the same kind of orders. |
| **Visitors** is missing | The Umami app is not installed or not connected. | Follow [Visitor analytics (Umami)](/merchant-guide/analytics/visitor-analytics). |
| The Live view globe is empty | No order has a shipping address in the last 24 hours. | Orders appear as soon as one does. |
