# Home dashboard

Home is the first page you see after you log in.

After you log in, you land on **Home**. At the top is a one-line summary of your day, for
example:

> Good evening, Asha. 14 orders today worth ₹23,400, 3 need shipping.

Below it, from top to bottom:

- **Ask anything about your store**: type a question for the [AI assistant](/merchant-guide/home/ask-ai)
  (shown only when the assistant is switched on).
- **Finish setting up**: three steps, **Add your products**, **Connect payments** and **Set up
  shipping**, each ticked when done. It goes away once all three are done.
- **To do**: the things only you can do next.
- **Sales today**: a chart you can switch between **Today**, **7 days** and **30 days**.
- **Latest orders**, and **Live now** beside it: visitors on your store, and today's orders and sales.
- **Suggested for you**: ideas based on your store, such as restocking a best seller or
  reminding people who left a cart. **Hide this suggestion** removes one on that browser.
- **More numbers** (closed at first): sales over time, products and payments.

The **To do** cards look like this:

| You'll see | Button | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "3 orders are ready to ship" | **Ship orders** | Book labels in Shipments → To book. |
| "2 UPI payments need checking" | **Check payments** | Match transfers in your bank app, then mark them paid. |
| "5 GST invoices are queued" | **Issue invoices** | Review and issue the queued GST invoices. |
| "4 products are running low" | **Restock** | Order more in **Stock → Purchase orders**, or update stock in **Stock → Inventory**. |
| "2 pre-orders are waiting on their balance" | **View pre-orders** | Request or chase the balance. |

When there's nothing to do it says "You're all caught up".

**Analytics → Live view** puts the same orders on a full-page globe. Drag to spin or tilt it;
scroll, pinch or use the + and − buttons to zoom (up to 4×). The closer you zoom, the more and
finer the dots, so India's coastline reads clearly up close. If your device has reduced motion
turned on, the globe stays still until you drag it. More in [Analytics](/merchant-guide/analytics/overview).
