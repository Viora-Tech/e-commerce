# AI assistant

Every page of your admin has an **AI bar** at the bottom (on a phone, the round button in the
corner). Press **Ctrl+J** (**⌘J** on a Mac) to open it from anywhere, and again to close it.

You can **ask** it about your store, and you can **tell it what to do**. It never changes
anything on its own: it prepares the change and shows you exactly what will happen, and you click
**Approve**.

## Ask

Type a question, or tap a suggestion:

- "How were sales this week?"
- "Which orders are late?"
- "What's running out?"
- "Top products this month?"

Every number comes from your own store. Tap **Data used** under an answer to see the numbers it
used. You can ask a follow-up, like "and last week?". **New chat** starts again. The conversation
stays while the tab is open.

It knows which page you are on. On a product it offers "Write this product in Hindi", on an order
"Draft a reply to this customer", and so on.

## Get it done

Ask for a change in plain words. Some examples:

- "Create discount DIWALI10: 10% off collection Mice, 20 Oct to 5 Nov"
- "Raise prices 5% in collection Mice"
- "Add 8 glasspads to a new collection Glasspads"
- "Write this product in Hindi" (on a product page)
- "Draft a reply to order #2250"

The assistant shows a **card** with the change: what it is, and every value before and after
(for a price change, every old and new price). Then:

- **Approve**: the change is made. The card shows what was done, with a link to open it.
- **Edit**: change a value first (the code, the percent, the dates, the text), then check the
  preview again.
- **Cancel**: nothing happens.

After a change you can usually **Undo** it, on the card or later in **Settings → AI → Activity**:

| Change | Undo does |
|---|---|
| New discount | switches the discount off |
| Price change | puts the old prices back (prices changed by hand since are left alone) |
| New collection | hides the collection (sets it to draft) |
| Products added to a collection | takes them back out |
| Product text (title, description, search text, tags) | puts the old text back |
| Note or reply draft on an order | cannot be undone (it stays on the timeline) |

### What it will not do

It never deletes anything, never refunds or takes payments, and never sends messages to your
customers. A reply draft is saved as a note on the order for you to copy and send yourself. One
change touches at most 200 products, and a price change goes from -50% to +100% (never above a
product's MRP). A card you do not approve within an hour expires.

### If a card shows a warning

If the assistant read text in your store (a product description, a customer's note) that looked
like an instruction to the AI, it ignores that text and the card says so. Check that change
carefully before you approve it.

## Write many products at once

On **Products**, tick the products you want, then click **Write with AI** in the bar above the
list. Choose what to write (description, search title and description, tags, photo alt text),
the language and the tone, and click **Start writing**.

It writes one product at a time in the background. You can close the panel: a banner on the
products list shows the progress and **View** opens it again. When it is done, untick anything
you do not want and click **Apply**. Each product counts as one AI request. You can undo an
applied run in **Settings → AI → Activity**.

## Who can use it

Anyone in your team can open the assistant, but it only does what their role allows: a staff
member who cannot edit discounts never sees a discount card. Approving a change checks their role
again. Only the person who asked can approve their own card. (Changes an AI app suggests over
MCP are different: the owner or anyone whose role can make the change may approve them, see
Settings → MCP & API.)

## Your data

- Your questions and the answers are not saved on our servers, only counted.
- Customer phone numbers, emails and addresses are never sent to the AI. To draft a reply it sees
  only the customer's first name, and it tells you so under the answer.
- See **Settings → AI** for what was used this month, to turn features off, and for Activity.

AI can make mistakes. Read the card before you approve.
