# Glossary

The words you'll see in your admin, courier dashboards and GST paperwork, in plain words, with
where each one shows up in Waimers.

## Shipping

### AWB

**Air waybill**: the courier's tracking number for one parcel, printed on the shipping label.
Every booked shipment gets one. In Waimers it's the **Waybill** column in **Orders →
Shipments**, and **AWB** on the order's shipping panel.

### NDR

**Non-delivery report**: the courier tried to deliver and couldn't (shopper not home, phone off,
address incomplete). You decide what happens next under **Delivery problems**: **Try delivery
again**, **Use a new address** or **Send it back (RTO)**. See
[Shipments](/merchant-guide/selling/shipments#delivery-problems-ndr).

### RTO

**Return to origin**: the parcel is sent back to your pickup address instead of the shopper.
Shows as **Returning to you** on the shipment. If every parcel on an order comes back, the
order's Fulfilment becomes **Returned**. Couriers usually charge for RTO, so fix NDRs quickly.

### Pickup and manifest

A **pickup** is a request for the courier to collect parcels from you on a date and time. A
**manifest** is the one-page list of parcels in that pickup, signed by the delivery person.

## Tax

### GSTIN

**GST identification number**: your 15-character GST registration, for example
`27AAPFU0939F1ZV`. The first two digits are your state code (27 is Maharashtra). Enter it in
**Settings → General** and in the GST invoices app's **Business details**. A B2B buyer's GSTIN
goes on their invoice as **Buyer GSTIN**.

### HSN

**Harmonised System of Nomenclature**: the code that says what kind of goods something is, which
decides its GST rate. Cotton T-shirts are `6109`, for example. In Waimers it's the product's
**HS (harmonised system) code** on the **Shipping** card, and it prints on every invoice line.

### CGST, SGST and IGST

The parts GST is split into. Selling within your own state: half **CGST** (central) and half
**SGST** (state). Selling to another state: all **IGST** (integrated). Waimers picks the split
from your state and the shipping state.

### GSTR-1

The monthly (or quarterly) GST return listing your sales. Export it from **Apps → GST invoices
→ Returns → Export GSTR-1** and give it to your CA.

### Credit note

A GST document that reduces an earlier invoice, issued when you refund. Waimers issues one
automatically for refunds on invoiced orders.

## Payments

### COD

**Cash on delivery**: the shopper pays the delivery person. COD orders are never auto-cancelled
for being unpaid. You can add a handling fee in **Settings → Payments**.

### UPI

**Unified Payments Interface**: India's instant bank-to-bank payments (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm,
BHIM). Waimers takes UPI three ways: through Razorpay checkout, as a single-use QR that confirms
itself (**UPI — confirmed automatically**), or straight to your UPI ID, which you confirm by hand
(**UPI to your own ID — you confirm it**).

### Webhook

A message a service (like Razorpay) sends to your store the moment something happens, such as
"payment captured". It's how orders mark themselves paid without you checking.

### Pre-order

Selling a product before it's in stock, with an expected ship date. Set it up under
**Pre-order & launch** on the product.

### Balance due

On a pre-order paid with a deposit, the part still to be paid. Say ₹500 deposit on ₹3,000:
₹2,500 is the balance due. You can't ship the order until it's collected.

### Refund needed

A payment arrived that the order can't keep (it was cancelled first, or paid twice). The order
shows **Refund needed** until you refund it.

## Products

### SKU

**Stock keeping unit**: your own code for one variant, like `KUR-M-IND` (kurta, medium, indigo).
Set it in **Inventory → SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)**.

### Variant

One buyable version of a product, made from its options. A kurta with Size (S, M, L) and Colour
(Indigo, Rust) has 6 variants, each with its own price, SKU and stock.

### Collection

A group of products shown as one category page: **Manual** (you pick the products) or
**Automatic** (products matching conditions, such as "Price is less than 999").

### MRP and compare-at price

The **Compare at price** on a product is shown struck through next to the real price, so
shoppers see the saving. Most Indian stores use it for the MRP.

## Online store

### Theme

The design of your whole store: colours, fonts, and the sections on each page. One theme is
**Live**; others wait in the theme library as **Unpublished**.

### Section

One building block of a page, like a banner, a product grid or a testimonials strip. You add,
move and style sections in the editor.

### App block

A section that comes from an installed app, found in the editor's **Add** panel on the **Apps**
tab: **Notify me** (Back in stock), **Delivery checker** (Delhivery, Shiprocket) and
**Pre-order countdown** (built in).

### Template

The layout for one kind of page (home, product, collection, cart...). A product can use an
alternate template for a different look.
