# Visitor analytics (Umami)

The **Umami** app adds a **Visitors** section to your **Analytics** page: how many people
opened your store, which pages they looked at, where they came from (Google, Instagram, a
WhatsApp link…), which ad or post campaigns brought them, and their phones, browsers and
countries.

[Umami](https://umami.is) is a free, open-source analytics tool. It sets **no cookies** and
keeps no personal data about your shoppers.

## What you need

- An Umami account. Use **Umami Cloud** (sign up at umami.is) or your own Umami server.
- An **API key** from Umami, so this page can read your numbers. In Umami: **Settings → API
  keys → Create key**.

## Connect it

1. Go to **Apps**, find **Umami** under **Connections** and click **Install**.
2. Open **Apps → Umami** in the sidebar.
3. Fill in **Umami address**: your Umami server's address, or `cloud.umami.is` for Umami Cloud.
4. Paste your **API key**. It is stored locked (encrypted) and never shown again.
5. Click **Create a website for this store**, or paste the **Website id** of a website you
   already added in Umami.
6. Click **Test connection**. You should see "Connected to … in Umami".
7. Click **Save**.

Your store starts counting visitors right away. Open **Analytics** and scroll to **Visitors**.

## Cookie consent

**Wait for cookie consent** is on by default: a visitor is counted only after they allow
analytics on your cookie banner. Umami sets no cookies, so you can turn this off to count
every visitor until they say no. A "no" on the banner always stops counting.

## Campaigns

To see which ad or post brought a visitor, add a campaign name to the link you share, for
example `https://yourstore.in/?utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=diwali-sale`. The
**Campaigns (UTM)** card on Analytics lists each campaign.

## If something is wrong

- **"Umami did not accept the API key"**: create a new key in Umami and paste it again.
- **"Umami has no website with that id"**: check the website id, or click **Create a website
  for this store**.
- **"Could not reach Umami"**: check the address, and that your Umami server is running. The
  address must start with `https://`.
- The rest of your Analytics page keeps working even when Umami cannot be reached.
