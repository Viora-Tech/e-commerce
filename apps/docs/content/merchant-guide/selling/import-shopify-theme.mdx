# Import a Shopify theme

Moving from Shopify? Bring your theme with you. Upload the theme file and your home page,
product, collection and other pages, header and footer look the way they do on Shopify. What shoppers do on them (adding to cart,
paying, searching, signing in) runs on this store's own features.

<Callout type="warning">
Only upload a theme you own or have the rights to. Themes bought from Shopify's Theme Store are
usually licensed for Shopify only. Themes you had built for you, or built yourself, are fine.
</Callout>

## Get the theme file from Shopify

1. In Shopify, open **Online Store → Themes**.
2. Next to your theme, press the three dots (**⋯**) and choose **Download theme file**.
3. Shopify emails you a link to a `.zip` file. Download it. Do not unzip it.

## Import it here

1. Open **Online store → Themes** and press **Import Shopify theme**. A new page opens.
2. Choose the `.zip` file (up to 50 MB).
3. Type your Shopify store's address, for example `waimers.in` or `yourstore.myshopify.com`.
   We copy your theme's pictures and hero videos from it into **Files**, so your new store does
   not depend on the old one. You can leave it empty and upload pictures yourself later.
4. Tick **I own this theme, or I have the rights to use it here**, then press **Import theme**.

The import takes up to a minute. It creates a **new** theme called "*your theme* (imported)".
Your live theme does not change until you publish the new one.

## Read the import report

After the import you land on the **Import report**. You can open it again any time from the theme
library (the **Import report** link under the theme's name).

| Result | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Imported exactly** | Looks and works the same as on Shopify. |
| **Runs on our features** | Same look; what it does runs here (for example, Add to cart puts the item in this store's cart). |
| **Not available yet** | The look is kept, but one part has no match here yet. The report says which part and what to use instead. |
| **Could not import** | The section's code uses something we cannot read yet. It does not show on the page. |

**Before you publish** lists what to do first:

- **Menus** your header and footer use that are not in this store yet. Add them in
  **Content → Menus**, with the same handle, or import them with the Shopify importer.
- **Collections and products** the home page shows that are not in this store yet. Import your
  catalogue first; the theme finds them by the same handles Shopify used.
- **Pictures or videos** that could not be copied. Upload them in **Files** and pick them in
  Customize.

## What changes and what stays

| On Shopify | Here |
| --- | --- |
| Add to cart buttons | This store's cart. The shopper lands on the cart page. |
| Cart drawer | The cart page. The drawer keeps its look. |
| Shopify checkout | This store's checkout, with your payment methods, COD rules and GST invoices. |
| Instant search suggestions | The search page. The search box keeps its look. |
| Customer accounts | This store's account pages, with sign in by one-time code. |
| Newsletter signup | Your subscriber list, with a confirm email. |
| Contact and advice forms | Install the **Forms** app (Apps). Every message is saved under Apps, Forms and emailed to your store contact email. Until you install it, the form shows "This form is not available right now". |
| Related products ("You may also like") | Works: products from the same collection first, then the same product type and tags. Sold-out and unpublished products are left out. |
| Shopify apps (Judge.me, Klaviyo…) | This store's apps where one exists (Reviews, Back in stock, EMI). With the [Reviews](/merchant-guide/selling/reviews) app installed, Judge.me's stars and review sections (Reviews showcase, Review stack, the comparison table's rating) show your reviews; without it they stay hidden. |
| Pre-order apps | The built-in pre-order: products set to pre-order show as pre-order in the theme. |
| Metafields and metaobjects | Read from your extra fields and content types, which the Shopify importer copies over. Extra fields on variants, pages, blog posts and the shop itself are not kept yet, so a theme reading those shows them blank. |
| Shopify fonts | The same font families, from Google Fonts. |
| Pictures and videos in Shopify Files | Copied into your Files during the import. |
| Country and currency picker | India and rupees only for now. |
| Prices | Shown in this store's money format (for example ₹7,499). |

Your **home page**, **product pages**, **collection pages**, **pages** (About, FAQ, Contact and
your other page templates), **header** and **footer** use your theme's own sections. On product
and collection pages, the part shoppers buy from (photos, price, options, add to cart, the
product grid and filters) is this store's own, among your theme's other sections such as specs,
size guides and related products. Cart, search, blog and account pages use this store's pages,
inside your theme's header and footer.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Imported your theme before 10 October 2026?** Back then only the home page, header and footer
  were converted, so product, collection and page templates fall back to this store's own. They
  are not added to an existing imported theme. To get them, run the import again: it makes a
  fresh copy and leaves the old one alone. Pages built from a theme template with a name, such
  as `page.about`, show in the report as **Pages: about** and arrive as the alternate **about**.
</Callout>

## Edit and publish

1. To experiment safely, open the imported theme's **⋯** menu and choose **Duplicate**, then work
   on the copy. In the theme library, press **Customize** on the theme you want to edit.
2. The left panel lists your theme's sections by their own names. Click one to change its
   settings on the right; they come from your theme, so they read the same as in Shopify's
   editor. Blocks (slides, logos, cards) are listed under **Blocks**.
3. You can reorder, hide, duplicate and remove sections. Adding brand-new sections from your
   Shopify theme comes later.
4. Press **Preview** to see it on your storefront, then **Publish** when you are happy.

Colours, fonts and layout come from your Shopify theme's own settings. The theme presets in
**Theme settings** do not apply to an imported theme.
