# Reviews

The **Reviews** app shows what customers say about your products, right on the product page,
with stars, photos and your replies. It replaces Judge.me when you move from Shopify.

## What this is for

- Shoppers trust a product with real reviews from real buyers. Each review from someone who
  bought the product gets a **Verified buyer** badge.
- You stay in control: you choose which reviews go live straight away and which wait for you.
- Customers are asked for a review by email a few days after their order arrives, so reviews
  keep coming without you chasing anyone.
- Your old Judge.me reviews come over with their stars, dates, photos and your replies.

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Apps**, find **Reviews** under **Built-in apps** and click **Install**.
2. **Reviews** now shows in the sidebar under **Apps**.
3. Your product pages show the reviews section (with **Write a review**) wherever your theme
   has the **Product reviews** section.

## The Reviews page

**Apps → Reviews** lists every review, newest first.

- The four boxes at the top: your **average rating**, how many reviews are **published**, how
  many are **waiting for you**, and how many have **photos**.
- The tabs: **All**, **Waiting**, **Published**, **Hidden**.
- Search by product, customer or words in the review. **Filter** by stars, product, or reviews
  with photos.
- Tick several reviews to **Approve**, **Hide** or **Delete** them together.

Click a review to open it. There you can:

- **Approve and publish** it, or **Hide** it.
- **Reply**. Your reply shows under the review on your store, and the customer gets an email
  the first time you reply. If AI is on for your store (Settings → AI), **Write with AI**
  suggests a reply you can change before posting.
- Switch **Show on product page** on or off.
- Switch **Feature on homepage** on: featured reviews come first in your theme's reviews
  showcase.
- **Delete review** removes it for good.

The **Activity** box shows what happened: the request email, when it was written, whether it
was held for you and why, and when it was published or answered.

## Settings

**Apps → Reviews → Settings.**

| Setting | What it means | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Publishing** | **Publish right away**, **Publish 4★ and 5★ right away, hold the rest**, or **Hold every review for approval**. | Publish 4★ and 5★, hold the rest |
| **Always hold reviews with photos** | Photos wait for you, so you can check them first. | On |
| **Who can write a review** | **Only customers who bought the product** (from the link in their review email, or signed in to their account) or **Anyone**. | Only buyers |
| **Most reviews one customer can send in a day** | A limit per email address. Bots are always blocked with a hidden trap field and a limit per visitor. | 3 |
| **Send review request emails** | Ask customers for a review after delivery. | On |
| **Send after delivery** | How many days after the order is delivered. | 7 days |
| **Reminder if no review** | One reminder this many days later. 0 sends none. | 5 days |
| **Email subject** | `{{ product.title }}` becomes the product's name. **Preview email** shows it. | `How was your {{ product.title }}?` |
| **Show star ratings on product cards** | Stars on product cards in your theme's collections and search. | On |
| **Show customer photos** | Photos on review cards. | On |
| **Sort reviews by** | Most helpful, newest, highest or lowest rating first. | Most helpful |

### Asking for reviews

The emails go out by themselves once an order is delivered (by the courier's tracking, or when
you mark it delivered). Every email has a **Stop review emails** link; customers who used it,
or who unsubscribed from your marketing emails, are never asked again.

Just switched from Shopify? Click **Ask for reviews** on the Reviews page: everyone whose
order was delivered in the last 60 days and was never asked gets one email within minutes.

## Bring your reviews over from Judge.me

1. In your Shopify admin, open **Judge.me → Settings → Data export → Export reviews**, choose
   **Product reviews** and download the CSV file Judge.me emails you.
2. In **Apps → Reviews**, click **Import from Judge.me** and choose that file.
3. Check the numbers: **Ready to import**, **No matching product** and **Photos to copy**. Each
   review is matched to your product by its SKU, then by its product handle (the Shopify
   importer keeps handles, so imported products match).
4. Choose whether to copy photos and publish the reviews straight away, then click
   **Import**.

Photos are copied into your **Files** in the background, with location data removed. It is
safe to import again later: reviews already imported are skipped.

## Imported Shopify themes

If your store uses an [imported Shopify theme](/merchant-guide/selling/import-shopify-theme),
its review parts work with this app once it is installed: the stars on product cards, the
**Reviews showcase** and **Review stack** sections, and the comparison table's rating row all
show your reviews.

## Good to know

- A review needs a product here. Reviews for products you have not brought over are left out
  of a Judge.me import; bring the product over, then import again.
- Average ratings and counts update straight away when you approve, hide, delete or import.
- When a customer asks for their data to be deleted, their reviews stay as text from "A
  customer", without their name or email.
