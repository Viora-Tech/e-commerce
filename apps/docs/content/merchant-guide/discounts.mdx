# Discounts

Go to **Discounts** in the sidebar. The list shows every discount with its value, how many
times it was used and the dates it runs. Select **Create discount** to make a new one.

## Discount type

Pick the type first, at the top of the page. It is fixed once the discount is saved.

| Type | What the shopper gets |
| --- | --- |
| **Amount off products** | A percentage or rupee amount off the products or collections you pick. |
| **Amount off order** | A percentage or rupee amount off the whole order. |
| **Buy X get Y** | Buy some items (or spend an amount) and get other items free or cheaper. |
| **Free shipping** | Shipping is free on qualifying orders, in every state or the ones you pick. |

A fixed amount is taken once from the order and never more than the items it covers are worth.

## Method: code or automatic

- **Discount code.** Shoppers type a code, such as `DIWALI10`, in the cart or at checkout.
  Case does not matter: `diwali10` works too.
- **Automatic.** Applied to every cart that qualifies, no code needed. When several automatic
  offers fit a cart, the one that saves the shopper the most wins (unless they combine, below).

## Amount off products: what it applies to

Choose **Specific collections** or **Specific products**, then select **Browse** (or the
search field beside it). A picker opens with **Products** and **Collections** tabs, a search
box, pictures, price and stock (or the number of products in a collection). Tick what you
want, or **Select all shown**, and select **Done**. The picks are listed on the card; select
the cross on a row to take one off. A collection covers every product in it, including
products added to it later.

### A different amount for each product

One code can take a different amount off different products, for example ₹200 off a keyboard
and ₹300 off a mouse.

1. Choose **Specific products** and pick the products.
2. Turn on **Different amount for each product**.
3. Type an amount next to each product: a percentage (up to 100%) with **Percentage off**,
   rupees with **Fixed amount off**.
4. Select **Save**.

The amount comes off **every piece** of that product: two keyboards with ₹200 off each means
₹400 off. A fixed amount never takes more than the product's price. The **Value** column on
the discount list shows the range, for example "₹200 to ₹300 off each item".

## Buy X get Y

- **Customer buys**: a minimum quantity of items, or a minimum purchase amount, from the
  products or collections you browse to.
- **Customer gets**: how many items, from which products or collections, and at what value:
  **Free**, a **Percentage** off, or an **Amount off each**.
- **Set a maximum number of uses per order** caps how many times one order earns it.

Shoppers add the "gets" items to their cart themselves; the cheapest qualifying items are the
ones discounted, and an item never counts as both bought and given. Example: "Buy 2, get 1
free" on three ₹1,000 shirts makes one shirt free. With a minimum amount, every multiple of
the amount spent earns another round.

## Free shipping

- **All states** or **Selected states**: tick the states that get free shipping. The state is
  checked once the shopper enters their address at checkout.
- **Exclude shipping rates over a certain amount**: rates above this price (an express
  courier, for example) are charged in full.

## Minimum purchase requirements

- **No minimum requirements**.
- **Minimum purchase amount**: the cart must be worth at least this much before discounts.
- **Minimum quantity of items**: the cart must hold at least this many items (for an amount
  off products, only the items the discount covers count).

## Customer eligibility

- **All customers**.
- **Specific customer segments**: browse to the segments you made under Customers → Segments.
  A shopper qualifies while they match the segment's rules.
- **Specific customers**: browse to the customers by name or email.

Eligibility is checked when a signed-in shopper views the cart, or at checkout once the
shopper enters their email. A shopper who does not qualify is told the code is not available
for their account.

## Maximum discount uses

Cap the total number of uses, or allow one use per customer (counted by email address).

## Combinations

By default a discount does not stack with others. Under **Combinations**, tick **Product
discounts**, **Order discounts** or **Shipping discounts** to let this one apply together with
discounts of those kinds. Both discounts must allow each other. At checkout the shopper's code
applies first, then the automatic discounts it combines with, at most one of each kind. An
order discount is worked out on what is left after the product discount. Every combined
discount counts a use, and the order shows each one's name.

## Active dates

When the discount starts, and optionally when it ends. The order keeps the discount's name,
code and the rupee amount it took off, so later changes never change an order already placed.

## Pause, resume and duplicate

Open a discount and use the buttons at the top (you need permission to change discounts).

- **Pause** stops shoppers using it, with nothing deleted. Confirm with **Pause discount**.
- **Resume** turns it on again.
- **Duplicate** makes a copy called "*name* (copy)". Its code gets `-COPY` added (then `-COPY2`
  and so on), its use count starts at zero, and it starts **paused**.
- **Delete** removes it. A discount already used on an order is disabled instead, so history
  stays intact.

## Turning a discount off

Set **Status** to **Disabled** to stop it working while keeping it for your records.
