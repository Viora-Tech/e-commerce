# Blog posts

## What this is for

A blog brings shoppers who search for topics, not products: "best mouse for FPS games", "how to
clean a mechanical keyboard". Posts publish to your storefront at `/blogs/<blog>/<post>`, show in
your theme's blog sections, and are listed in your sitemap so search engines find them.

## How it works

- A **blog** is a group of posts with its own address, for example **News** (`/blogs/news`) or
  **Guides**. Most stores need one or two.
- A **post** belongs to one blog, and is **Published**, **Scheduled** (goes live by itself at the
  time you set) or hidden (only staff see it).
- Posts imported from Shopify come in with their blog and web address, so old links keep working.

## Write a post

<Steps>

### Start

Go to **Content → Blog posts** and select **Write post**.

### Write

Give it a title, then write the **Content** with the editor (headings, lists, links, images).
The **Excerpt** is the short summary your theme shows on the blog page.

### Add a picture

Under the featured image, select **Add image**, pick one from Content → Files (upload it there
first) and write its **Alt text**: what the picture shows, for screen readers and search engines.

### Organise

Under **Organization** pick the blog (or **Create blog**), the **Author**, and tags.

### Search engine listing

Set the **Page title**, **Meta description** and **URL handle** Google shows. Left empty, they
are filled from the post.

### Publish or schedule

Under **Visibility**, publish now, or choose **Scheduled** and a **Publish date** (times are in
your browser's time zone). Select **Save**.

</Steps>

<Callout>
  A post that answers one question well does better than a long post about everything. Link from
  the post to the products it mentions.
</Callout>

## Manage blogs

**Content → Blog posts → Manage blogs** lists your blogs. **Create blog** sets its title, **URL
handle** (built from the title if left empty), **Comments** (**Off**, **On, after you approve**,
or **On, published right away**) and the **Theme template** it uses. Only a blog with no posts can
be deleted.

## The list

The list shows each post's blog, status, author and date. Filter by status, blog, or whether it
**Has a featured image**. From a row you can **Open post**, **View on store**, **Unpublish** or
delete. Deleted posts are removed from your storefront and cannot be recovered.
