# Customer list, segments and bulk edit

## What this is for

**Customers** is everyone who bought from you, made an account or signed up for your messages.
You use it to find one person quickly, and to pick groups: "people who bought twice in 60 days",
"big spenders who stopped ordering", "customers in Maharashtra". A saved group is a **segment**.
You can email a segment, give it a discount, or use it in campaigns and automations.

## The customer list

- The tabs at the top are quick groups: **All customers**, **New**, **Returning** and
  **Subscribed** (agreed to hear from you).
- The cards show how many customers you have, how many are repeat customers, and how many can get
  your emails.
- Search and filters narrow the list. **Save as segment** keeps the filter you built.
- Open a customer to see their orders, addresses, store credit, notes and tags. Editing one
  customer is in [Customers](/merchant-guide/customers/customers).

## Segments

A segment is a saved filter. It is never a fixed list: open it and you see who is in it **right
now**. A customer who places a second order moves into "Repeat buyers" by themselves.

### Start from a template

**Customers → Segments** shows ready-made segments most stores use. Select **Use template**, change
the numbers to suit your store, and save:

| Template | Who is in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Top spenders** | Spent ₹10,000 or more with you |
| **Repeat buyers** | Ordered at least twice |
| **One-time buyers** | Ordered once; a nudge can make them repeat buyers |
| **Bought in the last 30 days** | Fresh, happy customers |
| **No order in 90 days** | Customers drifting away; win them back with an offer |
| **Big spenders slipping away** | Spent ₹5,000 or more but no order in 60 days |
| **Subscribed to marketing** | Agreed to hear from you, so you can message them |
| **Business customers** | Saved a GSTIN; send them bulk offers or GST-ready invoices |

### Make your own

<Steps>

### Create

Select **Create segment** and give it a **Segment name**, for example "Repeat buyers in
Maharashtra".

### Add conditions

Select **Add condition** and pick what to check: **Total spent**, **Order count**, **Average
order value**, **Days since last order**, **Days since first order**, **Days as a customer**,
**State**, **City**, **Tag**, **Marketing consent**, **Customer type**, **Bought a product**,
**Cash on delivery orders**, **Returned to origin (RTO)** or **Abandoned a cart (30 days)**.
Then a comparison (**is at least**, **is at most**, **is between**, **is**, **includes**, …)
and a value.

### Match all or any

**Customers must match all conditions** (every rule) or **any condition** (at least one).
**Matching customers** shows the count live as you change things. With no conditions, every
customer matches.

### Save and use it

Select **Save segment**. From the segment you can **View customers**, **Email this segment**
(opens a campaign) or **Create discount for it** (a discount only those customers can use).

</Steps>

<Callout>
  Messaging a segment only reaches customers who agreed to marketing on that channel. Add
  "Marketing consent" as a condition to see how many you can actually reach.
</Callout>

Deleting a segment removes the saved filter only; customers are not affected.

## Edit many customers at once

Tick customers on the list; the bar that appears also has **Add tag**, **Remove tag**, **Email**
and **More actions** (export, delete). Choose **Bulk edit**, and **Edit
customers** opens a spreadsheet:

- Click a cell and type. Paste from a spreadsheet, drag the corner to fill down, or press
  **Ctrl+D** to copy the cell above.
- **Columns** picks what to show: name, phone, business name, GSTIN, city, state, PIN code (these
  three edit the default address), tags, note, and **Email marketing**, **WhatsApp marketing**,
  **SMS marketing**.
- **Change for all** applies one change to every row: **Add tags**, **Remove tags**, **Replace
  all tags with**, add to or replace the note, **Subscribe everyone** or **Unsubscribe everyone**.
- Save when done; **Discard** throws away unsaved changes.

Only subscribe someone to marketing if they agreed to it. Every change is kept in their consent
history ([Privacy and data requests](/merchant-guide/settings/customer-privacy)).

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| A segment shows 0 customers | Loosen one condition, or switch to **any condition**; check the numbers (₹ and days) |
| "A GSTIN has 15 characters" in bulk edit | Fix the GSTIN in that cell, like 06AAACW1234A1Z5 |
| A campaign to a segment reached fewer people | Only customers who agreed to that channel get marketing messages |
