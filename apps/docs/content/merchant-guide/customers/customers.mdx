# Customers

Every shopper who places an order gets a customer profile under **Customers**. Open one to see
their orders, a note for your team, the messages they were sent, and their contact details.

## Change a customer's name, email or phone

Select **Edit** on the **Contact** card (or in the ⋯ menu at the top of the customer's page), change the **Name**, **Email** or
**Phone**, then select **Save**. The shopper signs in to your store with this email; their past
orders keep the email they were placed with.

If another customer already has that email, the change is refused with a link to them: it is the
same person with two records, so merge them instead (below).

<Callout type="info">
  To change the details on one order only (for example, a shopper asks you to send this parcel to
  their office), use **Edit** on the order's **Customer details** card instead. See
  [Orders](/merchant-guide/orders/orders#change-the-customers-details-on-an-order).
</Callout>

## Saved addresses

The **Addresses** card lists the customer's saved addresses, with their default one marked
**Default**.

- **Add address**: type the name, mobile number and address. Type the **PIN code** and the city
  and state fill in on their own; change them if they are wrong. Tick **Make this the default
  address** to use it for their next order.
- **Edit**: change any line of an address and select **Save address**.
- **Delete** (the bin icon): removes it from their address book. Orders already shipped keep the
  address they went to. If you delete the default, the newest remaining address becomes the
  default.

A customer who has only ever checked out as a guest has no saved addresses yet. The card then
shows the address from their latest order, marked **From their latest order**. Select **Edit**
and **Save address** to add it to their address book.

## Store credit

The **Store credit** card shows the money your shop holds for the customer, and every change to
it: who made it, why and when. A line that belongs to an order links to that order. Select **Add
credit** or **Remove credit** to change it by hand, with a reason (the customer does not see the
reason). Refunds and returns to store credit go onto the same balance. Store credit is not a gift
card: no code is ever sent.

**Spending it at checkout.** A customer who is signed in sees "You have ₹X in store credit" in
the order summary, switched on. It pays what is left after discounts and gift cards:

- When it covers everything, there is nothing else to pay. The order is paid by store credit.
- When it covers part, the rest goes through the way to pay they pick online. Cash on delivery is
  not offered for the rest, the same as with a gift card.
- The credit is taken from the balance when the order is placed, so it can't be spent twice. If
  the online payment never arrives and the order is cancelled (unpaid orders cancel themselves
  after 30 minutes), it goes back onto the balance as **Returned (order not paid)**.
- Store credit is a way to pay, not a discount: the order total, the taxable value and the GST
  stay the same, and the invoice shows it under how the order was paid.
- A refund of the part paid with store credit always goes back onto the balance.

**Spending it on an order you build.** On a draft order for the customer, select **Apply store
credit** to pay part or all of it from their balance. See
[Drafts → Paying with store credit](/merchant-guide/orders/drafts#paying-with-store-credit).

Customers see their balance and its history under **Account → Store credit** on your store.
Guests don't see store credit at checkout.

**Expiry dates (optional).** When you **Add credit**, you can pick an **Expiry date**: the last
day it can be spent. Leave it empty and the credit never expires. The same optional **Store credit
expiry** is in the **Refund** dialog (when you refund to store credit) and in a return's **Store
credit** step: empty means it never expires.

**Store credit an order was paid with, coming back.** When you cancel such an order, or refund
its store credit payment, the dialog shows **Store credit expiry** already filled in with the
last day of the credit it was spent from (the latest one, or empty when any of it never
expired). Keep it, change it, or clear it so it never expires. When nobody is asked (an unpaid
online order cancels itself after 30 minutes), each part keeps the date of the credit it came
from. If that date has already passed, that part comes back already expired.

- Spending always uses the credit that expires soonest first, then credit that never expires.
- After the last day, whatever is left of that credit comes off the balance with its own line,
  **Expired**. Nothing else changes, and money that has expired can never be spent, even before
  that line shows up (it is written within the hour).
- The history shows "Can be spent until …" under a credit with a date, and the customer sees
  "use by …" on their account page.

**Emails.** The order confirmation says "Store credit used ₹X · balance ₹Y" when the order used
store credit. A refund or a return paid as store credit says "₹X added to your store credit ·
balance ₹Y". Other emails don't mention store credit. A draft invoice paid partly with store
credit shows the total, **Store credit −₹X** and **To pay ₹Y**.

**Old store-credit gift cards.** Before store credit was a balance, refunds to store credit made a
gift card, and the Shopify import brings Shopify's gift cards over as new codes. **Products → Gift
cards** lists these under **Gift cards that were store credit**. Refund cards made here are ticked
for you; Shopify does not say which of its cards were store credit, so tick those yourself. Select
**Move to balance**: the value left on each card goes onto its customer's store credit balance
(a customer is found or made from the card's email; a card with neither is left alone), and the
card is emptied and disabled so its code stops working. Both the card's history and the store
credit history say what happened, and a card keeps its expiry date. Each card moves once: running
it again changes nothing.

## Merge two customers into one

One shopper can end up with two customer records, for example when you corrected the email on
their order and they then signed in with the new email: their account shows no orders, because
the orders sit on the old record.

1. Open the customer you want to keep and select **More actions → Merge with another customer…**.
2. Search for the other record by name, email or mobile number and select it.
3. Check **What moves here**: orders, saved addresses, store credit, gift cards, sign-ins
   (Google, Apple, Facebook, Truecaller and passkeys), carts, timeline notes, reviews and inbox
   conversations. Tags, the note, marketing consent history and browsing history come too.
4. Under **Keep these details**, choose whose **Email**, **Phone** and **Name** to keep. The
   shopper signs in with the email you keep.
5. Select **Merge customers**.

Everything moves in one go, or nothing does. Store credit is added to the kept balance, and a
credit with an expiry date keeps that date; both store credit histories show the move. Your saved
default addresses stay the defaults. The other record is closed: it disappears from the list, its
sign-ins on other devices end, and an old link to it opens the kept customer. Both timelines say
what happened and who did it.

Merging needs both **Edit customers** and **Delete customers**, because it removes a record. It
can't be undone, and an erased customer can't be merged.

<Callout type="info">
  To move just one order to another customer, use **Change customer** on the order instead. See
  [Orders](/merchant-guide/orders/orders#move-an-order-to-another-customer).
</Callout>

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | Why it happens | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Enter a ten digit mobile number" | Couriers need a mobile number on every address. | Type a 10 digit mobile number, with or without +91. |
| "Enter a six digit PIN code" | Indian PIN codes are six digits and never start with 0. | Check the PIN code on the shopper's message. |
| City and state didn't fill in | The PIN code isn't in the directory, or it isn't installed on your server. | Type the city and pick the state yourself. |
| "Another customer already has this email" | The email belongs to a second record of the same person. | Open that customer, or merge the two (More actions → Merge with another customer…). |
| A shopper's account shows no orders | Their orders are on another record (an older email). | Merge the two records, or move the order with **Change customer**. |
| "An erased or already merged customer cannot be merged" | That record was erased on request, or already merged. | Nothing to merge; open the kept customer. |
