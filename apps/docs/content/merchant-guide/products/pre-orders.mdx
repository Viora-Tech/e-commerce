# Pre-orders

**Where to find it:** **Products**, then **Pre-orders**. **What to do:** create a campaign and add
the products of your launch. **What you'll see:** the last 30 days of pre-orders and their revenue,
every product taking pre-orders with how many are left under its limit, and every campaign with
its orders and revenue.

## One product, or a campaign

- **One product:** open the product, then the **Pre-order & launch** card. Leave **Pre-order
  campaign** on **None**, switch pre-order on and set the ship date, limit, button and payment.
- **A campaign:** one set of rules for many products, for example "Lofree launch" for three
  keyboards. Change the ship date once and every product in it follows.

A product joins a campaign from the campaign page (**Products** card, **Browse**) or from its own
**Pre-order campaign** picker. A product is in one campaign at a time.

## What a campaign sets

- **Shipping and limit:** an expected ship date, or "ships in N days"; and how many units of
  each variant may be sold beyond stock. Leave the limit blank for no limit.
- **Payment:** charge in full, a fixed deposit per unit, or a percentage deposit. The balance is
  asked for later, as with a single pre-order product (Settings, Payments, Pre-orders).
- **A product's own settings win.** In the product's **Pre-order & launch** card, tick **Keep this
  product's own** for the ship date, limit, button label, payment or note, and fill them in there.

In a campaign, a product sells as a pre-order when its stock runs out. While it has stock it sells
normally, at its usual price.

## A limit for one variant

The product's limit counts each variant on its own: with a limit of 20, every size or colour can
take 20 pre-orders. When one variant should take fewer or more, for example only 5 of a rare
colour, open the product, go to **Variants**, click that variant to open its page and fill in
**Pre-order limit** in the **Inventory** card. The field shows only while the product takes pre-orders.

- **Blank** uses the product's limit. The field shows it in grey ("Uses product: 20", or "No
  limit"). In a campaign it uses the campaign's limit, unless the product keeps its own.
- **A number** wins over the product's and the campaign's limit, for that variant only.
- The cart, checkout and draft orders all stop at the variant's limit. There is no limit on the
  product's total across its variants.

## Rules

- **Pre-order discount:** a percent or an amount off while the product sells as a pre-order.
- **Markets:** where the campaign applies. None ticked means every market.
- **Stock locations:** whose stock decides "sold out". None ticked means every location that ships
  online orders.
- **Sales channels:** **Online store** covers the cart and checkout. **Social** and **AI agents**
  show sold-out campaign items as "pre-order" in the Meta, Google and ChatGPT feeds.
- **Pre-orders and in-stock items in one cart:**
  - **Allow, ship in-stock items now:** the order is split; in-stock items ship first.
  - **Allow, ship everything together:** every parcel of the order shows as waiting for the
    pre-order. Pack them all once the pre-order stock arrives.
  - **Don't allow:** the cart refuses the mix and shows your message (set it in **Content**).
- **An item sells out while it is in a cart:** turn it into a pre-order at checkout, or stop the
  checkout with "out of stock".

The cart and checkout check every rule on the server, whatever the page showed.

## Content and design

The **Storefront** card has two tabs, with a live preview of the product button, the cart line and
the order beside them.

- **Content:** the button text, the badge, the note under the button, the cart line text, the note
  kept on the order, and the message for a refused mixed cart. Notes may use `{ship_date}` and
  `{ship_month}`. Blank fields use the product's note, then the store's (Settings, Payments).
- **Design:** the button and badge colours. Blank uses your theme's colours.

## Pausing and deleting

A **paused** campaign is kept; its products sell by their own pre-order settings meanwhile.
**Deleting** a campaign takes its products out of it; past orders keep their lines and revenue.

## From Shopify

The Shopify import turns each pre-order purchase option (a selling plan group named like
"Pre-order") into a campaign with its products. Set its ship date, payment and rules here after the
import; a later import keeps your changes.
