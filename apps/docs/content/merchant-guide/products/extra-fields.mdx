# Extra fields

**Where to find it:** a product's page → the **Extra fields** card, just above **More settings**.
**What to do:** type
a value into each field, such as a care guide or a warranty period, then press **Save extra
fields**. **What you'll see:** "Extra fields saved". Your theme can show these on the product
page, and a section can [connect to them](/merchant-guide/online-store/website-builder#show-your-stores-own-data-in-a-section).

The card is empty until you have made a field. Press **Add a field** right there to make one
without leaving the page. **Manage extra fields** opens **Settings → Custom data**, where you
can edit or delete them. A new product has to be saved once before its extra fields can be
filled in. Collections have the same card. More in
[Content types](/merchant-guide/content/content-types#extra-fields-on-products-and-collections).

## Filter by an extra field

**Where to find it:** **Settings → Custom data** → a product extra field → **Use as filter**.
**What to do:** switch it on and save. **What you'll see:** a **Filter** tag next to the field,
and a group with its values (Grip: Claw, Palm...) in the filters of your collection and search
pages. Only product fields of short text, choice, number or yes/no can be filters.

The link to a filtered page reads like Shopify's (`?filter.p.m.custom.grip=Claw`), so links
shared from your old store still work. Importing from Shopify switches this on for the fields
your Shopify store filtered by (Search & Discovery), read from its collection page. A store
behind a password shows none, so tick them here.
