# GST invoices

Install the **GST invoices** app from Apps to issue tax invoices, credit notes, packing
slips, order confirmations, receipt vouchers and refund vouchers straight from your orders.

## Setting it up

Under the app's **Settings**, fill in:

- **Business details** — your legal name (as registered for GST), GSTIN (checked against its
  check digit as you type), address, and logo/signature for the PDF. Printed as the supplier
  on every document, per CGST rule 46.
- **Numbering series** — how invoice numbers are generated.
- **Template and style** — how the PDF looks; the preview updates as you type.
- **Queue and delivery** — how and when documents go out.

Documents sit **In queue** until they're **Issued**; a document can also be **Cancelled**.

## Day to day

**Invoices waiting to be issued** shows what's queued. Once GST invoices is fully set up,
new eligible orders generate their tax invoice automatically; cancellations and refunds
generate credit notes and refund vouchers the same way. **GST documents** lists everything
issued and cancelled so far.
