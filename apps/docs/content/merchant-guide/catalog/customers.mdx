# Customers

Every shopper who places an order gets a customer profile under **Customers**. Open one to see
their orders, a note for your team, the messages they were sent, and their contact details.

## Change a customer's name or phone

Select **Edit** at the top of the customer's page, change the **Name** or **Phone**, then select
**Save**. The email comes from the orders they place, so it can't be changed here.

<Callout type="info">
  To change the details on one order only (for example, a shopper asks you to send this parcel to
  their office), use **Edit** on the order's **Customer details** card instead. See
  [Orders](/merchant-guide/catalog/orders#change-the-customers-details-on-an-order).
</Callout>

## Saved addresses

The **Addresses** card lists the customer's saved addresses, with their default one marked
**Default**.

- **Add address**: type the name, mobile number and address. Type the **PIN code** and the city
  and state fill in on their own; change them if they are wrong. Tick **Make this the default
  address** to use it for their next order.
- **Edit**: change any line of an address and select **Save address**.
- **Delete** (the bin icon): removes it from their address book. Orders already shipped keep the
  address they went to. If you delete the default, the newest remaining address becomes the
  default.

A customer who has only ever checked out as a guest has no saved addresses yet. The card then
shows the address from their latest order, marked **From their latest order**. Select **Edit**
and **Save address** to add it to their address book.

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | Why it happens | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Enter a ten digit mobile number" | Couriers need a mobile number on every address. | Type a 10 digit mobile number, with or without +91. |
| "Enter a six digit PIN code" | Indian PIN codes are six digits and never start with 0. | Check the PIN code on the shopper's message. |
| City and state didn't fill in | The PIN code isn't in the directory, or it isn't installed on your server. | Type the city and pick the state yourself. |
