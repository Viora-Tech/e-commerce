# Products and collections

## Products

**Products → Product list** shows everything you sell. Select **Add product** to create one.

When you're filling in a product, the form is organised into sections:

- **Media** — photos and videos.
- **Organisation** — where it fits (type, vendor, tags, collections).
- **Pricing** — price, compare-at price.
- **Inventory** — stock tracking, quantity.
- **Variants** — sizes, colours, or any combination you sell separately.
- **Shipping** — weight and dimensions used for shipping rates.
- **Pre-order & launch** — sell a product before it's in stock, with a launch countdown and
  an optional deposit (see below).
- **Search engine listing** — the page title and description shoppers see in Google.

You can bulk-select products to delete them, and import or export products as CSV from the
product list.

### Pre-orders and deposits

A product can be marked for pre-order with a launch date. Shoppers can pay a deposit now and
the rest later — see [Orders](/merchant-guide/orders) for how the balance gets collected.

## Collections

**Collections** group products together for a category page on your storefront —
"New arrivals", "Under ₹999", and so on.

There are two kinds:

- **Manual** — you add products one by one and drag them into the order shoppers see.
- **Automatic** — products matching conditions you set (price, tag, type, vendor…) are added
  and removed for you automatically, no manual upkeep.

Each collection also has its own **Search engine listing** (title and description) and a
**Status** (published or not), just like a product.
