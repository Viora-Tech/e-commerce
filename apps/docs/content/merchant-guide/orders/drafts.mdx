# Drafts

**Orders → Drafts** holds orders you build yourself for a customer, such as one agreed on
WhatsApp, and checkouts your shoppers have not paid for yet. They stay hidden from your store until
you convert them. The tabs are **All drafts**, **From checkout (unpaid)**, **Created by staff**,
**Open** and **Invoice sent**, and the tiles show open drafts, invoices sent and the value in
drafts. The filter button beside the search narrows the list by **Created** (a preset or your own
dates) and **Total** (a min and max in rupees). Open a draft, add products, set a discount or shipping if you need to, then use **Send
invoice** (the customer gets a pay link) or **Convert to order**. A converted draft is billed
for GST the same way a checkout order is, including the warehouse's GST number.

## Paying with store credit

When the draft's customer has [store credit](/merchant-guide/customers/customers#store-credit), the
**Store credit** card on the draft shows their balance. Select **Apply store credit** and enter how
much to use, up to the balance or the draft's total, whichever is less (**Use ₹X** fills in the
most). Select **Save**. The **Payment** card then shows **Store credit −₹Y** under the total and
**To pay** for the rest.

- **Nothing leaves the balance while it is a draft.** The credit is taken when the draft becomes
  an order, so changing, saving or deleting the draft never costs the customer anything. The
  customer's history shows **Paid an order** with the order number.
- **Covers everything**: select **Convert to order**. The order is paid by store credit and
  nothing else is collected. **Send invoice** is off, as there is nothing to pay.
- **Covers part**: the payment method you chose collects the rest. **Convert to order** takes
  the credit and leaves the rest due, to collect later with **Collect payment**. Or **Send
  invoice**: the pay page shows the store credit and asks the customer only for the rest. The
  credit is held when they press Pay and kept once their payment arrives; if the order is
  cancelled unpaid, it goes back onto their balance.
- **The balance changed meanwhile** (they spent it at checkout, or someone removed it): the draft
  can't be converted until you lower the store credit and save. The draft says why.
- **Picking another customer** removes the store credit from the draft, as it belongs to the
  customer it was applied for.
- Store credit is a way to pay, not a discount: the total, the taxable value and the GST stay the
  same.

## Unpaid checkouts

When a shopper checks out with an online payment (Razorpay, Cashfree) or with UPI to your own ID
or bank transfer, the checkout is kept as a draft with its own number, such as **#D57**, until the
money arrives. It only becomes an order, with the next order number, once it is paid. So your
**Orders** list only has real orders, and order numbers are not used up by payments that failed.
Cash on delivery is always an order straight away, and so is anything a gift card or store credit
pays for.

- **Where to find them**: **Orders → Drafts → From checkout (unpaid)**, newest activity first.
  Each row shows the shopper, the total and how the payment is going: **Unpaid**, **Payment
  failed ×3**, **Shopper says paid**, **Refund needed** or **Expired**.
- **Online payments**: when the payment comes in, the draft becomes an order by itself, and the
  shopper gets the usual order confirmation with the real order number. If the shopper's card
  fails, they can try again from their order page, on the same draft.
- **UPI or bank transfer**: the shopper lands on a page that says their order #D57 is received,
  with your QR code, UPI ID, the exact amount and your bank details, and gets the same by email.
  They can press **I've paid** and type the UTR. When the money is in your account, open the draft
  and select **Mark as paid**: it becomes an order and the shopper gets their confirmation.
- **Convert to order (unpaid)**: turns the draft into an unpaid order now, for example to ship
  on trust. The payment can still come in later.
- **Copy payment link**: the shopper's own page for this checkout, to send on WhatsApp.
- **Same items again**: if the same shopper (same email or phone) checks out the same items again
  within your [repeated checkouts](/merchant-guide/settings/payments) window (24 hours by default,
  counted from their last try), they carry on with the same draft and see "You already placed this
  order (#D57) and it is waiting for payment". Different items make a new draft. A shopper who
  uses a different email and a different phone number is a new shopper to the store.
- **Held stock**: the items are held for your unpaid window (30 minutes for online payments; 48
  hours for transfers unless you cancel transfers sooner). After that the draft holds nothing; if
  the payment still arrives and the items are in stock, it becomes an order as usual. If they sold
  out meanwhile, the draft shows **Refund needed**: refund the shopper from your payment company.
- **After 7 days** without activity an unpaid checkout stops waiting for payment and leaves the
  list.
- **Turn it off**: **Settings → Payments → More → Repeated checkouts → Keep unpaid checkouts as
  drafts**. With it off, unpaid checkouts are numbered orders again, as before.
