# Drafts

**Orders → Drafts** holds orders you build yourself for a customer, such as one agreed on
WhatsApp. They stay hidden from your store until you convert them. The tabs are **All drafts**,
**Open** and **Invoice sent**, and the tiles show open drafts, invoices sent and the value in
drafts. The filter button beside the search narrows the list by **Created** (a preset or your own
dates) and **Total** (a min and max in rupees). Open a draft, add products, set a discount or shipping if you need to, then use **Send
invoice** (the customer gets a pay link) or **Convert to order**. A converted draft is billed
for GST the same way a checkout order is, including the warehouse's GST number.
