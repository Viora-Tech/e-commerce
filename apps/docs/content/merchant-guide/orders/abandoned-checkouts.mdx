# Abandoned checkouts

**Orders → Abandoned checkouts** lists carts where a shopper left their email or phone but did
not pay. The last 7 days are summed up in four tiles: **Abandoned carts**, **Recovered**, **Value
waiting** and **Reminders sent**.

<Steps>

### Pick a tab

**All**, **Not recovered**, **Reminder sent** or **Recovered**. Select the **Recovered** or
**Reminders sent** tile to filter the same way, or use the search box.

### Open a checkout

Each row shows the customer, the first product and "+ 2 more", the value, how long ago they
left and whether a reminder went out. Select a row, or its **⋯ → View checkout**.

### Read the checkout

You'll see the cart with pictures, prices and totals; the customer (name, email, phone and where
they came from); whether a reminder was sent; and a short activity list: started, last active,
reminder sent, came back and ordered. Shipping shows "Worked out at checkout", because it is not
known until the shopper picks an address.

### Nudge them

Press **Send reminder** (or **Send again**). It goes by email and WhatsApp, to the channels the
customer has agreed to; if they have not agreed to marketing messages, nothing is sent. **Copy
cart link** gives you a fresh link that takes the shopper back to their cart, to paste into a
chat yourself. On the list, **Send reminders** sends one to every visible cart that has not had
one. A checkout with no email cannot be sent a reminder.

</Steps>
