# Edit or cancel an order

## What this is for

A customer calls to add a mouse pad, swap a size, or drop an item that ran out. Instead of
cancelling and asking them to order again, you change the order itself. The order keeps its
number, the stock and GST follow the change, and the money difference is collected or refunded
in the same step.

## How it works

- You can edit an open or completed order, shipped or not. Gift cards on the order cannot be
  changed.
- Items added after the order shipped go in a new parcel, so they ship separately.
- If the new total is **higher**, the customer owes the difference. You can email them a payment
  link, or collect it by hand from the order page.
- If the new total is **lower**, you choose how the refund goes back (below).
- Your apps follow the edit: for example the GST invoices app rebuilds a queued invoice, or
  replaces an issued one that is now wrong.
- If your role has **Needs approval** on refunds, an edit that refunds money waits for an approver
  (see [Approvals](/merchant-guide/settings/approvals)).

## Edit an order step by step

<Steps>

### Open the editor

Open the order and select **Edit order**.

### Change the items

- **Search products to add** finds a product; pick the variant and quantity.
- **Add custom item** adds a line that is not in your catalogue (a name and a price).
- Change **Quantity** or **Price** on a line, or select **Remove**. When you remove an item you
  can choose whether it goes back on sale (restock).
- **Add discount** on a line takes an **Amount** or **Percent** off each item.

### Change shipping and discount

Under **Shipping and discount**, change the **Shipping** charge and its **Shipping rate name**
(for example "Bluedart"), and the order **Discount** with its **Discount reason** (for example
"UPI discount").

### Review

Select **Update order**. **Review the edit** lists every change and the **Summary**: **New
total**, **Paid by customer**, and either **Amount to collect** or **Refund owed**.

- Amount to collect: tick **Email the customer a payment link** to send them a link to pay the
  difference online.
- Refund owed: pick how it goes back (see "Refund choices" below).
- **Send the customer the updated order** emails them (and sends WhatsApp when it is connected).
- **Reason for edit** is a note only staff see, on the order timeline.

### Save

Select **Update order**. The order shows **Order updated**, and the timeline records the edit.

</Steps>

<Callout>
  Already shipped? Changes do not change the shipment that is on its way. Create a new shipment
  for added items, or a return for removed ones.
</Callout>

## Refund choices

The same four choices show when an edit or a cancellation gives money back:

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Refund to the original payment** | The money goes back the way the customer paid, through your payment company |
| **Mark as refunded by hand** | You send the money yourself (UPI, bank transfer, cash); it is only recorded here |
| **Store credit** | The amount goes onto the customer's store credit balance; you can set an expiry date |
| **No refund** | Nothing goes back now; you can refund later from the payment card |

A payment taken on another platform, or through a payment company you have since disconnected,
can only be marked as refunded by hand.

## Cancel an order

1. On the order, select **More actions → Cancel order**.
2. Pick a **Reason for cancellation** (customer changed their mind, out of stock, fraudulent or
   suspicious order, payment declined, other) and add a note if you like.
3. Choose the refund (above). **Later** sends nothing back now.
4. Leave **Restock items** on to put unsent items back on sale.
5. Leave **Send the customer an email that the order was cancelled** on to tell them.
6. Select **Cancel order**.

If a courier was booked, you can cancel the label in the same step; if the courier refuses,
nothing is cancelled and you see the courier's reason. You can restore a cancelled order from
**More actions** as long as no money went back.

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "This order can no longer be edited." | The order is cancelled, or it was imported and not taken over. For an imported order still open, select **Manage this order in Koomerce** on its Imported card first |
| "The customer has no email address" | Collect the difference by hand from the order page, or add their email first |
| "Sent for approval" | Your role needs approval for refunds or cancellations. The owner or an approver decides in Settings → Approvals |

## FAQ

<Faq>
  <FaqItem question="Does the order number change?">
    No. The order keeps its number; the timeline shows each edit.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="What happens to the GST invoice?">
    With the GST invoices app, a queued invoice is rebuilt. An issued invoice that now bills too
    little is cancelled and issued again; a removed item is credited by its refund's credit note.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>
