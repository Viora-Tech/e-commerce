# Build your own section

Ready-made sections cover most pages. When you want something they don't offer, such as three
product cards next to a short story, or a "Why shop with us" strip with icons, you can build it
yourself from small pieces, then save it to use again.

## The pieces

**Layout blocks** hold other blocks. You can put a layout block inside another one.

| Block | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Group | A box. Blocks inside sit stacked or side by side, with space between them. |
| Columns | Two to four columns side by side. On phones they stack. |
| Grid | Blocks in rows and columns, as many across as you choose for each screen size. |
| Tabs | Tabs along the top; each tab holds its own blocks. |
| Accordion | Titles that open and close; each one holds its own blocks. |
| Carousel | Slides shoppers swipe sideways; each slide holds its own blocks. |

**Elements** are the things you see: Heading, Text, Image, Button, Icon, Video, Spacer,
Divider, Badge, Product card and Custom HTML.

## Build a section, step by step

1. Open **Online store → Themes → Customize** and pick the page.
2. In the left panel, press **+ Add section** and open **Layout**. Choose **Group** (or
   Columns). It appears on the page with an empty space inside.
3. In the Sections list, under your Group, press **Add block**. Choose an element, for example
   **Heading**. Add a **Text** and a **Button** the same way.
4. Click a block to change it on the right: its words, picture or link at the top, and its
   **Style** underneath.
5. To change the order, drag a block by its handle in the Sections list, or select it and press
   **Alt + Up** or **Alt + Down**. You can also drag blocks on the page itself, into or out of a
   Group.
6. Press **Save** at the top when you are happy.

## Style a block

Every block has a **Style** panel under its settings:

- **Spacing**: space above and below, and space inside.
- **Size**: automatic, full width or just as wide as its content; a largest width; a smallest
  height.
- **Text**: size, weight and alignment.
- **Colour**: text and background. Pick one of your theme's colours so it follows your colour
  scheme and dark mode, or choose **Custom colour** and type one, such as `#1f1f21`.
- **Corners and shadow**.
- **Visibility**: show or hide the block on phones, tablets and computers. Hidden blocks take no
  space on that screen size.

## Show store data in a block

Headings, text, images, buttons and links inside your section can show store data, just like
other sections: press the database button beside the setting and pick a source, such as the
product's title on a product page. What you typed before stays as the fallback. See
[Online store](/merchant-guide/online-store/website-builder) for how dynamic sources work.

## Save it as your own section

1. In the Sections list, open the **⋯** menu on your Group (or any section).
2. Choose **Save as my section**, give it a name and press **Save section**.
3. To use it again, press **+ Add section** on any page and open the **My sections** tab. Click
   it to add a copy.

Each copy is separate: changing it on one page does not change the others, or the saved one.
A saved section that holds sections from an imported Shopify theme shows in **My sections** only
while you edit that theme, because only that theme can draw them. You can keep up to 40 saved sections. To delete one, point at it in **My sections** and press
the bin.

## Copy and paste

Select a block and press **Ctrl + C** (**Cmd + C** on a Mac), then select where it should go
and press **Ctrl + V**. It is pasted just after the selected block. **Ctrl + D** duplicates
the selected block. **Ctrl + Z** undoes.

## Custom HTML and embeds

The **Custom HTML** element takes your own HTML, or an embed code from YouTube, Vimeo or
Instagram. For your shoppers' safety it is checked when you save and again when the page is
shown: scripts, forms, styles, class names and anything from other websites are removed, and videos play in
a safe box. If something you pasted does not show, it was removed by this check.

## Good to know

- The page's main content (for example the product details on a product page), the header and
  the footer cannot go inside a layout block.
- A block you hide with the eye stays in the list but is not shown to shoppers.
- Very large saved sections (hundreds of blocks) cannot be saved. Split them into two.
