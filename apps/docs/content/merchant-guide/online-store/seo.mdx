# SEO

## What this is for

**Online store → SEO** shows how well Google, Bing and AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity) can find
and show your products, collections and pages, and lists what to fix, most important first.
Better search titles, descriptions, photos and text bring free visitors who are already looking
for what you sell.

## How it works

- The page checks every live product, collection and published page, and your menus, and gives
  an **SEO score** out of 100: **Looking good**, **A few things to fix** or **Needs work**.
- **Issues to fix** lists each problem with its impact (**High**, **Medium**, **Low**). Select
  **Edit** to open the product, collection, page or menu and fix it by hand.
- With the Google Search Console app connected, it also uses your real Google results: pages
  shown often but rarely clicked, and pages just off page 1.

What it checks:

| Kind | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Search title | Missing, longer than about 60 characters, too short, or the same as another page's |
| Search description | Missing, longer than about 160 characters, shorter than 70, or repeated |
| Photos | A product with no photos; photos without alt text |
| Text | Product descriptions under 50 words; thin pages |
| Google Shopping | No brand (vendor), no barcode (GTIN), no product category |
| Web address | Not short lowercase words joined by hyphens |
| Links | A link to a page that no longer exists; a link still pointing at your old Shopify store |
| Collections | No live products; no collection image |
| Keywords | No search keywords chosen for a product |

## Fix with AI

If **Write with AI** is on in [Settings → AI](/merchant-guide/settings/ai), **Fix with AI** writes
what is missing:

- **Write search titles and descriptions**
- **Write photo alt text**
- **Write fuller product descriptions**
- **Suggest search keywords**

Nothing is saved until you review the changes and press **Apply**. One run covers a limited
number of products; run it again for the rest. More about keywords:
[Search keywords](/merchant-guide/products/search-keywords).

<Callout>
  Start with the **High impact** filter. Fixing missing photos, titles and descriptions on your
  best sellers does more than polishing everything else.
</Callout>

## Search opportunities

The **Search opportunities** tab (Online store → SEO) needs the
[Google Search Console](/merchant-guide/apps/google-search-console) app. It lists every product,
collection and page Google showed in the last 28 days, with its top searches, views, clicks,
average position, the search title and description Google shows now, and its gaps from the
report. The list starts with the biggest chance: pages Google shows often at position 4 to 15,
where a sharper title and description win the most clicks.

**Fix with AI** works on one row or up to 10 ticked rows. For each it writes a search title (up to
60 characters), a search description (up to 155) and an 80 to 150 word intro or FAQ, built on the
searches people really make. You see each draft beside what Google shows now and can edit it.
Nothing changes until you press **Apply**, which saves the ticked titles and descriptions. The
intro or FAQ is never saved for you: copy it into the description or page text if you like it.
You need the right to edit products (for products and collections) or the online store (for
pages).

While Search Console is connected, the store owner gets a **weekly SEO email** on Monday morning:
the pages that moved up or down since last week, and the top three things to fix next. Turn it off
in **Settings → Notifications** (Weekly SEO email).

## What the store does by itself

You do not have to set these up:

- **Sitemap** at `/sitemap.xml` and **robots.txt**, kept up to date with your live products,
  collections, pages and blog posts.
- **`/llms.txt`**, a plain summary of your store for AI search.
- Each page has its canonical address on your primary domain, and Open Graph tags for social
  shares.
- Theme previews and unpublished pages are never indexed.
- Changing a product's web address keeps the old one working through a redirect
  ([Menus and redirects](/merchant-guide/content/menus-and-redirects)).

**Online store → Preferences** has **Search engine visibility**: ownership tags for Google Search
Console, Bing and Meta, and a switch to keep the whole store out of search engines while you build
it ([Preferences](/merchant-guide/online-store/preferences)).
