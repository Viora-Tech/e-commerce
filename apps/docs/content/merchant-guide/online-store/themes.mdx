# Themes

Open **Online Store → Themes** to pick or change your theme. Edit it in the [website builder](/merchant-guide/online-store/website-builder), or bring one over with [Import a Shopify theme](/merchant-guide/online-store/import-a-shopify-theme).

## Zero Drift theme sections

The **Zero Drift theme** group at the top of the **Add** list holds the sections from our Zero Drift
theme, rebuilt for this editor. They start with the same text as the theme. Pick your
own photos, products and collections in each one's settings.

| Section | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Ships today** | The order-by time for same-day dispatch, with a few promises such as free shipping |
| **Category panels** | Your collections as tall photo panels that open when a shopper points at one |
| **Featured carousel** | Products you pick, shown one large slide at a time |
| **Reviews showcase** | Your star rating, a moving wall of reviews and a window of customer photos |
| **Promo bento** | Promo tiles of different sizes, each linked to a collection or product |
| **Latest drop** | One new product with a short blurb, spec chips and a countdown |
| **Drops teaser** | Cards for what is coming next, each with a date and a status |
| **Perfect match** | A "find your perfect match" heading with products circling it |
| **Brand lab** | The brands you carry, each with a logo and a short line |
| **Logo carousel** | Brand logos, photo tiles or press quotes in a moving row |
| **Drop reveal** | A box that opens as shoppers scroll, showing a drop's products |
| **Notes series** | Your latest blog posts as a numbered series |
| **Social wall** | Instagram, X and YouTube posts with tabs, as a grid or a moving row |
| **Community invite** | An invite to your Discord or community, with perks and a button |
| **Customer reviews** | Your rating and a list of reviews you type in |
| **Image comparison** | Two photos with a slider between them, for a before and after |
| **Mobile dock** | A bar of shortcuts at the bottom of the screen on phones |
| **Signup popup** | An email signup that pops up in a corner, with a photo, or with products |

Reviews and social posts are typed in by you: the editor does not read them from another app
yet. Each section starts with sample reviews or posts, so replace them before you publish.

### Header, footer and home page

- **Hero carousel** takes photo or video slides, with dots, a slide counter and a dark shade
  behind the text so it stays readable.
- **Tabbed product grid** can show how many products each tab has, and the heading can change with
  the tab.
- **Announcement bar** holds several messages. Pick **Rotate** to show one at a time, or
  **Scroll** to run them as a moving line. Add your social links to show them at the side.
- **Header** has menus with pictures, a search box with popular searches, brand shortcuts and
  feature cards in the menu. Choose whether it stays at the top while shoppers scroll.
- **Footer** has up to three help cards, your social links, payment icons and your policies.
- **Email signup** (newsletter) can ask shoppers what they are interested in and list why they should join.
- **Cookie banner** sits in the footer group. Change its wording and position there; your privacy
  settings still decide when it shows.

### Product page

Open a product template in **Customize** and pick a product to preview it with.

- **Product information** has a photo gallery that zooms where you point (on a computer), and
  switches for the extra rows under the buy button: payment offers, trust badges, a returns line,
  a WhatsApp question button, share links and more. These start switched off; turn on the ones
  you want.
- Add **Product tabs**, **Spec groups**, **Spec strip**, **Product features**, **Size guide**,
  **Size table**, **Product reviews** and **Sticky add to cart** from the **Products** group.
  Specification and size rows can be typed in, or connected to a product's custom field so each
  product shows its own.
- **Product reviews** shows a placeholder for now: reviews cannot be collected yet.

To try changes safely, go to **Online Store → Themes**, open **More actions** on your theme, select
**Duplicate**, and select **Customize** on the copy. Publish it only when you are happy.
