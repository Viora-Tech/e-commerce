# Attribution

**Growth → Attribution** is the detailed report behind the Growth overview. Pick the dates at
the top; every number is compared with the period of the same length just before it.

## What is on the page

- **Tiles**: sessions, conversion rate, average order value, total sales and sales from
  marketing, each with its change.
- **Sessions by traffic type**: direct, paid, organic and unknown.
- **How sales are credited**: the rule below, on the page so nobody has to guess.
- **Attributed sales over time**, daily or weekly.
- **Sales by source** (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Google, WhatsApp, AI assistants, Email,
  Direct, Other) or **by channel** (organic search, paid social and so on). Each row splits
  orders and sales into **first-time** and **returning** buyers. Select a source to see the
  campaigns and tagged links under it. On the channel view, **Paid** and **Organic** narrow the
  table.
- **What marketing earned**, split into campaigns, automations, discount codes and links, and
  your **best source** for the period.
- **Top campaigns**, from your sends, automations and tagged links.

## How credit is given

Koomerce uses **last non-direct click** with a **30-day window**:

- An order is credited to the last channel, link or campaign the shopper came from in the 30
  days before buying.
- A later visit typed straight into the browser (direct) does not take that credit away.
- Orders with no tracked visit at all (imported orders, drafts, orders made through the API)
  count as **Not tracked**, not as direct.

## FAQ

<Faq>

<FaqItem question="Why do Attribution and Analytics show different sales?">

Analytics counts every order. Attribution's **sales from marketing** only counts orders whose
last non-direct visit came from a channel, link or campaign.
