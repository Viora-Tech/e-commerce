# Marketing automations

Marketing messages that run on their own (abandoned checkout and cart reminders, welcome
messages, win-back offers for customers who stopped buying) are workflows in the
[Automations](/merchant-guide/apps/automations) app, under the **Marketing** category of its
templates. **Growth → Automations** opens them, and their sales still count in Growth's
sales-by-source table next to [Campaigns](/merchant-guide/growth/campaigns).

They follow the same rules as campaigns: only customers who agreed to marketing on that channel
get them, and every message carries an unsubscribe link.
