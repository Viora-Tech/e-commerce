# Campaigns

- **Campaigns** are one-off sends to a segment of your customers: an email or WhatsApp
  broadcast for a launch, a sale or a restock.
- **Automations** run on their own once turned on: abandoned-checkout reminders, a welcome
  message for new subscribers (sent once they confirm their email), and win-back messages for
  lapsed buyers.

Both count toward the sales-by-source table under **Email** or **WhatsApp**, and appear by name
in **Top campaigns** and in a source's expanded row.

## Design an email with the visual builder

An email campaign can be built two ways. Pick one at the top of the **Content** card:

- **Block composer**: stack ready-made blocks (header, text, image, button, products,
  discount, divider). Quick, and always on brand.
- **Visual builder**: a full-screen drag-and-drop editor. Click **Design with visual builder**.

The builder starts from your brand: your theme's logo, colours and social links. Drag blocks
from the right-hand panel onto the email:

- **Layout**: 1, 2 or 3 columns, divider, spacer.
- **Content**: text, image (double-click an image to upload or pick from **Content > Files**),
  button.
- **Store**: store logo, product card, product grid, discount code, social links and footer.
  Dropping a product block asks which products to show; their title, price and picture are
  copied in as they are now. Dropping a discount block asks for an active code from
  **Discounts**.

While editing text, the **Insert merge tag** list in the text toolbar adds a personal detail
such as `{first_name}` or `{store_name}`. For a button link, type a tag into the link, for
example `{shop_url}/collections/sale`.

**Unsubscribe link.** The **Footer** block carries `{unsubscribe_url}`. If your design has no
unsubscribe link, we add the standard footer (with your store address) to every email, as bulk
mail rules require.

Click **Save design**, then **Save** the campaign. The preview on the right shows the email as
customers get it, and **Send test** sends it to you. Switching back to the block composer keeps
the design, so you can return to it.

**Templates.** In the builder, **Templates** saves the current design for reuse in any campaign
of your store, or starts from a saved one (replacing what is on the canvas).
