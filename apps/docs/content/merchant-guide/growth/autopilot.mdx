# Autopilot

**Growth → Autopilot** plans campaigns for you. It looks at your abandoned carts, new products,
customers who have gone quiet and the festival calendar, writes a draft for each, and waits.
**Nothing is sent until you approve it.**

## Turn it on

1. Open **Growth → Autopilot** and switch **Autopilot** on.
2. Under **Goals and limits**, pick what it should work towards:
   - **Recover carts**: switch on abandoned-checkout reminders when carts are being left.
   - **Launch new products**: email your subscribers about products added in the last 14 days.
   - **Win back customers**: a campaign to subscribers with no order in 90 days (Autopilot
     makes a segment called *Autopilot: no order in 90 days* for it).
   - **Festive sales**: a campaign three days before Dhanteras, Diwali, Christmas, Holi, Raksha
     Bandhan and other sale days coming up in the next three weeks.
3. Set the **budget**: the most emails and campaigns it may plan in any seven days, and the
   **largest discount** it may offer (0 means never). Then **Save**.
4. Select **Plan campaigns**.

## Approve, edit or dismiss

Each proposal says why it was made ("38 subscribed customers have not ordered in 90 days"), who
it reaches, when it would go out (10:00 IST), and the discount if any.

- **Open draft** to read or change the email. It is a normal campaign draft.
- **Approve & schedule** creates the discount code (once per customer, valid 14 days, never
  above your limit), adds it to the email and schedules the campaign.
- **Approve & turn on** (cart recovery) switches the abandoned-checkout automation on.
- **Dismiss** removes the proposal and deletes its unsent draft.

The copy is written with AI when the AI writer is on for your store (Settings → AI, it uses
the product writer's allowance). Otherwise Autopilot uses a plain built-in message; each
proposal says which.

## Ad channels

Autopilot will also move ad budget between Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google Ads once
those apps can connect your ad accounts. Until then those rows say **Coming with the Facebook &
Instagram app** and **Coming with the Google app**, and Autopilot only plans emails.

## FAQ

<Faq>

<FaqItem question="Can Autopilot send something on its own?">

No. Every campaign waits for **Approve & schedule**. Turning Autopilot off stops new proposals;
approved campaigns stay scheduled and can be cancelled from **Campaigns**.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Why did Plan campaigns propose nothing?">

Either a proposal for that goal is already waiting, your weekly limits are used up, or the
numbers do not call for one (for example fewer than 10 subscribers, or no festival in the next
three weeks).

</FaqItem>

</Faq>
