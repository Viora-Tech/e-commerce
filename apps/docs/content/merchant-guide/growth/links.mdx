# Links

**Growth → Links** keeps short links like `waimers.in/l/diwali` with how many people clicked
each one, how many orders it brought and what those orders were worth.

<Steps>

### Open Links

Select **Growth → Links** in the sidebar, then **New link**.

### Say where it goes

Give it a **Name** you'll recognise ("Diwali sale WhatsApp") and paste the page under **Where
should it go?**: a path like `/collections/glasspads` or the page's full address on your store.

### Pick a short name

**Short name** is the end of the link: `diwali` makes `yourstore.in/l/diwali`. It's filled in
from the name; lower-case letters, numbers and hyphens only.

### Pick where you'll share it

Choose **Instagram bio**, **Instagram story**, **WhatsApp broadcast**, **Facebook post**,
**Google ads** or **Influencer** to fill in **Source** and **Medium**, and add a **Campaign**
(like `diwali`) to group several links under one campaign in the Growth overview.

### Create and share

Select **Create link**, then **⋯ → Copy link** and paste it wherever you picked.

</Steps>

Each click from a real browser is counted per day. Link previews (WhatsApp, Facebook, Slack
and others fetch the link once to draw the card) and bots are not. A shopper who clicks is
credited to the link for 30 days, so an order on a later visit still counts. Deleting a link
stops it working; the orders it already brought stay credited.
