# Growth

**Growth** (previously called Marketing) answers one question: where are your sales coming
from? Instagram, Google, WhatsApp, AI assistants like ChatGPT, or shoppers who typed your
address straight into their browser.

## What this is for

- Seeing which of your posts, ads or broadcasts actually turn into sales, not just clicks.
- Building a tagged link for your Instagram bio, a WhatsApp broadcast or an influencer, so that
  traffic shows up under the right source instead of "Direct" or "Other".
- Sending email and WhatsApp campaigns, and setting up automations that run on their own
  (abandoned-checkout reminders, win-back messages).

## What marketing earned

The top of the Growth overview says what your own marketing brought in for the dates you pick:
"Campaigns, automations, discounts and links brought in ₹92,500 in the last 30 days".

- **Sales from growth**: orders that came from one of your campaigns or automations, used a
  discount code, or came through one of your [short links](#links-short-links-you-can-track),
  with the change against the period before. Cancelled orders and drafts don't count.
- **Messages sent** (WhatsApp and email), **Automation orders** and **Subscribers** (with how
  many joined this week).
- **What marketing earned** splits that money four ways (campaigns, automations, discount
  codes, links), with each one's orders, its share and the top performer, like "Top: DIWALI10
  · ₹15,000". One order can come from a campaign email *and* a code; it shows under both, but
  the total counts it once.

## Sales by source

The Growth overview's main table groups every session and order into a source a shopper would
recognise: **Instagram**, **Facebook**, **YouTube**, **Google**, **WhatsApp**, **AI assistants**
(ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and similar), **Email**, **Direct**, or **Other**. Each row shows
sessions, orders, sales and a share-of-sales bar; select a row to see the campaigns and tagged
links that made it up. A callout above the table calls out your best-performing source for the
selected date range.

Prefer the older channel groupings (organic search, paid social, and so on)? Switch the **By
source / By channel** toggle above the table.

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | Why it happens | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everything shows as Direct | Links you shared were not tagged. | Build tagged links on the **Links** page and re-share them. |
| A social platform shows as Other | The visit's UTM tags or referrer did not match a known platform. | Use the **Links** page instead of pasting a bare URL. |
| No sessions at all | The store is new, or tracking was switched on recently. | Sessions are only counted from when tracking started; give it a few days. |
| An AI assistant's traffic looks small | Assistants often summarise your page without the shopper clicking through, or block the referrer. | Treat this as a floor, not the full picture; tagged links you post yourself are still the most reliable count. |

## FAQ

<Faq>

<FaqItem question="What happened to Marketing?">

It's the same section, renamed to Growth and reorganised around sources instead of only
channels and campaigns. Old links to **/marketing** still work and redirect here.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does this call out to Instagram, Google or WhatsApp?">

No. Growth only reads UTM tags on your own links and the referring site your shopper's browser
sent, both from your own storefront's visits. It never contacts any of these companies.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I save my tagged links for later?">

Yes. Every link you make in **Growth → Links** stays there with its clicks, orders and revenue,
and you can edit or delete it from its **⋯** menu.

</FaqItem>

</Faq>
