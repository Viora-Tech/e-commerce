# Growth

**Growth** (previously called Marketing) answers one question: where are your sales coming
from? Instagram, Google, WhatsApp, AI assistants like ChatGPT, or shoppers who typed your
address straight into their browser. It is laid out like Shopify's Growth section:

- **Growth** (this page): the overview.
- **[Attribution](/merchant-guide/growth/attribution)**: the full report by source, channel
  and campaign.
- **[Autopilot](/merchant-guide/growth/autopilot)**: campaigns planned for you, sent only
  after you approve them.
- **[Campaigns](/merchant-guide/growth/campaigns)**: your email and WhatsApp campaigns, and the
  **Tracking links** tab for [short links and QR codes](/merchant-guide/growth/links).

Marketing automations (abandoned-checkout reminders, welcome and win-back messages) are moving
to their own Automations app. Until it arrives, open them from **Marketing automations** on the
Campaigns page or in **Recent campaigns**, or search for them with ⌘K.

## The overview, top to bottom

- **Campaign Autopilot** banner: **Get started** opens [Autopilot](/merchant-guide/growth/autopilot).
  Once it is on, the banner says how many proposed campaigns wait for you.
- **Performance**, for the dates you pick (Today, 7 days, 30 days, or a custom range).
  **View details** opens [Attribution](/merchant-guide/growth/attribution) for the same dates.
  - **Sales attributed to marketing**: what your marketing sold, out of your total store sales,
    with the change against the period before. The solid line is this period, the dotted line
    the period before it.
  - **Sessions by traffic type**: **Direct** (typed in or bookmarked), **Paid** (ads),
    **Organic** (search, social, email, WhatsApp, AI assistants, referrals) and **Unknown**,
    with one bar showing the mix.
  - Your **top four sources**, like Google, Instagram, Direct or AI assistants: sessions, the
    value of the orders they brought, how many orders and the conversion rate.
- **Recommendations**: up to three next steps worked out from your own numbers.
- **Campaign Autopilot**: whether it is on, and a button to open it.
- **Recent campaigns**: your latest campaigns with when they were created and changed, and
  their sessions and sales this year (YTD, since 1 January). **View all** opens Campaigns.

Labels with a dotted underline explain themselves: point at one (or tab to it) to read what it
counts.

<Callout type="info">
  A source only shows up once a shopper's visit or order carries that source. Untagged links
  read as **Direct**, even if you shared them on social media. Use **Tracking links** to tag
  anything you share.
</Callout>

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | Why it happens | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everything shows as Direct | Links you shared were not tagged. | Build tagged links in **Campaigns → Tracking links** and re-share them. |
| A social platform shows as Other | The visit's UTM tags or referrer did not match a known platform. | Use a tracking link instead of pasting a bare URL. |
| No sessions at all | The store is new, or tracking was switched on recently. | Sessions are only counted from when tracking started; give it a few days. |
| An AI assistant's traffic looks small | Assistants often summarise your page without the shopper clicking through, or block the referrer. | Treat this as a floor, not the full picture; tagged links you post yourself are still the most reliable count. |

## FAQ

<Faq>

<FaqItem question="Where did Links and Automations go?">

Links are now the **Tracking links** tab of **Campaigns** (old `/growth/links` addresses still
work). Automations are moving to their own app; until then, **Marketing automations** on the
Campaigns page opens them.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What happened to Marketing?">

It's the same section, renamed to Growth and reorganised around sources instead of only
channels and campaigns. Old links to **/marketing** still work and redirect here.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Does this call out to Instagram, Google or WhatsApp?">

No. Growth only reads UTM tags on your own links and the referring site your shopper's browser
sent, both from your own storefront's visits. It never contacts any of these companies.

</FaqItem>

</Faq>
