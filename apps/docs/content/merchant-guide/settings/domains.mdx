# Domains and security certificates

Your store answers on its own address (for example `yourshop.vioratech.in`) and, if you like, on
a domain you bought (like `yourshop.in`). Shoppers see a padlock only when the address has a
**security certificate**. We get a free one for every address automatically.

Open **Settings → Domains** to see every address and its status.

## What the status means

| Status | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The address works and has its certificate. | Nothing. |
| **Issuing SSL** | We are getting the certificate. Usually a minute or two. | Wait. The page refreshes by itself. |
| **Waiting for DNS** | The address does not point at our server yet, so no certificate can be issued. | Add or fix the DNS record at the company where you bought the domain (see the records on the domain's page). We keep checking on our own. |
| **Certificate failed** | The address points at us, but the certificate was not issued yet. | Press **Retry certificate**. We also keep trying on our own for three days. |

The reason is written under the address, for example **DNS not pointing here yet** or
**Certificate pending**.

## Retry certificate

On a domain's page, **Retry certificate** starts a fresh attempt straight away. It takes about a
minute. You can press it once every two minutes. It is for owners and admins.

You usually do not need it: after you fix DNS we notice within minutes and retry for you, first
after a few minutes, then after 15 minutes, an hour, and then every six hours for three days.

## If it keeps failing

- Check the DNS records on the domain's page against what your registrar shows. A typo is the
  most common cause.
- If you use Cloudflare, turn the orange cloud off (DNS only) while the certificate is issued.
- Changes at some registrars take up to 48 hours to spread. The status updates once they do.
- Still stuck after a day? Contact support with the address.
