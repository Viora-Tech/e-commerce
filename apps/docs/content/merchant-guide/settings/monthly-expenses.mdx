# Monthly expenses

Order profit leaves out what it costs to run the shop. Add those costs here and the
[Profit](/merchant-guide/analytics/profit) report shows **net profit** for each month: the month's
order profit less these expenses.

## Adding an expense

Select **Add expense** and fill in:

- **Name**, like *Shop rent* or *Packer salary*.
- **Category**: staff and salaries, rent and office, apps and website, marketing, electricity
  internet and phone, accounting and other services, or other.
- **Amount per month**. If you are GST-registered, type it **before GST** (you claim the GST
  back); if not, type what you actually pay.
- **First month** it counts in.
- **Repeats every month**: on for rent and salaries. Turn it off for a one-off cost (it counts in
  its first month only). A **last month** is optional: leave it empty while you keep paying.

## Changing an expense

Select the pencil and pick **Change from**: the month the new amount starts. Months before it keep
the old amount, so past profit never changes. The list then shows both: the old amount up to the
month before, and the new one from your chosen month.

To stop paying something, edit it and set its **last month**. **Delete** is for an expense entered
by mistake: it stops counting in every month, past ones too.

## The month in progress

A month that is not over yet counts only the days gone. On the 10th of a 30-day month, ₹30,000 of
rent counts as ₹10,000 "so far", the same share of the month as the orders so far. The top of the
page shows what this month carries so far.

## Try it

1. Open **Settings → Monthly expenses** and select **Add expense**.
2. Add your rent from the month you started, repeating every month.
3. Open **Analytics → Reports → Profit by month**: the **Monthly expenses** and **Net profit**
   columns now use it.
