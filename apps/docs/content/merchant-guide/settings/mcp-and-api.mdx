# MCP & API

**Settings → MCP & API** lets AI tools work with your store. You can ask Claude about yesterday's
orders, or let Cursor read your products while it builds a theme. Each tool signs in with your
own account, so it can never do more than your role allows. Every change it suggests waits for
your approval.

The page has three tabs: **Overview**, **Clients & tokens** and **Activity log**.

## Connect an AI tool

1. Open **Settings → MCP & API**.
2. Under **Connect a client**, pick **Claude**, **ChatGPT**, **Cursor** or **VS Code**.
3. Follow the 3 steps:
   - **Copy the server URL.** Every store has its own address.
   - **Add it in the tool.**
     - **Claude:** Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
     - **ChatGPT:** Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings → Developer mode → Create.
     - **Cursor and VS Code:** click **Add to Cursor** or **Add to VS Code**.
   - **Sign in.** The tool opens a sign-in page. Sign in with your account, then choose what the
     tool can use: products, orders, customers, inventory, discounts, content and analytics, each
     as **Read** or **Read & write**. Click **Allow**.

The tool now shows as **Connected**. If you belong to more than one store, connect each store
separately; each one has its own address.

## What AI clients can do

Three switches on the Overview tab apply to every connected tool. Click **Save** after changing
them.

| Switch | On | Off |
|---|---|---|
| **Allow AI clients to make changes** | Tools can suggest edits to products, discounts and content | Tools can only read |
| **Changes need approval** (recommended) | Every change waits in AI approvals until you approve it | Changes happen as soon as the tool asks |
| **Let clients read customer details** | Tools can see names, phone numbers and addresses | Off by default; orders still show the city |

### Approving a change

When a tool suggests a change, the Activity log shows a row like "Claude proposed: …, waiting
approval".

1. Click **Open approval** on that row.
2. You see the same preview the AI bar shows: what changes, before and after.
3. Click **Approve**, **Edit** or **Cancel**.

Waiting changes also appear in **Settings → AI → Activity** with a **Review** button. Only the
person who connected the tool can approve its changes.

## Access tokens (for scripts)

Tokens are for scripts and servers, such as a script that syncs stock from your warehouse
software. People should connect a client instead, so their own staff permissions apply.

1. Go to **Clients & tokens** and click **Create token**.
2. Give it a name, tick only the areas it needs, and pick when it expires: 30 days, 90 days,
   1 year or never.
3. Click **Create token**. Copy the token **now**: it is shown only once. Keep it in your
   server's secret settings, never in code.

A token acts as you, limited to the areas you ticked. Unlike AI clients, a token's changes
happen at once and do not wait for approval. A token can never change settings, staff, billing
or money (no refunds or cancellations).

## Revoke a client or token

On **Clients & tokens**, open the **⋮** menu on a row and click **Revoke**. It stops working
right away. Changes already waiting in AI approvals stay there.

Revoke anything you don't recognise, and anything belonging to someone who left your team.

## Activity log

The **Activity log** tab shows everything clients and tokens did in the last 90 days, newest
first, grouped by day:

- **Reads:** a tool read your data, such as "Claude read sales".
- **Changes:** something changed.
- **Waiting approval:** a suggested change you haven't approved yet.
- **Blocked:** a switch or a token's areas stopped something.

Each tool and token can make up to 120 requests a minute, so one tool can't slow your store.
Click **Export CSV** to download the whole log.
