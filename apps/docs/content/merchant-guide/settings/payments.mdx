# Payments

Connecting a payment company, choosing how shoppers pay and the ₹1 test order are covered in
[Payments](/merchant-guide/money-and-gst/payments). This page covers the two switches on each
payment company's card in **Settings → Payments**: **Test / Live** and **Active / Inactive**.
They work the same for Razorpay, Cashfree and Decentro (UPI).

## Test keys and live keys

Every payment company gives you two sets of keys: **test** keys, where nothing real is charged,
and **live** keys, which take real money. Your shop keeps both. On the Connect page, pick
**Test** or **Live** under **Which keys?** to see and edit that set; saving one never touches
the other.

The **Keys in use** switch on the card picks which set checkout uses:

- **Test** — shoppers can go through checkout, but no real money moves.
- **Live** — real payments from real shoppers.

Switching never deletes keys, so you can go back and forth. Before switching to **Live**, your
shop checks the live keys with the payment company (nothing is charged) and asks you to
confirm. If you have not saved live keys yet, press **Add live keys** first.

If you connected before there were two sets, nothing changes: your keys are already in the right
place (Razorpay keys starting `rzp_test_` are test keys, `rzp_live_` are live keys).

## While you are in Test mode

- Settings → Payments shows an orange **Test mode — payments are not real** bar, and a small
  banner shows at the top of every admin page.
- Shoppers see a **Test mode** badge next to that way to pay at checkout.
- Orders paid in Test mode are marked **Test** in your orders list and on the order page. They
  are left out of your analytics, sales reports and GST invoices.
- When you are done testing, open **Orders** and use **Delete test orders**. It only ever
  deletes orders marked Test; real orders are never touched.

## Turning a payment company off

The **Active** switch on the card takes a payment company out of checkout straight away, without
disconnecting it. Its keys and settings stay saved; turn it back on any time and it returns with
what it had on (for Razorpay, its UPI QR too).

If it is your only online way to get paid, you are warned first: shoppers will then only see
cash on delivery or bank transfer, if you have those on.
