# Payments

Connecting a payment company, choosing how shoppers pay and the ₹1 test order are covered in
[Payments](/merchant-guide/money-and-gst/payments). This page covers the two switches on each
payment company's card in **Settings → Payments**: **Test / Live** and **Active / Inactive**.
They work the same for Razorpay, Cashfree and Decentro (UPI).

## Test keys and live keys

Every payment company gives you two sets of keys: **test** keys, where nothing real is charged,
and **live** keys, which take real money. Your shop keeps both. On the Connect page, pick
**Test** or **Live** under **Which keys?** to see and edit that set; saving one never touches
the other.

The **Keys in use** switch on the card picks which set checkout uses:

- **Test** — shoppers can go through checkout, but no real money moves.
- **Live** — real payments from real shoppers.

Switching never deletes keys, so you can go back and forth. Before switching to **Live**, your
shop checks the live keys with the payment company (nothing is charged) and asks you to
confirm. If you have not saved live keys yet, press **Add live keys** first.

If you connected before there were two sets, nothing changes: your keys are already in the right
place (Razorpay keys starting `rzp_test_` are test keys, `rzp_live_` are live keys).

## While you are in Test mode

- Settings → Payments shows an orange **Test mode — payments are not real** bar, and a small
  banner shows at the top of every admin page.
- Shoppers see a **Test mode** badge next to that way to pay at checkout.
- Orders paid in Test mode are marked **Test** in your orders list and on the order page. They
  are left out of your analytics, sales reports and GST invoices.
- When you are done testing, open **Orders** and use **Delete test orders**. It only ever
  deletes orders marked Test; real orders are never touched.

## Turning a payment company off

The **Active** switch on the card takes a payment company out of checkout straight away, without
disconnecting it. Its keys and settings stay saved; turn it back on any time and it returns with
what it had on (for Razorpay, its UPI QR too).

If it is your only online way to get paid, you are warned first: shoppers will then only see
cash on delivery or bank transfer, if you have those on.

## Several payment companies at once

Once two payment companies are connected (say Cashfree and Razorpay), **Settings → Payments**
shows two more cards:

- **Active providers** lists every connected payment company with an on/off switch. Drag them
  into order: the first one that is on is your main way to get paid. If a payment company cannot
  open a payment for a shopper (it is down, or does not answer within about 15 seconds), checkout
  quietly tries the next one that is on and takes the same way to pay. The shopper does not have
  to do anything, and each try is kept on the order's payment history with the company it used.
- **Route methods** (shown when two or more are on) picks which payment company takes each way
  to pay, for example **UPI → Cashfree** and **Cards → Razorpay**. A company that cannot take a
  way to pay is not offered for it. Anything you leave alone goes to the first company that is on.

At checkout, each company that is on shows as its own option with the ways to pay routed to it.
If you only ever connect one payment company, nothing changes: it takes every way to pay.

Turning a company off with its **Active** switch also takes it out of this order; turning it
back on puts it at the end.

## Manual payment methods

The **Manual payment methods** card holds the ways shoppers pay you directly:

- **UPI ID** — the shopper pays your own UPI ID (or scans its QR code).
- **Bank transfer / deposit** — the shopper sends NEFT, IMPS or RTGS to your account. Your bank
  name, account number and IFSC are shown on the thank-you page and in the order email.
- **Cash on delivery** — the shopper pays the courier. **COD rules** opens its settings
  (Settings → Checkout).

Press **Set up** or **Edit** on UPI ID or Bank transfer to fill in the details, write
instructions for the shopper (for example "Add your order number as the payment note"), and add
an optional fee or discount for paying this way. Each row has its own switch.

Orders paid by UPI ID or bank transfer wait as **Payment pending**. When the money reaches your
account, open the order and use **Mark as paid** (you can note the UTR and the date). Unpaid
transfer orders can be cancelled automatically from **Advanced → Unpaid orders**.

## Payment rules

**Payment rules** hide a way to pay for some orders, the way Shopify's payment customizations
do. Cash on delivery is where most returned-to-origin (RTO) losses come from, so this is the
usual use: no cash on delivery on big orders, in PIN codes with many refused parcels, or for
fragile products.

Press **Add rule**, then:

- **Hide these ways to pay**: tick cash on delivery, UPI or bank transfer to you, or a payment
  company.
- **When**: order total above an amount, order total below an amount, the delivery PIN code is
  in a list, or the cart has any of some products. The total is the items after discounts plus
  shipping. Choose whether **any** condition or **all** of them must be true.

The checkout hides the way to pay as soon as the rule applies (when the shopper types the PIN
code, or the total changes), and an order that tries it anyway is refused. Switch a rule off
without deleting it with **Rule is on**.

## Payment capture

By default the money is taken when the shopper pays (**Automatic**). With **Manual**, Razorpay
only holds the amount on the shopper's card or account; the order shows **Authorised** and a
**Capture payment** button. Capture once you have checked the stock or called the shopper. If
you cancel the order instead, the money is never taken: Razorpay releases a held payment it is not
told to capture within 5 days, so deal with these orders quickly.

Manual capture is available for Razorpay. Cashfree's pre-authorisation needs Cashfree to turn
it on for your account and covers cards only, so it shows as not available.
