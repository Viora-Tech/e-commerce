# AI features

Your store comes with two AI helpers. They are included in your plan, you do not need an
account anywhere else, and nothing changes in your store until you say so.

## Write with AI (product page)

Open any product. On the **Basic details** card, click **Write with AI**.

1. Pick a **tone**: Friendly, Premium, Playful, Technical or Simple.
2. Pick a **language**: English or Hindi.
3. Optional: add **notes**, for example the material, the size, who it is for or what is in the
   box. The photos cannot show everything, so notes help.
4. Click **Write it**. In a few seconds you see a suggestion next to what the product has now:
   - **Description**: an opening paragraph, short highlights and a closing line
   - **Key specs**: only what it could read in your photos or notes (added at the end of the
     description)
   - **Search title** and **Search description**: what Google shows
   - **Tags**: new tags are added to yours, nothing is removed
5. Tick the parts you like and click **Apply to product**. They go into the form, and the bar
   at the bottom asks you to **Save**. Nothing is saved until you click Save.

Tips:

- Add good photos first. It reads up to three photos: labels, ports, materials and colours.
- Not happy? Click **Change** to pick another tone or language, then **Write again**.
- Always read it before saving. AI can make mistakes, and it is told never to make up specs,
  prices or warranties.

## Ask your store (Home)

At the top of **Home**, type a question or tap one of the suggestions:

- "How were sales this week?"
- "What's running out?"
- "Orders waiting over 2 days?"
- "Top products this month?"
- "Abandoned checkouts this week?"

The answer appears as it is written. Every number comes from your own store data. Click
**Show the data used** under an answer to see exactly which numbers it was built from. You can
ask a follow-up, such as "and the week before?". **Clear** starts again.

It answers in Hindi when your admin is in Hindi (**Settings → Account**). It can only read your
data, never change it: ask it to cancel an order and it tells you where to do that.

## Settings → AI

- **This month**: how many AI requests your store used and how much the AI read and wrote,
  against your plan's monthly allowance. The allowance starts again on the 1st of each month.
- **AI features**: turn **Write with AI** or **Ask your store** off to hide the button for
  everyone in your store.
- **Your data**: what is and is not sent to the AI.

If you use up the month's allowance, the AI buttons say so and start working again on the 1st.
Doing many AI requests within a few minutes also pauses for a short while.

## Who can use it

- **Write with AI**: anyone whose role can edit products (Owner, Administrator).
- **Ask your store**: anyone who can see analytics (Owner, Administrator, Staff).
- **Settings → AI**: anyone who can see settings; only those who can change settings can turn
  features on or off.

## Your data

- Customer names, phone numbers, emails and addresses are never sent to the AI.
- For **Write with AI** it sends the product's name, type, brand, tags, description, your notes
  and up to three product photos.
- For **Ask your store** it sends your question and store totals such as sales, order numbers,
  stock counts and product names.
- Questions and answers are not saved. We only count how much AI your store used.
