# AI features

Your store comes with AI helpers. They are included in your plan, you do not need an account
anywhere else, and nothing changes in your store until you say so.

## AI assistant (every page)

The AI bar at the bottom of every page (**Ctrl+J**, or **⌘J** on a Mac) answers questions from
your own data and prepares changes you approve: discounts, price changes, collections, product
text and reply drafts. See [AI assistant](../ai-assistant) for everything it can do.

On **Home**, the **Ask AI** card shows a few things to ask and to get done; each one opens the AI
bar.

## Write with AI (product page)

Open any product. On the **Basic details** card, click **Write with AI**.

1. Pick a **tone**: Friendly, Premium, Playful, Technical or Simple.
2. Pick a **language**: English or Hindi.
3. Optional: add **notes**, for example the material, the size, who it is for or what is in the
   box. The photos cannot show everything, so notes help.
4. Click **Write it**. In a few seconds you see a suggestion next to what the product has now:
   - **Description**: an opening paragraph, short highlights and a closing line
   - **Key specs**: only what it could read in your photos or notes (added at the end of the
     description)
   - **Search title** and **Search description**: what Google shows, built around the product's
     [search keywords](../products/search-keywords) when it has some
   - **Tags**: new tags are added to yours, nothing is removed
5. Tick the parts you like and click **Apply to product**. They go into the form, and the bar
   at the bottom asks you to **Save**. Nothing is saved until you click Save.

Tips:

- Add good photos first. It reads up to three photos: labels, ports, materials and colours.
- Not happy? Click **Change** to pick another tone or language, then **Write again**.
- Always read it before saving. AI can make mistakes, and it is told never to make up specs,
  prices or warranties.

## Write with AI for many products (products list)

Tick products on **Products**, click **Write with AI**, choose what to write and start. It writes
in the background; review and **Apply** when it is done. Details in
[AI assistant](../ai-assistant#write-many-products-at-once).

## Settings → AI

- **This month**: the **AI credits** your store used against your plan's monthly allowance
  (Starter 500, Growth 3,000, Pro 15,000). Each answer or write uses a few credits, depending
  on how much the AI reads and writes. The allowance starts again on the 1st of each month.
- **Buy 1,000 credits (₹199)**: opens a secure payment page. Bought credits are added as soon as
  the payment goes through. They are used after the month's allowance and never expire.
- **AI features**: turn **AI assistant**, **Write with AI** or **Write with AI for many
  products** off to hide it for everyone in your store.
- **Writing quality**: **Standard** or **Best**. Best uses a stronger AI for product writing;
  each write then uses more credits. Questions and changes in the AI bar always use the fast
  AI.
- **Your data**: what is and is not sent to the AI.
- **Activity**: every change the AI made, who approved it and when, and **Undo** where the change
  can be undone.

If you use up the month's credits and have none bought, the AI says so and starts working again
on the 1st, or as soon as you buy more. Doing
many AI requests within a few minutes also pauses for a short while.

## Who can use it

- **AI assistant**: anyone; it only offers what their role allows (a staff member who cannot
  edit discounts never sees a discount card), and Approve checks their role again.
- **Write with AI** (one or many products): anyone whose role can edit products (Owner,
  Administrator).
- **Settings → AI**: anyone who can see settings; only those who can change settings can turn
  features on or off or change writing quality.

## Your data

- Customer phone numbers, emails and addresses are never sent to the AI. To draft a reply to an
  order, it sees only the customer's first name, and it says so under the answer.
- Text in your products and orders is treated as data, never as instructions to the AI.
- For **Write with AI** it sends the product's name, type, brand, tags, description, search
  keywords, your notes and up to three product photos.
- For **Suggest keywords** it sends the product's name, type, brand, category, description and
  price, its current keywords and, when Google Search Console is connected, the searches that
  brought visitors to the product.
- For the **AI assistant** it sends your question, the page you are on, and the store facts its
  tools look up, such as sales, order numbers, stock counts and product names.
- Conversations are not saved on our servers. We only count how much AI your store used.
