# Costs for profit

These feed the [Profit](/merchant-guide/analytics/profit) report and the **Profit** card on each
order.

## Actual fees come first

Razorpay and Cashfree tell us what they kept from each payment. That **actual fee** is what
profit uses. Razorpay usually sends it with the payment; Cashfree sends it a little later, once it
has worked out its charges. Until the fee arrives, profit uses the rates on this page, and the
order's Profit card says **Estimated**. When it arrives, the card says **Actual**.

So these rates matter most for brand-new orders, and for gateways that never report a fee.

## Fees by how the customer paid

Gateways charge different rates for UPI, debit cards, credit cards, international cards and so
on, so you set a rate for each:

| Way of paying | Filled in with |
| --- | --- |
| UPI | 2% |
| RuPay debit cards | 2% |
| Other debit cards | 2% |
| Credit cards | 2% |
| International cards | 3% |
| Corporate, Amex and Diners cards | 2.15% |
| Net banking | 2% |
| Wallets | 2% |
| EMI | 2% |
| Pay later | 2% |

These are **Razorpay's standard rates** (razorpay.com/pricing, checked 11 October 2026: 2% on
every domestic way of paying, 2.15% on corporate and business cards, up to 3% on international
cards). They are only a starting point: check your own plan, your rates may be lower. Type each
rate **before GST**, the way gateways quote them. **Use standard rates** puts them back.

This section shows when Razorpay or Cashfree is switched on.

## Fees by gateway

One rate per gateway. It is used when the gateway did not say how the customer paid, and for
gateways like Decentro that use one rate for everything.

## Cash on delivery

What your courier keeps for collecting cash from the customer, before GST. It shows when cash on
delivery is switched on.

## Delivery estimate

Until the courier's charge for a parcel is known, profit counts a cautious estimate: your highest
charge in the last 60 days for the same courier and state. With nothing on record, it counts the
**Default delivery cost per parcel** set here (₹100 until you change it). If you are
GST-registered type it before GST, otherwise what you actually pay. The order's Profit card says
*estimated* until the real charge arrives.

Packing is set per box in **Settings → Shipping** (each box's **Cost**).

## GST on fees

Gateways and couriers add 18% GST to their fees.

- If you have a **GST registration** (not composition), you claim that GST back, so profit leaves
  it out, the same way it leaves GST out of your sales.
- If you do not, the GST is a real cost, so profit adds 18% to each rate.

The page tells you which one applies to your store.

## Cashfree: turn on the fee update

Cashfree sends its fee in a separate update. In the Cashfree Merchant Dashboard's webhook
settings, subscribe the same address as your payment updates to **Success Payment TDR**.
Without it the fee still arrives, just later: we ask Cashfree for it every 30 minutes until it is
known.

## Try it

1. Open **Settings → Costs for profit**.
2. Compare the rates with your Razorpay or Cashfree plan and change any that differ. Leave a rate empty if you never take that way of paying.
3. Select **Save**.
4. Open a recent order paid online. The **Profit** card shows **Payment fees · Actual** when the
   gateway reported the fee, or **Estimated** while it uses these rates.
