# Customer accounts

**Settings → Customer accounts** decides how customers sign in to your store and whether they
need an account to buy. Every way to sign in is a switch. Customers only ever see the ways you
switched on.

## At checkout

- **Anyone can check out** (recommended): guests buy without an account. Customers can still
  sign in to see their orders and saved addresses.
- **Customers must sign in first**: every buyer signs in before paying.

## Ways to sign in with a code

Customers never use a password. They type their email or mobile number and get a six digit
code that works for ten minutes.

| Way | What the customer gets | What you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email code | A code in their inbox | Nothing extra: the store's email sender, or your own email app |
| SMS code | A text message | An SMS app in Notifications |
| WhatsApp code | A WhatsApp message | The WhatsApp app, with an approved sign-in (authentication) template |

A way you cannot send yet shows **Needs an app** and cannot be switched on. If you turn off an
app later, customers simply stop seeing that way; the others keep working. Keep at least one
code way on: it is how everyone can always get in.

<Callout>
When both email and a mobile way are on, the sign-in page opens on the first one and shows a
link to the other ("Use your mobile number instead").
</Callout>

## Sign in with an account they already have

| Way | Good for | Setup |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google | Almost everyone | None, or your own Google app |
| Apple | iPhone customers | None, or your own Apple app |
| Facebook | Customers who live on Meta apps | None, or your own Facebook app |
| Truecaller | Android customers in India: one tap, no code | None, or your own Truecaller app |

Switch one on and it is ready: customers go through our secure sign-in page and come straight
back to your shop, signed in. There is nothing to set up.

**Use my own app** (optional) puts your shop's name on Google's or Apple's sign-in screen. Tick
it, press **Set up**, and follow the short steps: create the app with the provider, paste the
one address we show into it, and copy two or three values back. Secret values are stored
encrypted and only the last four characters are ever shown again.

<Callout>
Instagram does not offer sign-in for shoppers. Facebook covers Meta accounts.
</Callout>

**Google One Tap** shows "Continue as Asha" with the customer's Google account right on your
sign-in page. It needs your own Google app, with your shop's web address added under
*Authorised JavaScript origins*.

### No duplicate customers

A customer who signs in with Google using the same email they ordered with before stays the
same customer, with the same orders. We only match on an email or mobile number the provider
has checked. Facebook does not tell us that, so the first time someone signs in with Facebook
we ask them to confirm their email with a code.

Customers can connect or remove Google, Apple and Facebook later from **Profile** in their
account.

## More ways to sign in

- **Sign-in link by email**: customers can ask for a link instead of typing a code. It works
  once, for 15 minutes. Needs email codes on.
- **Passkeys**: after signing in once, customers can add a passkey from **Profile** and then
  sign in with their fingerprint, face or phone screen lock. A passkey works on the web address
  where it was added: if you move your shop to a new domain, customers sign in another way once
  and add a new passkey.
- **Remember this device**: customers see a tick box. Ticked, they stay signed in for 90 days.
  Unticked (a shared phone or computer), they are signed out when the browser closes. With this
  off, everyone stays signed in for 30 days.

## Deleting an account

Customers can delete their own account from **Your data** in their account:

1. They read what happens and press **Send me a code to confirm**.
2. They type the code we send to their email (or mobile, for accounts without an email).
3. They are signed out everywhere, and a deletion request appears in
   **Settings → Customer privacy → Data requests** for you to finish.

Deleting the account also removes their connected Google, Apple, Facebook and Truecaller
sign-ins and their passkeys. See [Customer privacy](/merchant-guide/settings/customer-privacy)
for how to answer the request.
