# Plan & billing

**Settings → Plan & billing** shows your plan, what your store used this month, and your
invoices.

## Usage

The **Current plan** card shows this month's orders, staff accounts, AI requests and file
storage against your plan's limits. Usage starts again on the 1st of each month.

**Orders are never blocked.** If you go over your plan's orders, we ask you to move to a bigger
plan. On Pro, ask us about **Own it** (below).

## Choose or change a plan

1. Click **Compare plans**.
2. Pick **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then **Choose** a plan.
3. You go to a secure payment page. Pay with UPI, card or netbanking. Your plan starts as soon as
   the payment goes through.

**Moving to a smaller plan** waits until your current paid period ends; until then everything
stays as it is. You can click **Keep current plan** any time before that date.

## Renewals and unpaid bills

We create your next bill 3 days before your period ends, with a **Pay now** link. If it isn't
paid by the end date, the plan shows **Payment due**. After 7 more days it is **Paused**, and a
banner asks you to pay. Paying turns everything back on.

We email the pay link to your store's contact email when a renewal is billed, and once more if
it becomes overdue.

### UPI AutoPay

Under **Payment method**, click **Turn on UPI AutoPay** and approve the mandate in your UPI app
(once). From then on each renewal is charged automatically, and the GST invoice is emailed as
usual. If a charge fails, the plan goes back to pay links and the page says so. Click
**Turn off AutoPay** any time.

## Invoices

Every bill is a GST invoice. The **Invoices** card lists them with their status: **Due**,
**Paid** or **Cancelled**. Click **View** to open one, or **Pay** to pay a due bill. Invoices go
to your store's contact email (Settings → Store details).

## Paid apps

Some apps are included from a plan up, and on a smaller plan you can buy them by the month
(+ 18% GST):

| App | Included in | On a smaller plan |
|---|---|---|
| Reviews | Growth and Pro | ₹199 / month |
| Back in stock | Growth and Pro | ₹149 / month |
| Forms | Growth and Pro | ₹99 / month |
| EMI & pay later | Pro | ₹149 / month |
| Google Search Console | Pro | ₹199 / month |

Every other app (GST invoices, Shopify import, couriers, payment gateways, storage, email,
WhatsApp, Umami) is free on every plan.

To buy one:
1. Open the App Store and click **Install** on the app. The card shows its price.
2. Click **Buy for ₹… / month** and pay.

The app installs as soon as the payment goes through. It renews every month: we bill 3 days ahead,
and an unpaid renewal keeps the app working for 7 more days.

**Paid apps** on this page lists what you bought. Click **Stop renewing** to end one: it keeps
working until the paid month ends. If you move to a plan that includes the app, you don't need to
buy it any more.

## Own it

Want unlimited orders on your own server? **Own it** is a one-time licence (₹49,999) plus
₹3,000 a month for updates and support, with a 12-month minimum. Click **Talk to us** in Compare
plans.
