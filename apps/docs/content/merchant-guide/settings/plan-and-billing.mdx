## Usage

The **Current plan** card shows this month's orders, staff accounts, AI requests and file
storage against your plan's limits. Usage starts again on the 1st of each month.

**Staff and storage.** Your plan's staff accounts (you included) and file storage are real limits:
past them a new invite or a new upload is refused, with a note to free a seat or space, or move
up a plan. Nothing you already have is removed, and your storefront and checkout keep working.
Files in a bucket you connected yourself (Apps → storage) don't count.

**Orders are never blocked.** Past your plan's included orders, each order carries a small fee
(see below). When a bigger plan works out cheaper for you, **Compare plans** shows it.

The sidebar card warns you at 80% of your included orders, and from 100% it says what each
further order costs.

## The per-order fee

Each plan includes a number of orders a month. Every order after that carries your plan's fee,
a percentage of what the buyer paid (items after discounts, shipping and tax). **Scale has no
per-order fee at all.**

- **What counts:** every order placed on your store, cash on delivery included. A part refund
  still counts the whole order.
- **What doesn't:** cancelled orders, orders refunded in full before the bill is made, test
  orders, and orders placed during your free trial or while your plan is unpaid or paused.
- **Upgrading mid-month:** orders from that moment use the new plan's rate and included orders.
- **Your running total:** the **Current plan** card shows this month's orders with a fee and the
  estimated fee so far.

On the 1st you get one bill for last month's fees, by email with a **Pay** link and a GST
invoice. If last month's fees come to under ₹100, they wait and are added to the next month.

An unpaid fee bill never pauses your store. If it is still unpaid when your plan renews, it is
added to that renewal bill. **Per-order fee bills** on this page lists every month, with a
**CSV** of the orders it covered.

## Visits (fair use)

Each plan suits a number of storefront visits a month (table above). If your store goes past it
two months running, the page shows a notice and we get in touch to find the right plan with you.
Nothing is ever blocked, slowed or charged for visits.

## Choose or change a plan

1. Click **Compare plans**.
2. Pick **Monthly** or **Yearly**, then **Choose** a plan.
3. You go to a secure payment page. Pay with UPI, card or netbanking. Your plan starts as soon as
   the payment goes through.

**Moving to a smaller plan** waits until your current paid period ends; until then everything
stays as it is. You can click **Keep current plan** any time before that date.

## Renewals and unpaid bills

We create your next bill 3 days before your period ends, with a **Pay now** link. If it isn't
paid by the end date, the plan shows **Payment due**. After 7 more days it is **Paused**, and a
banner asks you to pay. Paying turns everything back on.

We email the pay link to your store's contact email when a renewal is billed, and once more if
it becomes overdue.

### UPI AutoPay

Under **Payment method**, click **Turn on UPI AutoPay** and approve the mandate in your UPI app
(once). From then on each renewal is charged automatically, and the GST invoice is emailed as
usual. If a charge fails, the plan goes back to pay links and the page says so. Click
**Turn off AutoPay** any time.

Per-order fee bills always come as a pay link, even with AutoPay on: AutoPay charges a fixed
amount, and the fee changes every month.

## Invoices

Every bill is a GST invoice. The **Invoices** card lists them with their status: **Due**,
**Paid** or **Cancelled**. Click **View** to open one, or **Pay** to pay a due bill. Invoices go
to your store's contact email (Settings → Store details).

## Paid apps

Some apps are included from a plan up, and on a smaller plan you can buy them by the month
(+ 18% GST):

| App | Included in | On a smaller plan |
|---|---|---|
| Reviews | Growth and up | ₹199 / month |
| Back in stock | Growth and up | ₹149 / month |
| Forms | Growth and up | ₹99 / month |
| EMI & pay later | Pro and Scale | ₹149 / month |
| Google Search Console | Pro and Scale | ₹199 / month |

Every other app (GST invoices, Shopify import, couriers, payment gateways, storage, email,
WhatsApp, Umami) is free on every plan.

To buy one:
1. Open the App Store website (**Apps → Browse App Store**) and click **Install** on the app's
   page. The page shows its price.
2. Click **Buy for ₹… / month** and pay.

The app installs as soon as the payment goes through. It renews every month: we bill 3 days ahead,
and an unpaid renewal keeps the app working for 7 more days.

**Paid apps** on this page lists what you bought. Click **Stop renewing** to end one: it keeps
working until the paid month ends. If you move to a plan that includes the app, you don't need to
buy it any more.

set it up on your server for ₹7,500. Click **Talk to us** in Compare plans.
