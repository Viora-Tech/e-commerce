# Staff and invites

**Settings → Staff** shows who can get into your store and what each person can do. It has two
tabs: **Members** (the people) and **Roles** (what each role may see and change). What the roles
mean is on [Security](/merchant-guide/money/security).

## Invite someone

1. Go to **Settings → Staff** and select **Invite member**.
2. Type their **Email address** and pick a **Role**.
3. Select **Send invite**.

They get an email with the subject "You're invited to manage *your store* on Vextro", saying who
invited them and with which role. They select **Accept invitation**, sign in with that same email
address, and they are in. An invite lasts **2 days**.

If the email could not be sent, you see **"Couldn't send the invite email"**. The invite is still
saved: it waits under **Pending invites**, where you can copy its link or try again.

## Pending invites

Under the team list, **Pending invites** shows everyone you invited who has not joined yet: their
email, the role they will get, who invited them, when it was sent and when it expires. Open the
menu (**⋯**) on a row to:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy invite link** | Copies their personal link so you can send it on WhatsApp or anywhere else. It only works for the invited email address. |
| **Resend email** | Sends the invite email again and gives them another 2 days. |
| **Cancel invite** | The link stops working straight away. |

Invites that ran out show **Expired** and are greyed. Select **Resend** to send a fresh invite
(it gets a new link, the old one stays dead), or **Cancel invite** to remove it.

When nobody is waiting, the list says "No pending invites".

## Who can do what

- Anyone whose role can view the team sees the pending list.
- Sending, resending and cancelling invites needs the staff **invite** permission. The Owner and
  Administrator roles have it; the Staff role does not.
- Nobody but the owner can invite someone to a role with more access than their own.

## Common problems

| Problem | Why | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| They never got the email | Spam folder, a typo, or the email could not be sent. | Check the address under **Pending invites**. Select **Resend email**, or **Copy invite link** and send it yourself. |
| "This invitation has expired" | More than 2 days passed. | Select **Resend** on the expired row. |
| "This invitation is for another email" | They signed in with a different address. | They sign out and sign in with the invited address, or you invite the address they use. |
