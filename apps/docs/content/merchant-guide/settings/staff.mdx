# Staff and invites

**Settings → Staff** shows who can get into your store and what each person can do. It has two
tabs: **Members** (the people) and **Roles** (what each role may see and change). What the roles
mean is on [Security](/merchant-guide/money-and-gst/security).

## Invite someone

1. Go to **Settings → Staff** and select **Invite member**.
2. Type their **Email address** and pick a **Role**.
3. Select **Send invite**.

They get an email with the subject "You're invited to manage *your store* on Koomerce", saying who
invited them and with which role. They select **Accept invitation**, sign in with that same email
address, and they are in. An invite lasts **2 days**.

If the email could not be sent, you see **"Couldn't send the invite email"**. The invite is still
saved: it waits under **Pending invites**, where you can copy its link or try again.

## Pending invites

Under the team list, **Pending invites** shows everyone you invited who has not joined yet: their
email, the role they will get, who invited them, when it was sent and when it expires. Open the
menu (**⋯**) on a row to:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy invite link** | Copies their personal link so you can send it on WhatsApp or anywhere else. It only works for the invited email address. |
| **Resend email** | Sends the invite email again and gives them another 2 days. |
| **Cancel invite** | The link stops working straight away. |

Invites that ran out show **Expired** and are greyed. Select **Resend** to send a fresh invite
(it gets a new link, the old one stays dead), or **Cancel invite** to remove it.

When nobody is waiting, the list says "No pending invites".

## Who can do what

- Anyone whose role can view the team sees the pending list.
- Sending, resending and cancelling invites needs the staff **invite** permission. The Owner and
  Administrator roles have it; the Staff role does not.
- Nobody but the owner can invite someone to a role with more access than their own.

## Off, Allowed and Ask first

Open a role in **Settings → Staff → Roles**. Its **Permissions** card has one control per
permission:

| Choice | What a member on this role gets |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** | They cannot do it. |
| **Allowed** | They can do it, straight away. |
| **Ask first** | They can ask: the change runs only after another member approves it in **Settings → Approvals**. |

With **Ask first**, a member who does that action, from the admin, the AI assistant, an AI app
(MCP) or an API token, does not change anything yet: the change waits in **Settings → Approvals**
and they see "Sent for approval". Viewing permissions (**Read**) and **Approvals** itself only have
**Off** and **Allowed**: looking, and working out a number such as a draft order's GST, never waits.

Only the owner can set **Ask first**. Built-in roles keep their permissions, but the owner can
still choose which of their changes ask first. **Save role** at the top saves everything at once.

New stores and new roles start with these on **Ask first**: refunding and cancelling orders, deleting
products and customers, discounts, payment gateways and payout keys (**Payments**), billing,
inviting staff and managing roles. Other settings are not ticked.

Who can approve a waiting change:

- The owner, always. The owner's own changes never wait.
- A different member whose role has the **Approvals** permission and can make that change, and
  whose own role has it on **Allowed** (not **Ask first**). The Administrator role has **Approvals**.
- Never the person who asked. They can only withdraw it.

**Settings → Approvals** lists what waits for you, with who asked, from where and when, with
**Reject** on the left and **Approve** on the right. Approving runs the change as you. A waiting
change expires after 7 days and is marked **Expired**; nothing changed. Under **Your requests**
you see what you sent and what became of it, and the Settings menu shows a count until you look.

A few things cannot wait, such as a browser sign-in to Razorpay, a file upload or creating an API
token: if your role needs approval for them, they are refused and the owner (or an approver) has
to do them.

## How many people

Your plan includes a number of staff accounts, you included: Starter 1, Growth 3 (also during
the trial), Pro 10, Scale 25. A pending invite holds a seat until it is accepted or cancelled.
When every seat is in use, a new invite is refused: cancel an invite, remove a member, or move up
a plan in **Settings → Plan & billing**. Members you already have are never removed.

## Common problems

| Problem | Why | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| They never got the email | Spam folder, a typo, or the email could not be sent. | Check the address under **Pending invites**. Select **Resend email**, or **Copy invite link** and send it yourself. |
| "This invitation has expired" | More than 2 days passed. | Select **Resend** on the expired row. |
| "This invitation is for another email" | They signed in with a different address. | They sign out and sign in with the invited address, or you invite the address they use. |
| They see an error page instead of a store page | Their role does not allow that page (the error says what is missing), or something went wrong. | Give them a role that allows it under **Roles**, or send us the message on the error page. |
