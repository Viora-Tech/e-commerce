# Approvals

## What this is for

Some changes are too important to happen on one person's click: a refund, cancelling an order,
deleting products, changing payment keys. **Settings → Approvals** is where those changes wait
until the owner, or another member allowed to approve, says yes. AI apps connected to your store
also send their changes here.

## How it works

- In **Settings → Staff**, each role sets every change permission to **Off**, **Allowed** or
  **Ask first**. With **Ask first**, the change runs only after another member approves it.
  Only the owner can change Ask first.
- The owner can do everything and never waits for approval.
- When someone with Ask first saves such a change, nothing happens yet. They see **Sent for
  approval** and the change waits here.
- Changes from an **AI app (MCP)** or an **API token** wait here too when the member they act for
  has Ask first. Some changes always wait when they come from an AI app: refunds, cancellations,
  deletes, payments, staff, billing, domains and MCP settings.
- Who can approve: the owner, or a member whose role can manage approvals, holds that permission
  and does not have Ask first on it. Nobody can approve their own request.
- Approving runs the change **as the approver, with their role**. It checks again that it still
  makes sense (for example, a product deleted in the meantime is refused).
- A request nobody approves within **7 days** expires and nothing changes.

Changes the AI suggests inside **Ask AI** are different: they show as a card in the chat that only
you approve, within 24 hours. See [Ask AI](/merchant-guide/home/ask-ai).

## Approve or reject

<Steps>

### Open Approvals

Go to **Settings → Approvals**. The sidebar shows a count when something is waiting.

### Read the request

**Waiting for you** lists changes from other members, and your own AI app's requests. Each
shows who asked, where it came from (**Admin**, **AI assistant**, **AI app (MCP)**, **API token**
or **Write with AI**), and exactly what will change.

### Decide

Select **Approve** to run the change as you, with your role, or **Reject** to throw it away.

</Steps>

## Follow your own requests

**Your requests** lists what you sent for approval in the last 30 days and what became of it:
approved, rejected, or **Expired: nobody approved it in time, so nothing changed**. You can
withdraw a request that is still waiting.

<Callout>
  Start with the defaults: new stores ask first for refunds, cancellations, product and customer
  deletes, discounts, payment settings, billing, and inviting or managing staff. Loosen a role
  once you trust it.
</Callout>

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| My staff member's change says "Sent for approval" | Their role has Ask first on that permission. Approve it here, or change the role in Settings → Staff |
| I cannot approve a request | You asked for it yourself, or your role does not hold that permission, or your role also has Ask first on it. Ask the owner |
| A request disappeared | It expired after 7 days, or the person withdrew it. See Your requests |
