# Business and GST

**Settings → Business and GST** holds your business (the name and PAN on your PAN card) and
your **GST numbers**. In India you get one GST number for each state where you have a shop or
warehouse. If you send orders from Haryana and from Delhi, you have two GST numbers, and both
belong to the same PAN.

## What this is for

- Keeping every GST number your business holds in one place.
- Choosing your **default** GST number: the one your shop uses today.
- Saying which GST number each warehouse bills under (see [Locations](/merchant-guide/settings/locations)).

## GST per state

Each order is billed from the GST number of the warehouse its products sell from (you choose
it per location on [Locations](/merchant-guide/settings/locations)). If that warehouse has no
GST number chosen, the order is billed from your default GST number.

Your prices already include GST, so this never changes what the shopper pays. It only changes
how the tax is split on the invoice:

| Warehouse in | Shopper in | Tax on the order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Haryana | Haryana | CGST + SGST |
| Haryana | Karnataka | IGST |
| Karnataka | Karnataka | CGST + SGST |

The order page shows which GST number it was billed from, for example **Billed from:
Haryana · 06AAPFU0939F1ZV**. Each GST number also numbers its own invoices and files its own
GSTR-1 (see [GST invoices](/merchant-guide/money/gst-invoices#more-than-one-gst-number)).

## How it works

- **Your business** shows your legal name and PAN. You do not type the PAN for your first GST
  number: we read it from the GST number itself (characters 3 to 12 of a GST number are the PAN).
- **GST numbers** lists each number with its state. We read the state from the first two
  digits, so you never pick it. The default one has a **Default** badge.
- **Add GST number** asks for the 15-character number from your GST certificate. As you type,
  the page shows "Registered in Haryana" (or your state). The other fields are optional: an
  invoice prefix such as `HR` (printed where your numbering uses `{GST}`), and the address on
  the certificate, which invoices from this GST number print as your address.
- If the number belongs to a different PAN, you see: *"This GST number belongs to a different
  PAN. Is it another business?"* Check for a typo first. If it really is another business, use
  **Add it as another business**.
- **Make default** (from the ⋮ menu) switches your default GST number. **Mark as cancelled** is
  only for a number the GST department has cancelled; old invoices keep showing it.

### More than one business

Open **Advanced: more than one business** only if a warehouse belongs to a different legal
business (a different PAN), for example a partner's stock you sell.

> Selling stock owned by another business makes you an e-commerce operator. Talk to your CA
> first.

### Settings → Taxes & GST

Before this page existed, your GST number lived on **Settings → Taxes & GST**. It still shows
there. Once you have a GST number here, Taxes & GST shows it read-only with a **Manage GST
numbers** button that brings you here. If you have no GST number yet, typing one on Taxes & GST
adds it here as your first.

## Try it

1. Open **Settings → Business and GST**.
2. Click **Add GST number**, type your GST number and check the state it shows.
3. Add a second one for another state, then use **⋮ → Make default** to switch.
4. Open **Settings → Locations** and pick which GST number each location bills under.

Only owners and administrators can change this page. Staff cannot open it.
