# Your account

**Your account** is about you, not your store. Everything here belongs to the person signed
in: your staff each have their own. Store-wide choices (payments, shipping, staff) stay in
**Settings**.

Open it from the **account menu**: click your photo or initials in the top right corner (or at
the bottom of the left menu) and choose **Account**. The pages are listed on the left:

| Page | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Your photo, your name and your mobile number |
| **Sign-in & security** | Change the email you sign in with, see if Google is linked, add passkeys, see recent sign-ins |
| **Sessions** | See every phone and computer signed in as you, and sign them out |
| **Notifications** | Choose which store events reach you by email or WhatsApp |
| **Stores** | See every store you work in, switch to one, or leave one |
| **Language & region** | English or हिंदी, 12-hour or 24-hour times, and the first day of the week |
| **Appearance** | Light or dark mode and your accent colour |

## Profile

### Your photo

1. Click **Upload photo** and pick a photo from your computer or phone (JPG, PNG or WebP).
2. A square appears. Drag the photo to move it and use the **Zoom** slider to make it bigger.
   The round shape shows exactly what your team will see.
3. Click **Use this photo**.

Your photo shows next to your name in the top bar, the left menu and the staff list. To go
back to your initials, click **Remove**.

Good to know: we shrink the photo to a small square and remove hidden details such as the
place it was taken.

### Your name and mobile number

Type your **Full name** and **Mobile number**, then click **Save** (or **Save** on the bar at
the bottom). For an Indian number, just type the 10 digits: we add +91 for you. The number is
used for your own WhatsApp alerts; customers never see it.

Your email is shown here too. It is the address you sign in with.

### Delete my account

At the bottom of **Profile**. It removes you from every store you work in and deletes your
sign-in, photo, passkeys and devices. The stores, their orders and their customers stay.

1. Click **Delete my account** and type your sign-in email.
2. Click **Send code**. We email you a 6-digit code.
3. Type the code and click **Delete my account**. You are signed out straight away.

If you **own** a store, the button is greyed out and tells you which store: a store always keeps
its owner. Hand the store to someone else first (Settings → Staff) or close it.

## Sign-in & security

### Change the email you sign in with

1. Next to your email, click **Change email**.
2. Type the new address and click **Send code**. We send a 6-digit code to the **new** address.
3. Type the code and click **Change email**. From now on you sign in with the new address.

Your old address gets an email saying the sign-in email was changed, so you notice if someone
else did it. Not getting the code? Check the address for typos; if that address already belongs
to another account, no code is sent.

### Sign in with Google

If the login page shows **Continue with Google**, you can use your Google account instead of
waiting for a code.

1. On the login page, click **Continue with Google**.
2. Pick the Google account that uses **the same email you sign in with**.
3. You are in. The first time, this links your Google account to your existing account: you
   keep the same stores, role and history. Nothing new is created.

New to the admin? Google works for sign-up too: you land on **Create your store**, exactly as
with a code by email. If sign-ups are invite-only, you see the same "invite-only" message.

Under **Sign-in & security**, the **Google** card shows **Linked** and the date once you have
signed in with Google. Good to know:

- Google only works when Google has confirmed the email address. If it has not, the login page
  says so; sign in with a code by email instead.
- A Google account with a **different** email never opens your account. Change your sign-in
  email first if you want to use another Google account.
- Signing in with a code by email always keeps working.

### Passkeys: sign in without a code

A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, your face or your phone's screen lock, so
you do not wait for an email.

1. Click **Add a passkey**, give it a name you will recognise (for example "Office laptop") and
   click **Add a passkey** again.
2. Your phone or computer asks for your fingerprint, face or PIN. That is it.

Next time, on the login page, click **Sign in with a passkey**. Add one on each device you use.
To rename or remove a passkey, click **⋯** next to it. Signing in with a code by email always
keeps working.

### Recent sign-ins

The last 10 times someone signed in to your account: the device and browser ("Chrome on
Windows"), roughly where (city, or a partly hidden internet address), how (code, Google or passkey) and
when. If one was not you, sign that device out on **Sessions** and change your email.

## Sessions

**Sessions** lists every phone and computer signed in as you. The one you are using says
**This device**.

- **Sign out** next to a device signs only that one out. Use it for a lost phone or a shop
  computer you no longer use.
- **Sign out everywhere else** signs out every device except this one.

Both ask you first. A device you sign out has to sign in again with a code or a passkey; a page
already open on it can keep working for up to 5 minutes.

## Notifications

Your own alerts for the store you are working in. Every person on the team picks their own, so
the owner can get every order while a packer gets nothing.

| Alert | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **New order** | An email (or WhatsApp) for each order as it comes in, with a button to open it |
| **Daily summary** | Once a day at the hour you pick: orders, sales and how many wait to be shipped. Before noon it covers yesterday; after noon, today so far |
| **Low stock**, **Payment or payout problem**, **App or integration error** | Marked *Soon*: your choice is saved now and starts sending in an update |
| **Your bug reports** | On the test server only: an email when a bug you reported is fixed, and again when the fix is live. Email only |

Switch each one on for **Email**, **WhatsApp** or both, then click **Save**. The store owner
gets new orders, payment problems and app errors by email until they change it; everyone else
starts with everything off, except **Your bug reports**, which is on by email for everyone (it is
only ever about a report you sent yourself).

- **WhatsApp** shows only when your store has WhatsApp set up in **Settings → Notifications**,
  and needs your mobile number on **Profile**.
- Alerts about orders only reach people whose role can see orders.
- The owner can switch team alerts off for everyone in **Settings → Notifications**, under
  *Messages you get*.

## My reports (test server)

On the test server, the account menu (your photo, top right) has **Report a bug** and **My
reports**. **My reports** lists every bug you reported from this store, newest first:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Received** | We have it and will look at it |
| **Being fixed** | Someone on our team is working on it |
| **Fixed – in the next update** | The fix is done; it reaches your admin with the next update |
| **Live** | The fix is in the admin you use now |
| **Won't fix** | We decided not to change this; the reply says why |

- Each report shows the page you were on, the screenshot (click it to see it full size) and our
  team's latest reply.
- A blue dot on your photo and next to **My reports** means a report has news. Opening **My
  reports** clears it, and the news shows as **New** on that report.
- When a report is fixed or goes live you also see it in the bell, and get an email if **Your
  bug reports** is on in **Notifications**.
- The store owner can switch to **All reports from this store** to see what the team reported.
  Everyone else sees only their own reports. Reports never show in another store.

## Stores

**Stores** lists every store you can open, with your role in each (for example *Owner* or
*Staff*) and the day you joined. The store you are working in now says **Current**.

- **Switch to** opens another store. You can also switch from the store name at the top of the
  left menu.
- **Leave** removes you from a store. You lose access straight away, and an owner or admin has
  to invite you again if you want to come back.

The owner of a store cannot leave it: a store always keeps its owner.

## Language & region

- **Admin language**: English or हिंदी (Hindi). See [Admin language](/merchant-guide/settings/language)
  for what changes.
- **Time format**: *12-hour* shows 4:05 pm, *24-hour* shows 16:05.
- **Week starts on**: Monday or Sunday, for day lists such as **Days you send parcels** in
  Settings → Shipping.

These choices are saved on this browser and change straight away. Times are always India time
(IST).

## Questions

**Can my staff see my mobile number?** No. It is only used to send you your own alerts.

**Is a passkey safe?** Yes. Your fingerprint or face never leaves your device, and a passkey
only works on this admin's own address, so a fake login page cannot use it.

**I changed my photo but the old one still shows on another computer.** Refresh that page. A
photo can take a few minutes to update everywhere.

**I left a store by mistake.** Ask the store's owner or an admin to invite you again from
**Settings → Staff**.
