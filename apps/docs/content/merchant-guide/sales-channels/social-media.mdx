# Social media and Google

**Sales channels → Social media** puts your products on Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and Google
Shopping, and keeps them in sync: new products, prices, stock and photos. Shoppers tap a product
and buy on your own store, so every order arrives in **Orders** as usual.

## How the channels connect

| Channel | Signs in with | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp | Facebook (your Meta business) | We make one catalog for your store in your Meta business. All three read it. WhatsApp shows it on your business profile. |
| Google | Google (Merchant Center) | Your products go to your Merchant Center account for free listings and Shopping ads. |

You need:
- **Meta:** a Meta business account (Meta Business Suite). For Instagram, an Instagram business
  account linked to it. For WhatsApp, a WhatsApp Business account.
- **Google:** a Merchant Center account (merchants.google.com) with your website verified.

## Connect a channel

1. Open **Sales channels → Social media** and pick the channel.
2. **Before you connect** lists products the platform may reject, such as no photo, no
   description or no barcode. Fixing them first means more products get listed.
3. Click **Connect**, pick **All active products**, and click **Continue with Facebook** or
   **Continue with Google**.
4. Sign in and allow access. You come back to the channel page, which says it's connected and the
   **first sync** has started.

If you already connected Facebook for one channel, connecting Instagram or WhatsApp uses the same
account straight away, without signing in again.

If you see **Request access** instead of Continue with…, that platform isn't switched on for
your store yet. Send the request and we'll email you when it's ready.

## Sync

Your catalog is sent again every 6 hours. Each time, only products that changed are sent, and
products you archive are removed from the platform. To send changes now, open the channel,
click **Manage**, then **Sync now**.

The bar at the top of the channel page shows where things stand:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **First sync** | Sending your catalog for the first time |
| **Syncing** | Sending changes now |
| **Connected** | Up to date; it shows when it last synced |
| **Error** | The platform refused something; its own words are shown. It tries again at the next sync |
| **Access expired** | The platform no longer accepts the saved sign-in, for example after you removed the app in Facebook or Google. Click **Reconnect** |
| **Paused** | You paused it; nothing new is sent |
| **Disconnected** | You disconnected it |

### Products the platform did not accept

After each sync the channel page lists how many products Google or Meta refused to show, with a
few examples and the platform's reason (a missing image, an invalid barcode, a policy problem).
Fix the product here; the next sync sends it again and the count goes down. Google and Meta take
a few hours to review, so a product can show here up to a day after you fixed it.

## Pause or disconnect

On the channel page, click **Manage**:

- **Pause listings** stops sending changes. The platform keeps what it already has. Click
  **Resume listings** to sync again.
- **Disconnect** stops the channel. Products already on the platform stay there until you remove
  them on the platform. When no channel uses Facebook or Google any more, the saved sign-in is
  deleted.

## Orders from these channels

Orders that came from Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp or Google Shopping show **via Instagram**
(and so on) on the order page, and are counted on the channel page.
