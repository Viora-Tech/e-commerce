# Agentic sales

More and more people ask an AI, such as ChatGPT, Perplexity or Copilot, what to buy.
**Sales channels → Agentic sales** gets your products into those answers, and sends shoppers to
your store to check out.

## Connect ChatGPT

ChatGPT lists stores through OpenAI's merchant programme, and OpenAI approves each store.

1. Open **Agentic sales** and click **Connect** on the ChatGPT card.
2. Choose what ChatGPT can see, then click **Request access**. The card shows **Waiting for
   approval**.
3. When OpenAI approves your store, they send you a **feed address** and a **feed key**. Open
   the request (**View request**) and click **Add OpenAI feed details**. Paste both and click
   **Save and sync**.

Your catalog then goes to ChatGPT, and again every 6 hours. Click **Manage** on the card to
**Sync now**, **Pause listings** or **Disconnect**.

Perplexity, Copilot and Google's AI mode stay as requests for now. Google's AI answers already
use your Google Merchant Center listings, so connect **Google** under
[Social media](./social-media) as well.

## Controls (what agents see)

**Agentic sales → Controls** decides what every AI agent sees:
- **Products:** all live products, or only chosen collections.
- **Hide tagged products:** products with a tag such as `no-ai` are never shown to agents.
- **Prices:** your store prices, and whether the MRP shows struck out.
- **Checkout:** shoppers check out on your store.

## Catalog readiness

**Catalog readiness** scores how well agents can read your products (0–100). It lists what to
fix: missing descriptions, barcodes (GTIN), photos, categories and policies. **Fix with AI** can
write missing descriptions for you.
